### 👤 User

How much epoxy do I need to clearcoat a 16 x 20 plaque?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

For a 16" × 20" plaque:

- Area = 320 square inches

Epoxy coverage depends on thickness, but for a typical **clear flood coat (about 1/8 inch thick)**:

- You need roughly **1 oz per 25–30 sq in**

So:
- 320 ÷ 25 ≈ 12.8 oz  
- 320 ÷ 30 ≈ 10.7 oz  

👉 That puts you at about **11–13 oz total mixed epoxy**

Since epoxy is mixed 1:1 (for most brands):
- ~5.5–6.5 oz resin  
- ~5.5–6.5 oz hardener  

**Safe recommendation:**  
Go with **14–16 oz total** to give yourself a little extra for edges and leveling.

If you’re doing a thinner seal coat instead, it’ll be less—but for a glossy top coat, that’s your range.

### 👤 User

Explain to me in detail how I use a measuring cup like this to measure at a 2 to 1 epoxy

---

### 🤖 Assistant

That’s a multi-ratio mixing cup—perfect for epoxy. Yours has columns labeled **1:1, 2:1:1, 3:1:1**, etc. For a **2:1 epoxy (resin:hardener)**, you’ll use the **“2:1:1” section**.

Here’s exactly how to use it:

### What “2:1” means
- 2 parts **resin**
- 1 part **hardener**
- The extra “:1” on the cup is for thinner (ignore it unless you’re adding solvent)

---

### Step-by-step using your cup

1. **Find the 2:1:1 column**
   - It’s labeled near the top
   - You’ll see stacked boxes with numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, etc.)

2. **Pick a number line (your total batch size)**
   - Each horizontal level is a complete mix set
   - Example: if you go to the “3” line:
     - That gives you a total batch using that row

3. **Pour Part A (resin) first**
   - Pour resin up to the **first line of that number set** (in the 2:1 section)
   - In a 2:1 system, the cup is visually split so the first larger portion is resin

4. **Add Part B (hardener)**
   - Continue pouring until you reach the **final line of that same number row**

- That completes the 2:1 ratio automatically

---

### Easy way to think about it
Each numbered row is pre-calculated so:
- Bottom of row → start
- First mark → resin amount
- Top of that row → finished mix (resin + hardener)

You’re basically “filling the row in two steps”

---

### Practical example (for your plaque)
Let’s say you want ~12 oz total:

- Pick a row close to that volume (like 3 or 4 depending on cup size)
- Fill resin to the resin line in that row
- Then add hardener until the top of that row

---

### Pro tips
- Always pour **resin first**, then hardener  
- Mix for **2–3 minutes**, scraping sides and bottom  
- Don’t switch rows mid-mix—stick to one number line  
- If your epoxy is truly 2:1 (not by weight), this cup is calibrated correctly
