## **Key Considerations:**

**SFP+ vs RJ45:**
- **SFP+ switches** (like MikroTik) are cheaper but require additional SFP+ modules (~$20-40 each)
- **RJ45 switches** work with standard Cat6A cables but cost more upfront

**Power & Noise:**
- **Fanless models** (TRENDnet TEG-S750, MikroTik) are silent but may run warm
- **Fan-cooled models** (TP-Link TL-SX1008) run cooler but can be noisy

**Best Overall Value:**
- **MikroTik CRS309** at ~$230 for 8 ports (best price per port)
- **TRENDnet TEG-S750** at ~$330 for 5 ports (best plug-and-play option)
- **TP-Link TL-SX1008** at ~$450 for 8 ports (good balance of features/price)
