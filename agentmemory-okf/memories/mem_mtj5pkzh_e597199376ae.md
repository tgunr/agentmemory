To connect AirPods to Apple TV:

1. **Automatic Connection**: If your Apple TV and AirPods are linked to the same Apple ID, you should see the AirPods in the "Control Center" of Apple TV. 
2. **Manual Connection**: Go to `Settings` > `Remotes and Devices` > `Bluetooth`, and then select your AirPods from the list of available devices.

Once connected, the audio will play through the AirPods, and the TV's internal or external speakers will be disabled.
