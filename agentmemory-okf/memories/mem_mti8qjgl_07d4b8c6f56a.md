### 👤 User

What can be done to improve this shellac finish? It’s been on here several hours and it still feels slightly tacky. It’s got two coats of 1 pound shellac.

---

### 🤖 Assistant

From the photo, the shellac looks fairly even, but I can see some gloss variation and what appears to be a relatively heavy buildup in the carved areas. A 1-pound cut shellac applied in thin coats would normally feel dry within minutes and should not remain noticeably tacky for several hours under normal conditions.

Possible causes:

- High humidity or low temperature slowing solvent evaporation.
- Shellac that is old or past its useful shelf life.
- Coats applied too heavily, especially in the carved recesses.
- Residual oil, wax, or contamination in the wood.
- Inadequate airflow around the piece.

Things you can do now:

1.

Give it more time
- Place it in a warm, dry area with good air circulation.
- A fan moving air across the piece can help.
- Wait overnight before making major changes.

2. Check for trapped solvent
- Press a fingertip on an inconspicuous area for a few seconds.
- If it feels rubbery rather than just slightly grabby, solvent may still be trapped.

3. If it's only slightly tacky tomorrow
- Lightly rub with a brown paper bag or very fine synthetic finishing pad.
- This often removes the "grabby" feel while preserving the finish.

4. If it remains tacky after 24 hours
- Wipe lightly with a cloth dampened (not wet) with denatured alcohol.
- This can reflow the shellac and help excess material level out.
- Allow it to dry again in a warm environment.

5. If the finish is still soft after a day or two
- The shellac itself may be the issue.
- Remove it

with alcohol and reapply fresh shellac mixed from fresh flakes or a newly opened can.

For a carved piece like this, I often prefer:
- One sealer coat at a 1-pound cut.
- Followed by one or two very light coats at about a 1–1.5 pound cut.
- Applied quickly and thinly to avoid pooling in the carvings.

A few questions:
- What type of shellac are you using (premixed can or mixed from flakes)?
- How old is it?
- What are the temperature and humidity conditions where you're finishing it?
- Did you sand or apply any oil finish before the shellac?
