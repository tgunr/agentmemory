### 🤖 Assistant

To remove AirWeights adhesive gasket material from your fixture plate or vacuum table, follow these instructions based on official guidelines from [AirWeights](https://airweights.com/products/on-board-gasket).

### **Removal Process**

According to AirWeights, the adhesive used on their gasket material is designed to **peel up cleanly**, allowing you to reuse your fixture plate or reset your vacuum table layout without leaving a heavy residue.

1.  **Peel Manually:** Start at one end of the gasket strip or corner of the sheet. Gently but firmly pull the material away from the surface. 
2.  **Surface Cleanup:** If any small bits of adhesive or foam remain, the surface can usually be cleaned with a standard solvent like **Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA)** or a dedicated adhesive remover (like Goo Gone) if the surface material of your fixture plate allows. 
3.  **Preparation for Re-use:** Before applying a new gasket, ensure the surface is completely dry and free of oils or dust to ensure the new adhesive bonds

correctly. 

### **Gasket Types & Usage Tips**

Depending on which adhesive gasket you are using, the removal and replacement frequency might vary:

*   **On-Board Gasket:** Designed for "easy one-time or short-term setups," this gasket is meant to be peeled up and discarded as you move between different part sizes.
*   **Adhesive Grid Gasket:** Often used for more permanent setups directly in the table grid. If you are cutting through parts, it is recommended to use a **fixture plate** (semi-sacrificial surface) to protect the table and the permanent gasket.
*   **Tile Gasket Sheets:** These are often applied to 1/4" MDF to create a "tile" that turns the entire table into a suction surface. While the gasket can be peeled from the MDF, it is often easier to replace the entire MDF/Gasket assembly for different projects.

For more detailed setup and maintenance information, you can refer to the official [AirWeights Fixture Plate Instructions](https://airweights.com/pages/airweights-fixture-plate-instructions)

and their guide on [Tile Gasket + MDF](https://airweights.com/pages/tile-gasket-mdf).
