Guacamole is a beloved avocado-based dip that originated in Mexico and has become a staple in cuisines worldwide. At its core, it is a simple yet flavorful combination of ripe avocados, lime juice, and salt, though many variations include additional fresh ingredients to enhance its texture and taste.

The key to a perfect guacamole is using avocados that are perfectly ripe—they should feel slightly soft to a gentle squeeze but not mushy. While some prefer a completely smooth consistency, many enjoy a chunkier texture where bits of whole avocado remain.

Common additions to a classic guacamole include:

*   Finely diced white or red onions for a sharp crunch.
*   Fresh cilantro, which adds a bright, herbaceous note.
*   Minced jalapeños or serrano peppers for those who enjoy a bit of heat.
*   Diced tomatoes, which provide a juicy sweetness and vibrant color.
*   A touch of minced garlic or a sprinkle of cumin for deeper savory undertones.

Beyond being the ultimate companion for tortilla chips, guacamole is

incredibly versatile. It serves as an excellent topping for tacos, burritos, and nachos, and it can even be used as a healthy spread for toast or a creamy addition to salads and sandwiches. To prevent it from browning, it is best to press plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the dip before storing it in the refrigerator, as this limits exposure to air.
