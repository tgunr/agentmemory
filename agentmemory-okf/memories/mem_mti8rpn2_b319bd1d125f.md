### 🤖 Assistant

Here are some solid iOS/iPad apps that make planning a Tesla road trip to Boston easy:

### 🔋 Tesla-specific route planning
- **Tesla App (official)**  
  - Built-in navigation plans Supercharger stops automatically  
  - Real-time battery prediction, traffic, and charger availability  
  - Best for *actually driving* the trip

- **A Better Routeplanner (ABRP)**  
  - The gold standard for EV trip planning  
  - Lets you customize: speed, weather, battery %, charging time  
  - Can sync with your Tesla for live data  
  - Great for planning ahead before you leave

### 🗺️ General road trip planners
- **Roadtrippers**  
  - Helps you find cool stops (food, scenic spots, landmarks) along the way  
  - Not Tesla-specific, but fun for building the itinerary

- **Google Maps / Apple Maps**  
  - Good for backup navigation and traffic  
  - Apple Maps now supports EV routing, but not as detailed as Tesla/ABRP

### ⚡ Charging network apps (backup)
- **PlugShare**  
  - Shows *all* chargers (not just Tesla)  
  -

Useful if you need alternatives or destination chargers in Boston

---

### 👍 Best combo
- Plan the trip: **ABRP**
- Add fun stops: **Roadtrippers**
- Drive: **Tesla built-in navigation**
- Backup chargers: **PlugShare**
