## 🧠 What is Intermittent Fasting (IF)?
Intermittent fasting is an eating pattern that cycles between periods of eating and fasting. It’s not about *what* you eat as much as *when* you eat.

**18:6** means:
- 18 hours fasting
- 6-hour eating window (e.g., eat from 12 PM to 6 PM, fast the rest)

---

## ✅ Advantages of the 18:6 Protocol

### 1. Weight Loss & Fat Loss
- Fewer eating hours → naturally reduced calorie intake  
- Extended fasting increases fat burning (your body taps into stored fat)
- Helps regulate insulin, which plays a key role in fat storage

### 2. Improved Insulin Sensitivity
- Lower insulin levels during fasting
- May reduce risk of type 2 diabetes over time

### 3. Simplicity
- No calorie counting required (though it helps)
- Fewer meals → less planning, shopping, cooking

### 4. Metabolic Health
- May improve:
  - Blood sugar levels  
  - Cholesterol  
  - Inflammation markers

### 5. Mental Clarity (for some people)
- Stable blood sugar → fewer energy crashes
- Some report better focus during fasting hours

### 6. Cellular Repair (Autophagy)
- Longer fasting periods (like 18h) may trigger autophagy  
- This is your body’s “cell cleanup” process (linked to longevity, though still being researched in humans)

---

## ⚙️ How to Do 18:6 (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Choose Your Eating Window
Common options:
- 12 PM – 6 PM  
- 1 PM – 7 PM  
Pick something sustainable with your schedule.

### Step 2: Fast for 18 Hours
During fasting:
- No calories
- Stay hydrated

### Step 3: Ease Into It
If you’re new:
- Start with 12:12 → 14:10 → 16:8 → 18:6  
Jumping straight to 18:6 can be rough.

### Step 4: Be Consistent
- Try to follow it daily or at least 5–6 days/week
- Consistency matters more than perfection

---

## 🍽️ What Can You Eat?

### During the Fasting Window (Strict)
Allowed:
- Water (still or sparkling)
- Black coffee (no sugar, no cream)
- Plain tea (no sweeteners

ideally)

Avoid:
- Anything with calories (even small snacks)
- Sugary drinks, juices, milk

---

### During the Eating Window

You *can* technically eat anything, but results depend heavily on food quality.

#### Best foods for fat loss and health:
- **Protein**: chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, Greek yogurt  
- **Healthy fats**: avocado, olive oil, nuts  
- **Complex carbs**: rice, oats, quinoa, potatoes  
- **Vegetables**: lots (fiber helps fullness)  
- **Fruits**: in moderation  

#### What to limit:
- Ultra-processed foods  
- Sugary snacks/drinks  
- Excess alcohol  

👉 Important: IF is not magic—if you overeat junk in 6 hours, weight loss won’t happen.

---

## ⏳ How Long Until You See Weight Loss?

### Short Answer:
- **1–2 weeks**: initial weight drop (often water + some fat)  
- **3–6 weeks**: noticeable fat loss if consistent  
- **8–12 weeks**: meaningful body composition changes  

---

### How Long Should You Stay on It?
- You can do 18:6 **long-term** if it feels sustainable  
- Many people cycle

it:
  - 5 days on, 2 days more relaxed  
- Others switch between 16:8 and 18:6 depending on lifestyle

---

### Realistic Fat Loss Rate
Healthy rate:
$$0.5 - 1 \text{ kg per week (1–2 lbs)}$$

Depends on:
- Calorie intake
- Activity level
- Starting weight
- Sleep & stress

---

## ⚠️ Who Should Be Careful
- People with diabetes (especially on medication)
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- History of eating disorders
- Very underweight individuals
