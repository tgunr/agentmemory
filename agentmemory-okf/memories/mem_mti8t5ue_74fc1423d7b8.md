What is corned beef

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Corned beef is a cut of beef, typically brisket, that has been salt-cured. The name "corned" comes from the large-grained rock salt, also called "corns" of salt, used in the curing process [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corned_beef), [New England](https://newengland.com/food/cooking-advice/what-is-corned-beef/). Sugar and spices are sometimes added to the curing mixture [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corned_beef).

The salt-curing process, which can be compared to pickling, makes a tough cut of meat like brisket tender [Delish](https://www.delish.com/food/a26533013/what-is-corned-beef/). Nitrates are often included in the brine, which give corned beef its characteristic pink color and help prevent the growth of bacteria like *Clostridium botulinum* [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corned_beef), [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/what-is-corned-beef-23312704). Beef cured without nitrates will have a gray color and is sometimes called "New England corned beef"

[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corned_beef), [New England](https://newengland.com/food/cooking-advice/what-is-corned-beef/).

Corned beef is a popular ingredient in many cuisines and is often associated with St. Patrick's Day in the United States, where it's commonly paired with cabbage [Delish](https://www.delish.com/food/a26533013/what-is-corned-beef/), [The Pioneer Woman](https://www.thepioneerwoman.com/food-cooking/cooking-tips-tutorials/a46506476/what-is-corned-beef/). It's also a key ingredient in a Reuben sandwich [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/what-is-corned-beef-23312704).

### Corned Beef and Cabbage (Stovetop, Slow Cooker, or Instant Pot)

#### Ingredients (serves 6)
- 3–4 lb corned beef brisket (flat cut), with spice packet
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 10–12 peppercorns (if no spice packet), plus 2 bay leaves
- 4 cups low-sodium beef broth (or water), plus more to cover
- 1 lb carrots, peeled, cut into 2–3" chunks
- 1.5 lb small red potatoes, halved
- 1 small green cabbage, cut into 6–8 wedges
- Optional: 1 tbsp mustard seeds, 6 coriander seeds, 4 whole cloves
- To serve: Dijon or whole-grain mustard, prepared horseradish, chopped parsley

#### Stovetop Method
1. Rinse the corned beef under cold water (removes excess surface salt). Place in a large pot with onion, garlic, spice packet (or whole spices), and broth; add water to just cover.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook until fork-tender, about 2.5–3.5 hours (check after 2 hours). Skim foam as needed. Target internal temp 195–205°F for tenderness.
3. Add carrots and potatoes; simmer 20–

25 minutes.
4. Add cabbage wedges on top; simmer 10–15 minutes until tender.
5. Rest beef 10–15 minutes. Slice against the grain. Serve with vegetables and broth.

#### Slow Cooker Method
- Add beef (fat-side up), onion, garlic, spices; pour in broth to come ~¾ up the meat.
- Cook 8–10 hours on LOW (or 5–6 hours on HIGH) until fork-tender.
- Add carrots, potatoes for last 2–3 hours; add cabbage for last 45–60 minutes.

#### Instant Pot Method (6–8 qt)
1. Beef, onion, garlic, spices, and 4 cups liquid. Pressure cook 85–90 minutes (high); natural release 15 minutes, then quick release.
2. Remove beef to rest. Add carrots, potatoes, cabbage; pressure cook 3–4 minutes (high), quick release.

#### Tips
- If broth tastes too salty, dilute with water or swap to low-sodium broth.
- Trim excess surface fat after cooking; slice thinly across the grain for tenderness.
- “Red” corned beef uses curing salt (pink color); “gray” is nitrate-free—cook the same way.

#### Serving Ideas
- Spoon hot cooking broth over servings.
