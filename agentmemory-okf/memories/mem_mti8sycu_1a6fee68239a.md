### 🤖 Assistant

Here are some tasty poke bowl recipes you can mix and match depending on what you like:

### 1. Classic Ahi Tuna Poke
**Ingredients:**
- Sushi-grade ahi tuna (cubed)
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Green onions
- Sweet onion (thinly sliced)
- Sesame seeds
- Optional: chili flakes or sriracha

**Directions:**
Mix everything gently and let it marinate for 10–15 minutes. Serve over white rice with seaweed and avocado.

---

### 2. Spicy Salmon Poke
**Ingredients:**
- Sushi-grade salmon (cubed)
- Mayo
- Sriracha
- Soy sauce
- Cucumber (diced)
- Green onions

**Directions:**
Mix mayo + sriracha first, then fold in salmon and other ingredients. Serve over rice or greens.

---

### 3. Hawaiian-Style Shoyu Poke
**Ingredients:**
- Ahi tuna
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Hawaiian sea salt
- Maui onion (or sweet onion)
- Limu (seaweed) or substitute with wakame

**Directions:**
Keep it simple—mix and let the fish shine. Serve with rice.

---

### 4. Shrimp Poke (Cooked option)
**Ingredients:**
- Cooked shrimp
- Soy sauce
- Lime

juice
- Garlic
- Chili flakes
- Avocado

**Directions:**
Toss everything together and chill slightly before serving.

---

### 5. Vegan Tofu Poke
**Ingredients:**
- Firm tofu (pressed, cubed)
- Soy sauce or tamari
- Sesame oil
- Rice vinegar
- Maple syrup
- Cucumber, carrots, avocado

**Directions:**
Marinate tofu for at least 20 minutes. Serve over rice or quinoa.

---

### Base + Toppings Ideas
- **Base:** sushi rice, brown rice, greens, quinoa  
- **Toppings:** avocado, edamame, mango, cucumber, pickled ginger  
- **Crunch:** wonton strips, crispy onions  
- **Sauces:** spicy mayo, ponzu, eel sauce
