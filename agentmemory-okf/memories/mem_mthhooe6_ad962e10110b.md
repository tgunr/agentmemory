### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-08-24T11:50:06Z

**Simparica Trio** (often misspelled as “Siparica Trio”) is a monthly chewable tablet for dogs made by Zoetis. It provides broad-spectrum protection against external and internal parasites.<grok:render card_id="a0d696" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>

### What it protects against
- **Fleas**: Kills adult fleas (starts within ~4 hours, reaches high efficacy by 8 hours) and helps prevent infestations and flea allergy dermatitis.  
- **Ticks**: Treats and controls multiple species (including *Ixodes scapularis*/black-legged/deer tick, *Amblyomma americanum*/Lone Star, *Dermacentor variabilis*/American dog tick, *Rhipicephalus sanguineus*/brown dog tick, and others) for about 1 month (some species up to 5 weeks).  
- **Heartworm**: Prevents heartworm disease caused by *Dirofilaria immitis*.  
- **Intestinal worms**: Treats and controls roundworms (*Toxocara canis*, *Toxascaris leonina*) and hookworms (*Ancylostoma caninum*, *Uncinaria stenocephala*). It can also help with certain other nematodes depending on the region.<grok:render card_id="c21728" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">17</argument></grok:render>

### Active ingredients
- **Sarolaner** – kills fleas and ticks.  
- **Moxidectin** – prevents heartworm and helps against certain internal parasites.  
- **Pyrantel** (as pamoate/embonate) – targets intestinal roundworms and hookworms.<grok:render card_id="36e595" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>

### Key details
- **Form**: Liver-flavored chewable tablet that most dogs accept readily. Can be given with or without food.  
- **Age/weight**: Approved for puppies and dogs **8 weeks of age and older**, weighing at least about 1.3 kg / 2.8 lbs.  
- **Dosing**: One tablet once a month, dosed by the dog’s weight (different tablet strengths/colors correspond to weight ranges, e.g., smaller dogs get lower-dose tablets containing 3 mg sarolaner / 0.06 mg moxidectin / 12.5 mg pyrantel, scaling up for larger dogs). Do not split tablets. For continuous protection, give year-round.  
- **Duration**: Protection generally lasts about 1 month (28–35 days depending on the parasite).<grok:render card_id="2f029e" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">16</argument></grok:render>

It is prescription-only in many countries and is one of the most commonly recommended combination preventives by veterinarians.

**Important**: Always consult a veterinarian before starting any parasite preventive. They will confirm the correct dose based on your dog’s exact weight, check for existing heartworm infection if needed, and advise on local parasite risks or any contraindications. Do not use in cats or other animals unless specifically directed.
