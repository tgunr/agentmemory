**Vast.ai** is a marketplace for affordable GPU cloud computing, primarily aimed at AI/ML workloads such as training, inference, fine-tuning, and related tasks.<grok:render card_id="dc11eb" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

It connects **hosts** (individuals with gaming PCs, mining setups, research labs, or professional data centers that list underutilized GPUs) with **renters** who need compute. Hosts set their own prices and terms; renters search and filter offers by GPU type/model, RAM, CPU, bandwidth, location, reliability, price, and other factors, then rent instances (typically Linux Docker containers) on demand.<grok:render card_id="6a5cc3" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">3</argument></grok:render>

### Key Features
- **GPU Cloud** — On-demand instances across many data centers and a large pool of GPUs (reports cite 17,000–20,000+ GPUs from hundreds of hosts). Deploy via web interface, CLI, Python SDK, or REST API.
- **Serverless** — Model endpoints with autoscaling (including to zero) and pay-per-use compute.
- **Clusters** — Multi-node setups (including InfiniBand) for larger-scale training.
- Prebuilt templates for popular open-source models and frameworks.
- Access methods include SSH, Jupyter notebooks, and programmatic control.
- Pricing is market-driven (often significantly lower than major hyperscalers like AWS, GCP, or Azure for comparable hardware). Options include on-demand, interruptible/spot-style, and reserved.<grok:render card_id="63f395" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">2</argument></grok:render>

### Background
Founded in 2016 (incorporated June 28) by Jake Cannell (CEO) and others, with the goal of democratizing AI compute by tapping into underutilized hardware rather than concentrating it with a few large providers. Headquarters are in Los Angeles (with a San Francisco office). The platform emphasizes transparent, programmatically accessible infrastructure.<grok:render card_id="865659" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>

**Official site**: [vast.ai](https://vast.ai/)  
**Documentation**: [docs.vast.ai](https://docs.vast.ai/)

It is especially popular among researchers, startups, and independent developers seeking cost-effective GPU access, though quality and reliability can vary by host (filters and scores help mitigate this). Note that it currently focuses on Linux Docker environments and does not support Windows.
