You’re referring to the **Battle of Rorke’s Drift** (1879, Anglo‑Zulu War).

### Casualties

According to historical records:

- **British side**
  - 17 killed  
  - About 10–15 wounded  
  - Force size: ~139–150 men  

- **Zulu side**
  - Around 350–600 killed  
  - Several hundred wounded  
  - Force size: ~3,000–4,000 warriors
