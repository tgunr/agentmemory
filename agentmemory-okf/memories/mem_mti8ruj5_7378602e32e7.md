### 🤖 Assistant

If you mean the **Air Force airman on a C-47–type aircraft in Vietnam who received the Medal of Honor**, that was **John Lee Levitow**.

### Who he was
- **Rank at the time:** Airman First Class
- **Aircraft:** **AC-47** gunship, which was the armed combat version of the **C-47**
- **Conflict:** Vietnam War
- **Action date:** February 24, 1969, over Long Binh, South Vietnam

### Why he received the Medal of Honor
His AC-47 was hit by enemy fire during a night mission. A live flare was loose inside the damaged aircraft, and it could have detonated and destroyed the plane. Although badly wounded, Levitow crawled to the flare, smothered it with his body, and threw it out the cargo door, saving the aircraft and crew.
