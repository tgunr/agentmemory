### 🤖 Assistant — 2025-09-10T17:50:31Z

Gmail has several attachment limits:

**File size limits:**
- **25 MB per email** - This is the total limit for all attachments combined in a single email
- If you try to attach files larger than 25 MB, Gmail will automatically upload them to Google Drive and share a link instead

**Workarounds for larger files:**
- **Google Drive integration** - Gmail automatically uses Drive for files over 25 MB, allowing you to share files up to 15 GB (or whatever your Drive storage limit allows)
- **Multiple emails** - You can split large attachments across multiple emails
- **Cloud storage links** - Share links to files stored in Google Drive, Dropbox, or other cloud services

**Other considerations:**
- The recipient's email provider may have different limits
- Some file types may be blocked for security reasons (like .exe files)
- The 25 MB limit includes the email content itself, though this is usually negligible

If you're regularly sharing large files, using Google Drive sharing or other cloud storage services is generally more efficient than email attachments.
