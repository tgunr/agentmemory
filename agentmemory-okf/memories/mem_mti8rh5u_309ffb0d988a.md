### Classic Bread Stuffing Recipe

Serves 8–10

#### Ingredients
- 1 loaf (about 1 pound / 450 g) day-old bread, cut into 1-inch cubes (about 10 cups)
- 1/2 cup (115 g) butter
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried sage
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried rosemary (optional)
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2–3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

#### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).

2. If the bread isn't already dry, spread the cubes on baking sheets and bake for 10–15 minutes until lightly crisp.

3. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and celery and cook for 8–10 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.

4. Stir in the sage, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper.

5. Place the bread cubes in a large bowl. Add the vegetable mixture and parsley. Toss well.

6. Mix the eggs with 2 cups of broth. Pour over the bread mixture and gently

toss. Add more broth as needed until the bread is moist but not soggy.

7. Transfer to a greased 9×13-inch baking dish. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes.

8. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until the top is golden brown.

#### Optional Add-Ins
- 1 cup cooked sausage
- 1/2 cup dried cranberries
- 1 chopped apple
- 1/2 cup toasted pecans or walnuts
- Fresh herbs instead of dried herbs

Serve warm alongside roast turkey, chicken, pork, or holiday meals.
