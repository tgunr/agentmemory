### 🤖 Assistant

Certainly! Here's a basic recipe for shrimp curry:

Ingredients:

- 1 pound (450g) of shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tablespoons curry powder (adjust to taste for desired spice level)
- 1 can (14 ounces) of diced tomatoes
- 1 can (14 ounces) of coconut milk
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro leaves for garnish (optional)
- Cooked rice or naan bread for serving

Instructions:

1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet or pan over medium heat.

2. Add the chopped onion, minced garlic, and grated ginger. Sauté for a few minutes until the onion becomes translucent and fragrant.

3. Stir in the curry powder and cook for another minute or until it becomes aromatic.

4. Add the diced tomatoes (with their juice) to the pan and stir to combine with the onion mixture. Allow it to simmer for about 5 minutes, or until the tomatoes start to break down.

5. Pour in the coconut milk and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.

6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

7. Add the peeled and deveined shrimp to the pan. Cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the shrimp turn pink and opaque. Be careful not to overcook them as they can become tough.

8. Once the shrimp are cooked, remove the pan from heat.

9. Serve the shrimp curry hot over cooked rice or with naan bread.

10. Garnish with fresh cilantro leaves if desired.

Enjoy your homemade shrimp curry! You can adjust the spice level by adding more or less curry powder or adding chili powder if you like it spicier.
