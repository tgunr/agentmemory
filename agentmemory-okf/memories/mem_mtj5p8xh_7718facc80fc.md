### 🤖 Assistant

The "Missing Metadata" status in App Store Connect usually indicates that required information for the subscription products is incomplete or missing. To resolve this, you will need to provide the necessary metadata for each subscription product. Here are the steps to fix the "Missing Metadata" issue:

1. **Navigate to the Subscription Details**:
   - In App Store Connect, go to your app and then to the "Subscriptions" section.
   - Click on the subscription that shows the "Missing Metadata" status.

2. **Complete Required Fields**:
   - You will be prompted to fill in various details about your subscription. Ensure that all required fields are completed. Common required fields include:
     - Subscription Name
     - Subscription Description
     - Pricing
     - Availability
     - Subscription Group Display Name
     - Localizations
     - Review Information
     - Offer Codes (if applicable)

3. **Provide Subscription Group Information**:
   - If you haven’t already, make sure to set up a subscription group. A subscription group allows users to subscribe to one of multiple subscriptions at different service levels. Provide a display name for the subscription group and ensure that all subscriptions within the group are correctly configured.

4. **Check Localizations**:
   - Make sure that you have provided all necessary localization information. This includes localized names, descriptions, and other relevant metadata for all languages that your app supports.

5. **Upload Required Screenshots**:
   - For each localization, you may need to upload promotional images or screenshots showcasing the subscription.

6. **Review Pricing and Availability**:
   - Ensure that the pricing and availability of the subscriptions are correctly set. This includes the price for each region where your app will be available.

7. **Submit for Review**:
   - Once all the required metadata is completed, save the changes and submit the subscription for review.

Here's a step-by-step example of how you might complete the metadata for a subscription:

1. **Go to Subscriptions** in App Store Connect.
2. **Select the Subscription** (e.g., `GCodeGen-Yearly`).
3. **Fill in the Required Fields**:
   - **Subscription Name**: GCodeGen Yearly
   - **Description**: Access premium features with a yearly subscription.
   - **Pricing**: Set the price for different regions.
   - **Availability**: Ensure the subscription is available in the desired regions.
   - **Localizations**: Add descriptions and other metadata in different languages.
   - **Screenshots**: Upload required promotional screenshots.

4. **Save and Submit**:
   - After completing all fields, save your changes.
   - Submit the subscription for review.

If you have followed these steps and still encounter issues, it might be helpful to consult the App Store Connect documentation or reach out to Apple Support for further assistance.
