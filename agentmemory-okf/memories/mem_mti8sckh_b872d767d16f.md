### If you want a **powered “small machine” cultivator** (most popular for raised beds)

These are the best fit when:
- Beds are 4' wide or less  
- Soil is already established (you’re not busting sod)  
- You want to mix compost and weed quickly

#### 1. **Honda FG110 Mini Tiller/Cultivator** – premium small gas choice
- 25cc 4‑stroke Honda engine (no gas/oil mixing)  
- About 9" wide, up to ~8" deep – ideal to drop into a raised bed  
- Strong enough for compacted beds, still nimble and light (~27 lb)

- Rated a top “best cultivator” in independent testing of rototillers and cultivators for garden work, especially for small areas and raised beds [source: Bob Vila rototiller tests](https://www.bobvila.com/articles/best-rototiller/).

**Best for you if:** you want something durable that can handle slightly tougher soil and you’re OK with gas and seasonal maintenance.

---

#### 2. **Greenworks 40V Cordless Cultivator (10")** – quiet, cordless, raised‑bed friendly
- Battery powered; no cord, no fumes  
- 8.25–10" width, about 5" depth – very controllable in a 4' bed  
- About 21 lb; easy to lift in/out of beds  
- Evaluated as one of the best battery tillers for small spaces and raised‑bed‑type work [source: Bob Vila rototiller tests](https://www.bobvila.com/articles/best-rototiller/).

**Best for you if:**  
You want:
- Lightweight  
- Easy start (push button)  
- Enough power for annual bed prep and in‑season cultivation, but not to break brand‑new sod.

---

#### 3. **Earthquake Mini Cultivator / MC43 /

MC440 series** – strong mini gas option
- 6–10" adjustable width, good depth for mixing in compost  
- Specifically recommended for raised beds and tight spaces in multiple reviews [source: Farmstand guide to garden tillers](https://www.farmstandapp.com/30919/7-best-garden-tillers-for-preparing-vegetable-beds/).  
- More aggressive than many cordless electrics.

**Best for you if:** you want a “muscular” but still small gas cultivator for existing beds and tougher soil.

---

### If you want **corded electric** (cheapest power, but with a cord)

These are great if your raised beds are near an outlet and you’re OK managing an extension cord.

#### 4. **Sun Joe TJ604E (13.5‑amp corded tiller/cultivator)**
- 16" wide, up to 8" deep, 6 steel tines  
- Powerful for the size; highly rated in small‑garden tiller roundups [source: Sow Small Garden’s electric mini tiller picks](https://sowsmallgarden.com/best-small-garden-tiller-reviews/).  
- Slightly wide for narrow beds, but you can make overlapping shallow passes

and just use part of the width in the bed.

**Best for you if:**  
You have several beds in one area and want maximum power per dollar, and don’t mind a cord.
