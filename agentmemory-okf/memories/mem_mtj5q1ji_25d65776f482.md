Sous vide beef shank steak

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Sous vide is an excellent method to prepare beef shank steak because it helps break down the tough connective tissues, resulting in tender and flavorful meat. Here's a guide to sous vide beef shank steak:

### **Ingredients**
- Beef shank steak (1-2 pieces)
- Salt and pepper (to taste)
- Fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, or bay leaf, optional)
- Garlic cloves (optional)
- Olive oil or butter (for searing)

---

### **Instructions**

#### 1. **Prepare the Beef Shank**
   - Season the steak generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
   - Optionally, add fresh herbs and a crushed garlic clove for extra flavor.

#### 2. **Vacuum Seal**
   - Place the seasoned steak into a vacuum-seal bag or a resealable freezer bag.
   - If using a resealable bag, use the water displacement method to remove air.

#### 3. **Set the Sous Vide**
   - **Temperature**: 
     - 131°F (55°C) for medium-rare
     - 140°F (60°C) for medium
     - 185°F (85°C) for fall-apart tender
   - **Time**: Cook for **24-36 hours**. The long cooking time breaks down the collagen, making the meat tender.

#### 4. **Cook the Beef Shank**
   - Submerge the bag in the water bath and let it cook for the set time.
   - Ensure the bag stays fully submerged, adding weights if necessary.

#### 5. **Sear for Flavor**
   - After cooking, remove the steak from the bag and pat it dry with paper towels (important for a good sear).
   - Heat a skillet over high heat and add a small amount of oil or butter.
   - Sear the steak for 1-2 minutes per side until a golden-brown crust forms.

#### 6. **Rest and Serve**
   - Let the steak rest for a couple of minutes before slicing.
   - Serve with your favorite sides, such as mashed potatoes, roasted vegetables, or a hearty gravy.

---

### **Tips**
- Save the juices from the bag to make a sauce or gravy.
- Consider marinating the beef before vacuum-sealing for added flavor.
- Use a bone-in shank for richer flavor, as the marrow will add depth during cooking.

Enjoy your tender, flavorful sous vide beef shank steak!
