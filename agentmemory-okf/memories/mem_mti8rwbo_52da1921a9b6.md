### US airbases in Libya (name)

The main (and essentially only major, long-term) U.S. air base in Libya was:

- **Wheelus Air Base** (near **Tripoli**) — also commonly referred to as **“Tripoli Air Base”** and associated with the **Mellaha** area/airfield. It operated as a U.S. base from WWII-era use through the Cold War, until Libya took it over in **1970**.

### 🤖 Assistant

The United States currently operates two major military bases in Spain, both located in the southern region of Andalusia. While these facilities host thousands of American personnel and strategic assets, they are technically owned by the Spanish Ministry of Defense and operate under a Spanish commander.

### Active US Bases

#### 1. Naval Station Rota (NAVSTA Rota)
Located in the Province of Cádiz, this is the largest American military community in Spain. It is often referred to as the "Gateway to the Mediterranean" due to its strategic position near the Strait of Gibraltar.
*   **Role:** It serves as a major port and airbase, supporting the US Sixth Fleet and Air Mobility Command.
*   **Key Assets:** The base is a primary hub for the Aegis Ballistic Missile Defense System, hosting forward-deployed destroyers such as the `USS Arleigh Burke` and `USS Roosevelt`.
*   **Joint Operations:** It is a shared facility with the Spanish Navy, which maintains the headquarters of its fleet there.

#### 2. Morón Air Base

(Base Aérea de Morón)
Located about 35 miles southeast of Seville, this base is a vital link for US military operations moving toward the Middle East and Africa.
*   **Role:** It provides expeditionary combat support and acts as a staging area for aircraft and personnel. 
*   **Permanent Presence:** Under a 2015 agreement, the US was granted a permanent presence for up to 3,000 troops and civilians, primarily from the Special Purpose Marine Air-Ground Task Force - Crisis Response - Africa.
*   **Strategic Use:** Its massive flight line and refueling capabilities make it essential for transiting aircraft.

### Status and Sovereignty
Under the **Agreement on Defense Cooperation (ADC)**, the relationship between the two nations is a partnership:
*   **Command:** The bases remain under the Spanish flag and are commanded by Spanish officers (a Vice Admiral at Rota).
*   **Customs:** US military customs, such as flying the American flag, are generally not observed except on special occasions like the Fourth of

July.
*   **Control:** Spain retains sovereignty over its airspace and territory. In recent developments, Spain has at times restricted its airspace to US military missions that conflict with Spanish foreign policy.

### Former Bases
The US previously operated two other major bases that were turned back over to Spanish custody in 1992:
*   **Torrejón Air Base:** Located near Madrid, it was a major hub for the Sixteenth Air Force for nearly 40 years.
*   **Zaragoza Air Base:** Located in northeastern Spain, it served as a tactical fighter base and a NASA Space Shuttle emergency landing site. While no longer operated by the US, it is occasionally used for NATO exercises or specific missions.
