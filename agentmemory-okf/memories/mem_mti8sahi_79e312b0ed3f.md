### 👤 User

What is the stipend for Medal of Honor

---

### 🤖 Assistant

In the United States, living Medal of Honor recipients receive a **monthly tax‑free stipend from the Department of Veterans Affairs**.  

As of the most recent publicly available information, that **special Medal of Honor pension is $1,000 per month**, adjusted periodically by Congress. An example discussion of this amount appears in federal benefits coverage noting the “monthly stipend that is currently paid is $1,000 per month” to Medal of Honor recipients, with past proposals to increase it but not necessarily enacted as law yet \([FedSmith](https://www.fedsmith.com/2015/09/22/legislation-seeks-to-increase-monthly-stipend-for-medal-of-honor-recipients/)\).

A few important clarifications:

- This is **in addition to** any other military retired pay, VA disability compensation, or other benefits the person may be entitled to.
- The amount can change if Congress passes new legislation, so for the **current official rate** you’d want to check:
  - The latest VA benefits tables on the VA website, or  
  - The

current text of **38 U.S.C. § 1562** (the law governing the Medal of Honor pension).

If you’d like, I can walk you through how to look up the exact current figure in the U.S. Code or VA references.
