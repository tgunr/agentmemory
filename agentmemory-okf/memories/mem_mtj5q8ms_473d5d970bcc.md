### Recipe 1: Spicy Lemon-Dill Salmon Patties with Yogurt Sauce

#### Ingredients:
- 1 lb fresh salmon fillets, skinned and deboned
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tablespoon capers, drained and chopped
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil for frying

#### Yogurt Sauce:
- 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and pepper to taste

#### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the Salmon Mixture:**
   - Finely chop the salmon fillets or pulse in a food processor until coarsely ground.
   - In a large bowl, combine the salmon, breadcrumbs, egg, dill, capers, red onion, jalapeño, lemon zest, lemon juice, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine.

2. **Form Patties:**
   - Shape the salmon mixture into 6-8 patties, about ½-inch thick. Place them on a plate and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up.

3. **Cook the Patties:**
   - Heat a large skillet over medium heat and add enough olive oil to coat the bottom. Cook the salmon patties for about 3-4 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and cooked through.

4. **Make the Yogurt Sauce:**
   - In a small bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, dill, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. Stir until smooth.

5. **Serve:**
   - Serve the salmon patties hot, topped with a dollop of yogurt sauce. Garnish with additional fresh dill and lemon wedges.

### Recipe 2: Asian-Inspired Salmon Patties with Ginger-Soy Glaze

#### Ingredients:
- 1 lb fresh salmon fillets, skinned and deboned
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 2 green onions, finely sliced
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Vegetable oil for frying

#### Ginger-Soy Glaze:
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon water

#### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the Salmon Mixture:**
   - Finely chop the salmon fillets or pulse in a food processor until coarsely ground.
   - In a large bowl, combine the salmon, panko breadcrumbs, egg, green onions, cilantro, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, lime juice, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine.

2. **Form Patties:**
   - Shape the salmon mixture into 6-8 patties, about ½-inch thick. Place them on a plate and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to firm up.

3. **Cook the Patties:**
   - Heat a large skillet over medium heat and add enough vegetable oil to coat the bottom. Cook the salmon patties for about 3-4 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and cooked through.

4. **Make the Ginger-Soy Glaze:**
   - In a small saucepan, combine the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and ginger. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.
   - Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook until the sauce thickens, about 1-2 minutes.

5. **Serve:**
   - Serve the salmon patties drizzled with the ginger-soy glaze. Garnish with additional green onions and sesame seeds.
