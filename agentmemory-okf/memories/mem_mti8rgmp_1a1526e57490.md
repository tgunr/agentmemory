### 🤖 Assistant

Based on the image, your Frigidaire stove is displaying a **Loc** code, which indicates the control lock is active.

### How to Unlock the Controls
To disable the lock, follow these steps:
*   Locate the **Clock icon with a Padlock** symbol on the digital display (it is situated to the right of the numbers 4, 5, and 0).
*   **Press and hold the Clock/Padlock button** for at least 3 seconds.
*   You should hear a beep, and the **Loc** text and lock indicator light should disappear.

### If that doesn't work:
*   **Try the Cancel/OFF button:** On many Frigidaire Professional models, pressing and holding the **OFF or CANCEL** button for 6 seconds acts as a secondary way to toggle the control lock [Frigidaire](https://owner.frigidaire.com/support-articles/article/1847356-ranges-what-does-the-lock-feature-do-and-how-can-i-activate-it-).
*   **Hard Reset:** If the touch panel is unresponsive, turn off the power at the circuit breaker for 60 seconds, then turn it back on to reset the control boar
