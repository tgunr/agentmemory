## The core limitation: bonding to cured epoxy

A flood coat is almost always a **2-part epoxy** that's already fully cured. UV epoxy cures by a completely different mechanism (photoinitiators + UV light) and **won't chemically bond** to the existing cured 2-part epoxy. You're relying on mechanical adhesion only — meaning the touch-up sits on top rather than fusing with the original. That opens the door to:

- Visible seams or lines
- Delamination / peeling over time
- Slight height differences that catch light
