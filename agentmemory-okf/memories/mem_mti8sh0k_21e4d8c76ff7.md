### 👤 User

In Affinity, how do I make text follow a curve?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To make text follow a curve in Affinity Pro (which integrates the tools from the classic Affinity suite), you use the **Path Text** feature. This allows you to treat any vector line or shape as a baseline for your typography.

#### Creating Path Text on a Custom Curve
If you want to draw a specific curve for your text, start by selecting the **Pen Tool** or **Node Tool** from the toolbar. Draw the curve or line exactly how you want it to appear. Once your curve is drawn, select the **Artistic Text Tool** (the "A" icon) and hover your cursor over the curve. You will notice the cursor change to a "T" with a small wavy line beneath it. Click anywhere on the curve, and you can begin typing; the text will automatically flow along the path you created.

#### Creating Path Text on a Shape
You can also wrap text around geometric shapes like circles or polygons. Select the shape you've drawn, then go to the **Layer** menu, select **Convert to Text Path**, and start typing. Alternatively, just like with a custom

curve, you can select the **Artistic Text Tool** and click directly on the stroke of the shape to begin typing.

#### Adjusting the Text Position
Once your text is on the path, you will see green and red handles (triangles) on the line. The **green handle** represents the start point of the text, and the **red handle** represents the end point. You can click and drag these handles with the **Move Tool** or **Node Tool** to slide the text along the curve. If you want the text to sit on the inside of a shape or the opposite side of a line, simply drag the handles across the path to flip the text orientation.

#### Fine-Tuning the Look
You can further refine the appearance using the **Character Panel**. Adjusting the **Baseline Offset** will move the text further away from or closer to the curve, while adjusting **Kerning** or **Tracking** helps manage how the letters space out as the curve bends. If the curve is very sharp, increasing the tracking slightly often makes the text more readable.
