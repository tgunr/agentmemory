### Why the eufyMake / “eufy UV” laminator may not “auto‑feed”
From what eufyMake and users describe, the laminator **doesn’t continuously “auto-feed” on its own** like a printer. It generally **feeds only when you’ve (a) threaded the roll correctly and (b) insert something into the rollers / start the lamination step**.

The two most common reasons people think “auto feed is broken” are:

1. **First-time setup: the B-film roll is installed but not threaded yet**  
   Angie Holden notes the roll “is already installed, however it does need to be fed through the machine,” and she uses the included white board to get it to grab and feed through. [Country Chic Cottage tutorial](https://www.thecountrychiccottage.net/eufymake-laminating-module/)

2. **Auto-feed is triggered during lamination by the “Heating” step (and/or once ready)**  
   eufyMake’s UV DTF guide says: “Hit the **heating** button, and your design should **automatically begin to feed through the machine**.” [eufyMake UV DTF

guide](https://www.eufymake.com/blogs/printing-guides/how-to-make-uv-dtf-transfers)

### Quick checklist to get it feeding
#### A) Thread/prime the B-film roll (common on a brand-new unit)
1. Power on.
2. Open lid, **find the end of the film**, pull out some length.
3. **Close the lid** and **insert the included white board** into the front so the rollers can grab and pull it through. [Country Chic Cottage tutorial](https://www.thecountrychiccottage.net/eufymake-laminating-module/)
4. Once it comes out the back, trim with the cutter and separate layers as needed.

#### B) During actual lamination
1. Put your printed sheet into the laminator **as described for UV DTF**.
2. Press **Heating** and wait until it’s ready (Angie mentions indicator lights turning green before laminating). [Country Chic Cottage tutorial](https://www.thecountrychiccottage.net/eufymake-laminating-module/)
3. At that point, it should start pulling the sheet through automatically per eufyMake. [eufyMake UV DTF

guide](https://www.eufymake.com/blogs/printing-guides/how-to-make-uv-dtf-transfers)

### If it’s beeping/blinking and never grabs anything
Some people report an “out of the box” condition where it **won’t take the feed** and **beeps/blinks**. (Example report here: [Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/eufyMakeOfficial/comments/1rpftpb/laminator_wont_work_out_of_the_box/).) In that case it may be an error state or hardware issue rather than “normal not-auto-feeding,” and it’s worth going straight to official support/warranty.

### One question so I can pinpoint it
When you say “does not auto feed,” do you mean:
- **the B-roll film won’t thread through at all** (empty machine, trying to load the roll), **or**
- **a printed sheet won’t pull through after you press Heating**?
