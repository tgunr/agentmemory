This menu features a variety of classic Mexican and Central American dishes typically found at a food truck or taqueria. Here is a breakdown of what the items are:

### Main Food Items

#### Popular Dishes
*   **Quesabirria:** These are a cross between a taco and a quesadilla. They feature beef birria (slow-cooked, shredded meat) and melted cheese inside a tortilla that has been dipped in the stew's fat and grilled. They are usually served with a side of consome (broth) for dipping.
*   **Pizza Birria:** A large-scale version of quesabirria where tortillas are layered with birria meat and cheese to resemble a pizza, then sliced into wedges.
*   **Burrito California:** Typically a large burrito filled with meat (often steak), french fries, cheese, salsa, and guacamole or sour cream.
*   **Torta / Torta Cubana / Torta Chilanga:** Mexican sandwiches on crusty rolls. A "Cubana" is usually loaded with multiple meats like ham, breaded steak, and hot dogs, while a "Chilanga" often features breaded steak (milaneza)

and is a specialty from Mexico City.
*   **Pambazo:** A specific type of sandwich where the bread is dipped in a red guajillo chili sauce and then lightly fried. They are often filled with potatoes and chorizo.

#### Traditional Staples
*   **Baleadas:** A traditional Honduran dish consisting of a thick flour tortilla folded in half and filled with mashed fried beans, cream, and crumbled cheese.
*   **Pupusas (implied by the Gorditas/Sopes section):** While not explicitly listed as "Pupusas," the menu includes **Gorditas** and **Sopes**, which are thick corn masa cakes topped or stuffed with beans, meat, cheese, and salsa.
*   **Sincronizada:** Two flour tortillas with ham and cheese in the middle, grilled together like a sandwich.
*   **Tostadas:** Toasted or deep-fried corn tortillas topped with beans, meat, lettuce, and cheese.

### Beverages (Bebidas)

*   **Aguas Frescas:** Light, refreshing drinks made by blending fruits, grains, or seeds with sugar and water. Common flavors seen in the colorful

barrels at the bottom left usually include Horchata (rice/cinnamon), Jamaica (hibiscus), and Tamarindo.
*   **Jarritos:** A popular Mexican brand of fruit-flavored sodas known for being made with natural sugar and coming in many colors (tamarind, mandarin, lime, etc.).
*   **Sidral:** An apple-flavored soda; the menu notes this is available on Mondays.
*   **Coca Mexicana:** Often called "Mexican Coke," this is highly sought after because it is famously made with cane sugar instead of high fructose corn syrup and comes in glass bottles.

If you are looking for a recommendation, the **Quesabirria** or the **Torta Cubana** are usually the most filling and flavorful choices at places with this type of menu.
