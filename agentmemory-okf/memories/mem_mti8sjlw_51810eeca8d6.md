### 🥘 Rustic Seafood & Roasted Root Veg Bowl (best option IMO)
This is super flavorful and pretty easy.

**Use:** potatoes, carrots, maybe parsnips, onions

**Steps:**
- Chop root veggies into bite-sized pieces  
- Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, garlic  
- Roast at ~425°F (220°C) for ~25–30 min until crispy  
- While that’s going, sauté your seafood mix separately with garlic + butter + a squeeze of lemon  
- Combine at the end and finish with herbs (parsley if you’ve got it)

👉 Key: cook seafood separately so it stays tender

---

### 🍲 Hearty Seafood & Root Vegetable Stew
Great if you want something cozy.

**Steps:**
- Sauté onion + garlic  
- Add chopped root veggies + cook a few minutes  
- Add broth (chicken or veggie) + herbs (thyme, bay leaf)  
- Simmer until veggies are tender  
- Add seafood mix in the last

5–7 minutes (important!)  
- Finish with lemon juice + olive oil

👉 This turns into a really nice rustic soup

---

### 🍳 Seafood Hash (surprisingly good)
Think breakfast/dinner hybrid.

**Steps:**
- Dice potatoes small and pan-fry until crispy  
- Add onions + any other root veg (carrots/parsnips grated or small dice)  
- Once everything is golden, add seafood mix  
- Season with paprika, garlic, maybe a little chili  
- Optional: top with a fried egg

👉 Crunchy + savory + quick

---

### 🌮 Root Veg + Seafood Tacos
- Roast root veggies with cumin + chili powder  
- Cook seafood with lime + garlic  
- Combine in tortillas with something creamy (sour cream/yogurt)
