### 🤖 Assistant — 2025-07-06T12:50:14Z

Here are some excellent resources to help your granddaughter learn 3D modeling and printing, progressing from beginner to more advanced skills:

## Getting Started with 3D Modeling

**Tinkercad** is perfect for beginners - it's free, runs in a web browser, and designed for kids. It uses simple drag-and-drop shapes that can be combined to create more complex objects. The interface is intuitive and there are built-in tutorials.

**Fusion 360 for Personal Use** offers a free license for students and hobbyists. While more advanced, it has excellent educational resources and grows with her skills.

## Learning Resources

**YouTube Channels:**
- "Lars Christensen" has beginner-friendly Fusion 360 tutorials
- "Tinkercad" official channel for basic modeling concepts
- "Teaching Tech" covers 3D printing fundamentals

**Online Courses:**
- Tinkercad's built-in lesson plans and tutorials
- Instructables has many kid-friendly 3D modeling projects
- MyMiniFactory has educational content specifically for young makers

## Snapmaker-Specific Resources

The Snapmaker comes with Snapmaker Luban software for slicing models. Check the official Snapmaker website for their educational materials and community forum. They often have project ideas suitable for beginners.

## Project Ideas to Start With

Simple items like keychains, cookie cutters, phone stands, pencil holders, or custom name tags work well for building confidence. These teach fundamental concepts without being overwhelming.

The key is starting simple with Tinkercad, then gradually moving to more sophisticated tools as her interest and skills develop. Most importantly, let her creativity drive the projects she wants to make!
