Here’s a classic **chicken pot pie recipe** you can make at home:

---

### Ingredients
**For the filling:**
- 2 cups cooked chicken (shredded or cubed, rotisserie works well)
- 1 cup carrots (diced)
- 1 cup peas (frozen is fine)
- 1 cup potatoes (diced, optional)
- ½ cup celery (diced)
- 1 small onion (chopped)
- 4 tbsp butter
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 cup milk or half-and-half
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp thyme (dried)
- ¼ tsp garlic powder (optional)

**For the crust:**
- 2 pie crusts (homemade or store-bought, top & bottom)

---

### Instructions
1. **Preheat oven** to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add onions, carrots, celery, and potatoes. Cook until softened, about 5–7 minutes.
3. Stir in flour and cook for 1 minute to form a roux.
4. Slowly whisk in chicken broth and milk. Stir until mixture thickens and becomes creamy (5–7 minutes).
5. Add cooked chicken, peas, salt, pepper, and thyme. Stir well, then remove from heat.
6. **Assemble pie:** Place bottom crust in a 9-inch pie dish. Pour filling into crust. Cover with top crust, crimp edges, and cut slits for steam to escape.
7. Bake for 30–35 minutes until crust is golden brown. If edges brown too quickly, cover them with foil.
8. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.

---

### Variations
- **Bisquick topping:** Instead of pie crust, use a biscuit topping (drop biscuit batter over filling and bake).
- **Casserole style:** Pour filling into a dish and top with puff pastry for a flakier crust.
- **Herbs:** Fresh parsley, sage, or rosemary can be added for extra flavor.
