### Quick scam-likelihood checklist (common on TikTok)
It’s *often* a scam (or at least misleading) if you see any of these:
- **“Guaranteed” money/results**, “anyone can do it,” “no experience,” “passive income”
- **Urgency**: “only today,” “limited spots,” “DM me ‘INFO’”
- **Vague mechanism**: no clear, testable explanation of *how* it works
- **Fake proof**: income screenshots, Stripe/PayPal dashboards,

“withdrawals” with no context
- **Upfront payment**: course fee, “processing,” “verification,” “shipping,” “activation”
- **Redirect off-platform** to Telegram/WhatsApp/a sketchy site
- **Recruiting focus** (affiliate/MLM): money mainly from getting others to join
- **“Secret loophole”** claims: bank “glitches,” coupon “exploits,” government “unclaimed money” requiring fees

### How to verify fast (before paying/clicking anything)
- Ask: **What is the product/service? Who pays whom?** Where does the money come from?
- Google the **company/site name + “scam” + “reviews”** (look for *consistent* complaints).
- Check if there’s a **real business model** (terms, refund policy, physical address, support).
- If it’s about **government grants/unclaimed funds**, only trust **official government domains** (they won’t ask for upfront fees via DMs).
