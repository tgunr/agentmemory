### 👤 User

How to cancel or stop iCloud from downloading files and folder?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To stop iCloud from automatically downloading files and folders to your Mac, you can adjust your iCloud Drive settings. This process involves disabling the feature that syncs your Desktop and Documents folders or turning off iCloud Drive entirely for the device.

### Stop Syncing Desktop and Documents

If you want to keep iCloud Drive active but stop it from automatically managing your main folders, follow these steps:

1.  Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner and select System Settings.
2.  Click on your name or Apple ID at the top of the sidebar, then select iCloud.
3.  Click on iCloud Drive.
4.  Look for the option labeled Desktop & Documents Folders and toggle it off.
5.  A warning will appear stating that files will stay in iCloud and be removed from your Mac; you can then manually move them back from the iCloud Drive folder in Finder if needed.

### Disable iCloud Drive Completely

If you want to stop all iCloud Drive activity on your Mac:

1.  Go to System Settings and click your name, then

iCloud.
2.  Select iCloud Drive.
3.  Toggle the switch for iCloud Drive to Off.
4.  You will be asked if you want to keep a copy of your iCloud data on the Mac; if you choose not to, the files will remain in the cloud but be removed from your local storage.

### Manage "Optimize Mac Storage"

If your primary concern is iCloud downloading files to save space or filling up your hard drive, you can toggle the Optimize Mac Storage setting:

1.  In the iCloud settings menu, find the Optimize Mac Storage toggle.
2.  When this is on, your Mac will keep only recently opened files on your disk when space is low.
3.  Turning this off will force the Mac to download and keep a local copy of every file stored in iCloud Drive, provided you have enough disk space.

For individual files or folders that are currently downloading, you can sometimes right-click the item in Finder and select Remove Download to keep the file in the cloud while freeing up local space, though this does not "cancel" an active sync process globally.

### 👤 User

I don't want to turn it off, I just want to stop the current folder from downloading. How do I even tell which folder is being downloaded?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To identify which folder is currently downloading and stop it without turning off iCloud entirely, you can use the built-in status indicators in Finder.

### How to Tell Which Folder is Downloading

You can see the download progress directly in your Finder sidebar or within the iCloud Drive folder:

1.  **Check the Sidebar:** Open a Finder window. Look at **iCloud Drive** in the left sidebar. If a download is active, a small **pie chart icon** will appear next to it. Clicking this icon often reveals a progress bar for the overall transfer.
2.  **Enable the iCloud Status Column:**
    *   Open your **iCloud Drive** folder in Finder.
    *   Change the view to **List View** (Command + 2).
    *   Right-click the column headers (like Name or Date Modified) and select **iCloud Status**.
    *   Look for the **Pie Chart icon** next to specific folders or files. This indicates they are currently in the process of downloading [Apple

Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/check-icloud-drive-file-folder-status-mac-mchlc994344b/mac).
3.  **Look for the Cloud Icon:** A cloud icon with a downward arrow indicates the file is only in the cloud and *not* yet downloading. Once it starts, that icon turns into the progress pie chart.
