Yes, you can use AirPods 2 with an Apple TV. AirPods 2 are compatible with Apple TV 4th generation and later models. Connecting your AirPods to your Apple TV allows you to enjoy audio privately or enhance your listening experience.

**Here's how to connect your AirPods 2 to Apple TV:**

1. **Ensure Both Devices Are Updated:** Make sure your Apple TV is running the latest version of tvOS, and your AirPods are charged.

2. **Automatic Connection (if using the same Apple ID):**
   - If your Apple TV and AirPods are linked to the same Apple ID, your AirPods should automatically appear as an audio option.
   - On your Apple TV remote, press and hold the **Play/Pause** button to bring up the audio output options.
   - Select your AirPods from the list.

3. **Manual Pairing:**
   - On your Apple TV, go to **Settings** > **Remotes and Devices** > **Bluetooth**.
   - Open the lid of your AirPods case with the AirPods inside.
   - Press and hold the **setup button** on the back of the case until the status light flashes white.
   - Your AirPods should appear in the list of available devices on your Apple TV. Select them to pair.

**Benefits of Using AirPods with Apple TV:**

- **Private Listening:** Watch movies or play games without disturbing others.
- **Seamless Switching:** Easily switch audio between other Apple devices logged into the same Apple ID.
- **Spatial Audio Support:** If using AirPods Pro or AirPods Max, enjoy spatial audio features for an immersive experience (requires compatible Apple TV and content).

**Note:** Features like automatic switching and spatial audio require tvOS 14 or later.
