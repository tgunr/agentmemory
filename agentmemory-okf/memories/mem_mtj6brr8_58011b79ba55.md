I should provide practical methods covering different tools and approaches.There are several ways to reverse a video clip depending on what software you're using:

## Video Editing Software

**Adobe Premiere Pro:**
1. Right-click the video clip in your timeline
2. Select "Speed/Duration"
3. Check "Reverse Speed" and click OK

**Final Cut Pro:**
1. Select the clip in the timeline
2. Go to Modify > Retime > Reverse Clip

**DaVinci Resolve:**
1. Right-click the clip in the timeline
2. Select "Change Clip Speed"
3. Check "Reverse" and apply

**iMovie:**
1. Select the clip in your timeline
2. Click the speed button (speedometer icon)
3. Choose "Reverse"

## Free Alternatives

**OpenShot (free):**
1. Right-click the video clip
2. Select "Time" > "Reverse"

**VLC Media Player:**
1. Go to Tools > Effects and Filters
2. Video Effects tab > Geometry
3. Check "Transform" and select "Rotate by 180 degrees" (though this rotates rather than reverses time)

## Command Line (FFmpeg)

If you're comfortable with command line tools:
```
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf reverse output.mp4
```

## Online Tools

Several websites offer video reversal services like Kapwing, EZGIF, or Clideo where you can upload your video and download the reversed version.

The method you choose depends on your current setup and whether you need additional editing features beyond just reversing the clip.
