### How to make sure it fits an 11×14 plaque cleanly
- Choose a design with a **wide wingspan composition** (horizontal layout usually fits 11×14 better).
- In your CAM, set the **job size to 11×14**, then scale the eagle to leave a **border margin** (often 0.5"–1" looks good on plaques).
- Prefer files advertised as **3D relief / CNC carving file** (not just “image” or “clipart”).
