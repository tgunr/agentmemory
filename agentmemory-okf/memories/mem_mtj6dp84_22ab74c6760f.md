In Windsurf editor, you can move panels around by dragging and dropping them. Here's how to move the Klein panel to the right of the Explorer:

1. **Click and hold** on the Klein panel's title bar (where it says "Klein")
2. **Drag** it to the right side of the Explorer panel
3. You should see a **drop zone indicator** appear showing where the panel will be placed
4. **Release** the mouse button when you see the indicator in the desired position

Alternatively, you can:
- **Right-click** on the Klein panel tab and look for "Move Panel" or similar options in the context menu
- Use the **View menu** at the top to access panel layout options
- Look for a **panel management icon** (usually three dots or a grid icon) on the Klein panel that might give you repositioning options

The exact method might vary slightly depending on your Windsurf version, but drag-and-drop is typically the most straightforward approach for rearranging panels in most modern code editors.
