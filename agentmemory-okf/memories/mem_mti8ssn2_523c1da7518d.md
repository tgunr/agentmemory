### 🤖 Assistant

To install Google Drive for desktop, you can follow these steps:

1.  Visit the official [Google Drive download page](https://www.google.com/drive/download/).
2.  Click the **Download Drive for desktop** button.
3.  Once the installer (GoogleDriveSetup.exe for Windows or GoogleDrive.dmg for Mac) has finished downloading, open it and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.
4.  After installation, sign in with your Google account to begin syncing your files.

---

### 🤖 Assistant

If you have already installed Google Drive for desktop and are looking for it in your computer's file explorer (Windows Explorer or macOS Finder), it typically appears as a dedicated virtual drive or a shortcut in the sidebar.

#### On Windows
In File Explorer, you should see a new drive letter (usually **Google Drive (G:)**) listed under **This PC** in the left sidebar. If it doesn't appear there, look for a "Google Drive" shortcut under your **Quick Access** or **Favorites** section at the very top of the sidebar.

#### On macOS
In Finder, Google Drive usually appears under the **Locations** section in the sidebar. It will look like a mounted disk or a folder named "Google Drive."

#### Troubleshooting
If you don't see it in either location, ensure the application is actually running. Check your system tray (bottom right on Windows) or menu bar (top right on Mac) for the Google Drive icon. If the icon is missing, you may need to launch the "Google Drive" application from your Start menu or Applications

folder.
