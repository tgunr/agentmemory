### 👤 User

Beef sirloin, onion, stewed tomatoes, black garlic, harissa, potatoes. What recipes can use these

---

### 🤖 Assistant

That is a fantastic combination of ingredients that balances the richness of sirloin with the deep, umami sweetness of black garlic and the spicy, smoky kick of harissa.

Here are three distinct recipes you can make using these ingredients:

### 1. Spicy Harissa & Black Garlic Beef Stew
This is a slow-cooked, comforting dish where the stewed tomatoes and harissa create a vibrant base, and the black garlic melts into the sauce to provide a balsamic-like depth.

*   **Preparation:** Cube the beef sirloin and potatoes. Slice the onions.
*   **Method:** Sear the beef in a heavy pot until browned, then remove. Sauté the onions until soft, then stir in a generous spoonful of harissa and several mashed cloves of black garlic. Add the stewed tomatoes (breaking them up with a spoon) and the potatoes. Return the beef to the pot, add enough water or broth to cover, and simmer on low heat for about 45–60 minutes until the beef and potatoes are tender.
*   **Why it works:** The acidity of the tomatoes cuts through the

richness of the sirloin, while the black garlic rounds out the heat from the harissa.

### 2. Harissa-Glazed Sirloin with Black Garlic Mashed Potatoes
If you prefer a more "steak and potatoes" presentation, this deconstructed approach highlights the individual textures of the ingredients.

*   **Preparation:** Keep the sirloin as steaks or thick medallions. Peel and boil the potatoes.
*   **Method:** Mash the boiled potatoes with butter, a splash of milk, and 3–4 cloves of finely minced black garlic. For the beef, rub the sirloin with a mixture of harissa and oil, then pan-sear to your preferred doneness. In the same pan, sauté the onions and stewed tomatoes with a pinch of sugar to create a thick, jammy relish to serve over the steak.
*   **Why it works:** The black garlic turns the mashed potatoes into a gourmet side dish, and the tomato-onion relish provides a bright contrast to the spicy steak.

### 3. North African-Inspired Beef & Potato Hash
This is a quicker, one-pan meal that works well for a hearty

dinner or even a bold breakfast.

*   **Preparation:** Dice the beef, potatoes, and onions into small, uniform cubes (about 1/2 inch).
*   **Method:** Par-boil the potato cubes for 5 minutes so they are partially cooked. In a large skillet, fry the beef cubes over high heat until crispy, then remove. Add the onions and potatoes to the pan, frying until the potatoes are golden and crisp. Stir in the harissa, mashed black garlic, and the stewed tomatoes (drained of excess liquid). Toss the beef back in and cook for another 2 minutes to marry the flavors.
*   **Why it works:** The high-heat frying caramelizes the onions and potatoes, which pairs perfectly with the concentrated flavor of the black garlic and the heat of the harissa.
