### 👤 User

What year was the Vietnam wall built?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

The Vietnam Veterans Memorial Wall was built in **1982**.

It was authorized by Congress in 1980, and construction was completed in late October 1982. The wall was officially dedicated on **November 13, 1982**, in Washington, D.C. It was designed by Maya Lin, who was a Yale University student at the time she won the national design competition for the project.

While the main wall was completed in 1982, other parts of the memorial site were added later:
*   **The Three Servicemen statue** was added in 1984.
*   **The Vietnam Women's Memorial** was dedicated in 1993.
*   **The "In Memory" plaque** was added in 2004.

For more information, you can visit the [Vietnam Veterans Memorial Fund](https://www.vvmf.org/About-The-Wall/) or the [National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/vietnam-veterans-memorial.htm) websites.
