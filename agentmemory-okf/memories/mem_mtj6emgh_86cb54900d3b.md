### Best all-in-one option: Pot Roast / Dutch Oven Roast
Brown the beef roast, then braise it low and slow with:
- The onion (quartered)
- Carrots (cut into big chunks)
- Potatoes (halved or quartered, leave skins on)
- Fennel (cut into wedges — it becomes sweet and mild when cooked)

Add a little beef broth, red wine or water, salt, pepper, garlic, and herbs (thyme, rosemary, or bay leaf). Cook until the beef is tender.  

**Zucchini note:** Add the zucchini only in the last 20–30 minutes so it doesn’t turn to mush. Or roast the zucchini separately and serve alongside.

### Other good options
- **Sheet-pan roast dinner**: Sear or season the beef, then roast it on a sheet pan with the potatoes, carrots, onion, and fennel tossed in oil, salt, and pepper. Add zucchini toward the end.
- **Beef stew**: Cube the roast (or use leftovers), then simmer with the same vegetables for a hearty stew. Again, hold the zucchini until near the end.
- **Roasted vegetable side + sliced roast**: Cook the beef simply (season and roast or slow-cook), then roast the potatoes, carrots, onion, and fennel together. Quickly sauté or roast the zucchini separately as a bright side.

Fennel works especially well with beef — it softens and takes on a mild anise sweetness that pairs nicely with the rich meat. The mix of red and yellow potatoes gives good texture and color.

**Pot Roast Seasoning Guide**

### 1. Season the Beef (Most Important Step)
Pat the roast dry, then season generously on all sides.

**Base (do this every time):**
- Kosher salt — 1 to 1½ tsp per pound of meat
- Freshly ground black pepper — generous amount

**Strongly recommended add-ons:**
- Garlic powder — 1–2 tsp
- Onion powder — 1–2 tsp
- Paprika (sweet or smoked) — 1–2 tsp

Optional but excellent:
- Dried thyme or rosemary — 1 tsp
- A light dusting of flour (helps browning and thickens the gravy later)

Let the seasoned roast sit 20–30 minutes at room temperature (or up to overnight in the fridge) before searing.

### 2. Aromatics & Herbs for the Pot
These go in with the meat and liquid:

| Ingredient          | Amount (for 3–4 lb roast) | Notes |
|---------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| Onion               | 1 large, quartered        | You already have this |
| Garlic              | 4–6 cloves, smashed       | Fresh is best |
| Fresh thyme         | 4–6 sprigs                | Or 1 tsp dried |
| Fresh rosemary      | 2–3 sprigs                | Or ½–1 tsp dried |
| Bay leaves          | 2                         | Remove before serving |
| Fennel              | Your bulb, cut into wedges| Adds mild sweetness that pairs beautifully with beef |

### 3. Braising Liquid Flavor Boosters
After searing the meat and sautéing the onion/garlic:

- 1–2 cups beef broth (low-sodium preferred)
- ½–1 cup dry red wine (optional but highly recommended)
- 1–2 Tbsp tomato paste (adds depth and color)
- 1–2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Optional: 1 Tbsp soy sauce or a splash of balsamic vinegar for extra umami
