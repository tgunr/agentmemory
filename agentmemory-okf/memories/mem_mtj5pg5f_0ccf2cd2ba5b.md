### 🤖 Assistant

Here's a creative recipe for catfish with a peach sauce:

### Ingredients:

- Catfish fillets
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil
- Fresh peaches
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Honey
- White wine vinegar
- Fresh basil
- Chili flakes (optional)

### Instructions:

1. **Peach Sauce Preparation:**
   - Peel and chop the peaches.
   - In a saucepan, combine peaches, minced garlic, grated ginger, honey, a splash of white wine vinegar, and a bit of water.
   - Simmer until the peaches are soft.
   - Blend the mixture until smooth. Add chopped basil and chili flakes if desired. Season with salt to taste.

2. **Catfish Preparation:**
   - Season the catfish fillets with salt and pepper.
   - Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
   - Cook the catfish until golden brown and cooked through, about 4-5 minutes per side.

3. **Serving:**
   - Plate the catfish and generously spoon the peach sauce over the fillets.
   - Garnish with additional basil leaves.

Enjoy this unique combination of savory catfish and sweet, slightly spicy peach sauce!
