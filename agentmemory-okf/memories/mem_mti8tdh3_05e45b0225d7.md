The Director of the **United States Marshals Service (USMS)** is **Gadyaces S. Serralta**.

Director Serralta was sworn into office on August 1, 2025, after being nominated by President Donald Trump and confirmed by the U.S. Senate. He is the 13th director of the agency and leads an organization of more than 5,000 employees responsible for fugitive apprehension, judicial security, and federal prisoner operations.

Before taking this national leadership role, Serralta served as the U.S. Marshal for the Southern District of Florida and spent 35 years in law enforcement, including a lengthy career with the Miami-Dade Police Department.
