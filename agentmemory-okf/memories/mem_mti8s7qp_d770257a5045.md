### 👤 User

I am spending about $100 per month on ai models. I am thinking about getting a computer just for local ai models using Ollama or similar. What would you recommend?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

If you're already spending around $100/month on AI services, a dedicated local AI machine can make sense, but the right choice depends on what models you actually use.

A few questions determine everything:

- Do you mainly use text models (Llama, Qwen, DeepSeek, Gemma)?
- Do you want image generation (FLUX, SDXL)?
- Do you want coding models running locally?
- Are you okay with quantized models, or do you want the largest models possible?
- Do you need fast responses, or just privacy and low ongoing cost?

For most people in 2026, I would recommend prioritizing VRAM above almost everything else.

### Option 1: Best value local AI box (~$2,000–$3,000)

- Ryzen 7 9700X or Ryzen 9 9900X
- 64 GB DDR5 RAM
- NVIDIA RTX 5090 32 GB VRAM (if available within budget)
- 2–4 TB NVMe SSD
- 1000W+ quality PSU

Why:
- 32 GB VRAM is the sweet spot for local AI.
- Runs most 32B models comfortably.
- Excellent for Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, Open WebUI.
- Great for FLUX image generation.

### Option 2

: Budget AI workstation (~$1,200–$1,800)

- Ryzen 7 7700
- 64 GB RAM
- RTX 5080 16 GB or used RTX 4090 24 GB
- 2 TB SSD

Why:
- 24 GB VRAM (4090) remains extremely attractive.
- Can run:
  - Qwen3 30B quantized
  - DeepSeek-R1 distilled models
  - Llama 3.x 70B at lower speeds using CPU offload
- Excellent coding assistant.

### Option 3: Serious enthusiast machine (~$4,000–$8,000)

- Dual 5090s or multiple professional GPUs
- 128–256 GB RAM

Why:
- Enables larger 70B+ models with better performance.
- Useful if you're replacing cloud usage entirely.

### What local models are actually good?

For general use:

- Qwen3 32B
- DeepSeek-R1 distilled variants
- Llama 4 local releases (if available in your preferred tooling)
- Gemma 3

For coding:

- Qwen-Coder
- DeepSeek-Coder
- Open-source coding models derived from Qwen

For reasoning:

- DeepSeek-R1 variants
- Qwen reasoning models

### What most people underestimate

The challenge isn't the CPU or RAM.

It's VRAM.

Approximate experience:

- 8 GB VRAM: frustrating
- 12 GB VRAM: entry level
- 16 GB VRAM: usable
- 24 GB VRAM: excellent
- 32 GB VRAM: ideal
- 48+ GB VRAM: enthusiast/professional

### Financial perspective

At $100/month:

- Annual spend: ~$1,200
- Two years: ~$2,400
- Three years: ~$3,600

A $2,500 AI workstation can pay for itself if it substantially replaces your subscriptions.

That said, many people end up keeping one cloud subscription anyway because frontier models (GPT-5-class, Claude Opus-class, Gemini Ultra-class, etc.) still outperform local models on many complex tasks.

My practical recommendation today would be:

- Buy a used RTX 4090 (24 GB) if you can find one at a good price.
- Build around a Ryzen 7 or Ryzen 9.
- Install Ollama + Open WebUI.
- Use local models for 80–90% of your work.
- Keep one premium cloud subscription for the hardest tasks.

If you tell me:

- your exact monthly AI subscriptions,
- whether you want text only or images too,
- and your budget,

I can suggest a specific parts list.
