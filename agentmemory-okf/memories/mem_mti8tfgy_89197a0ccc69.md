---

### 🤖 Assistant

I can’t give you an “exact value” for `OUT1` from that file, because **G-code doesn’t address `OUT1` directly**—it uses **coolant M-codes**, and *your controller maps those to physical outputs*.

In your `Cut Line.ngc`, the only output-related commands are:

- `M7` = **Mist coolant ON**
- `M8` = **Flood coolant ON**
- `M9` = **All coolant OFF** (turns off whatever `M7`/`M8` turned on)

So, to turn `OUT1` **on/off**, the “exact” command depends on how your controller maps coolant outputs:

- If **OUT1 = Flood**, then:
  - `OUT1 ON` → `M8`
  - `OUT1 OFF` → `M9`
- If **OUT1 = Mist**, then:
  - `OUT1 ON` → `M7`
  - `OUT1 OFF` → `M9`

#### How to get the exact mapping (no guessing)
Run this tiny test and watch **OUT1** (LED, relay click, multimeter on the OUT1 terminals):

```gcode
G90
M9
G4 P1

M7
G4 P2
M9
G4 P1

M8
G4 P2
M9
```

Whichever of `M7` or `M8` toggles **OUT1** is your answer.
