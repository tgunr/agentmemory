Let me explain what 10-point touch means and how it relates to using a touchscreen as a mouse replacement."10-point touch" means the touchscreen can simultaneously detect and track up to 10 separate touch points (like fingers) at the same time. This is called "multi-touch" capability.

For using a touchscreen like a mouse, this is actually more than you need - a single touch point would be sufficient for basic mouse functions like clicking, dragging, and moving a cursor. However, multi-touch support gives you additional capabilities:

**Basic mouse functions work with just 1 touch point:**
- Tap = left click
- Tap and hold = right click (usually)
- Drag = click and drag

**Multi-touch adds gesture support:**
- Two-finger scrolling
- Pinch to zoom in/out
- Rotate gestures
- Multi-finger swipes

**Why 10 points matters:**
- More responsive and accurate tracking
- Better palm rejection (ignoring accidental touches)
- Support for multiple users touching simultaneously
- Future-proofing for apps that use complex gestures

For your mouse replacement needs, any multi-touch screen (even 2-point) would work fine, but 10-point is common in quality monitors and ensures smooth, reliable operation. The main things to focus on for mouse-like usage are the screen's responsiveness, accuracy, and whether it supports the specific gestures your operating system uses.
