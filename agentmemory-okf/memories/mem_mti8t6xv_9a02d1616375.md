### 🤖 Assistant

Here are some solid places to find 3D models for CNC carving:

### Good websites for CNC carving models

- [3DWave](https://3dwave.us/)  
  Paid STL files specifically for CNC wood carving.

- [CNC Design Hub](https://cncdesignhub.com/)  
  Large library of CNC-focused STL and DXF designs, especially decorative reliefs and ornamental work.

- [DXF Home – Free STL Files for CNC Routers & 3D Carving](https://dxfhome.com/3d-stl/)  
  Free STL files aimed at CNC routing and relief carving.

- [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/)  
  Huge free model repository. Best if you search specific terms like `relief`, `plaque`, `panel`, or your exact project type.

- [CGTrader](https://www.cgtrader.com/)  
  Mix of paid and free STL files. Good filtering and lots of high-quality models.

- [Thangs](https://thangs.com/)  
  Large repository/search engine for 3D models, including free files that can work for CNC.

- [TurboSquid](https://www.turbosquid.com/)  
  Mostly paid, but useful if you filter by STL and search

for relief-style models.

- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/)  
  Very good for CNC-ready paid STL bundles, plaques, flags, wildlife, signs, and decorative carvings.

### Helpful guides on where to look
- [Carbide 3D – 3D Models for CNC: Find & Use Them](https://carbide3d.com/learn/3d-models-for-cnc/)  
- [Inventables – 8 Best Sites to Download STL Files](https://www.inventables.com/blogs/inventables-blogs/8-best-sites-to-download-stl-files-for-3d-carving)  
- [Snapmaker – Models, Designs and Videos for CNC Carving](https://www.snapmaker.com/blog/going-from-art-to-part-models-designs-and-videos-for-cnc-carving/)

### Quick tip
For CNC carving, the best files are usually:
- `STL` format
- relief or flat-backed models
- not overly dense/huge meshes
