What is the going market rate for four-year-old Aberdeen cows in East Texas?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Short answer: In East Texas, good 4-year-old bred cows are generally trading around $2,200–$3,200 per head right now, with quality, months bred, and papers driving the high end. If she’s with a calf at side (pair), figure roughly $3,000–$3,800 per pair. Open (not bred) cows can be closer to $1,300–$1,900 depending on flesh and kind.

Why I’m saying that:
- Emory Livestock (northeast/east TX) most recent sale shows stocker cows $1,900–$3,000/hd and pairs $2,500–$3,550/pr [Emory Livestock Market Report, Oct 4, 2025](http://emorylivestockauction.com/emory/emory-market-reports/).
- East Texas Livestock (Crockett) reported stocker cows $1,850–$3,350/hd and pairs $3,300–$4,000/pr on 7/1/25 [Southern Livestock – East Texas Livestock](https://southernlivestock.com/market-reports/).
- Broader Texas references: Producers San Angelo reported bred cows $2,550–$3,250/hd and pairs $2,900–$3,950/pr (10/2/25) [Producers Livestock, San Angelo](https://www.producersandcargile.com/market-report). West Auction (central TX)

showed “good pregnancy tested cows” $3,000–$4,300/hd; 6–7 yr PT cows $1,800–$3,000 (10/2/25) [West Auction](https://www.westauction.us/marketreport.cfm).

Notes on “Aberdeen”:
- If you mean Aberdeen Angus-type commercial cows, there’s usually no special premium beyond quality, condition, and vet status.
- If you mean registered “Aberdeen” (Lowline) cattle, that’s a niche; prices swing more with registration, frame size, and private-treaty demand.

If you can share whether she’s bred (and how many months), open, or a pair, plus registered vs. commercial, I can zero in on a tighter number and even check the latest report from your nearest sale barn.
