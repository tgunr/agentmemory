Describe for me methods in my macOS for saving websites and URLs and PDFs, categorized by topics for later review and analysis. I want to be able to use AI prompts like "what is the best course to go with agentic coding."

### 🤖 Assistant — 2025-08-06T11:57:31Z

Here are the best methods for organizing web content on macOS for AI-assisted retrieval and analysis:

## Browser-Based Solutions

**Safari Reading List & Bookmarks**
- Use bookmark folders to categorize by topic (AI/ML, Coding, Business, etc.)
- Reading List saves articles offline for later access
- Export bookmarks as HTML files that AI can analyze
- Limited search capabilities within categories

**Arc Browser Collections**
- Create topic-based "Spaces" for different subjects
- Collections feature automatically groups related tabs
- Built-in notes for each saved item
- Better organization than traditional bookmarks

## Dedicated Knowledge Management Apps

**Obsidian**
- Save web clippings with the Web Clipper extension
- Create interconnected notes with tags and links
- Excellent for building a knowledge graph
- AI can analyze your entire vault of connected information

**Notion**
- Web Clipper saves full articles with metadata
- Database views for filtering by topic, date, or custom properties
- Built-in AI features for querying your saved content
- Great for collaborative research

**Logseq or Roam Research**
- Block-based note-taking with bidirectional links
- Daily notes capture with automatic backlinking
- Graph database structure ideal for AI analysis

## PDF and Document Management

**DevonThink**
- OCR for searchable PDFs
- Automatic categorization using AI
- Smart groups that auto-organize content
- Excellent search and retrieval capabilities

**Zotero**
- Academic paper management with web snapshots
- Automatic metadata extraction
- Tag-based organization
- Integrates well with research workflows

## AI-Optimized Approaches

**Readwise + Reader**
- Saves articles, PDFs, and web content
- AI-powered tagging and summarization
- Exports to other tools like Obsidian
- Built for knowledge workers who want AI assistance

**Cubox or Matter**
- AI-powered content analysis and tagging
- Automatic summarization
- Search within saved content
- Mobile apps for capturing on-the-go

## Recommended Workflow

For your specific use case of querying with AI prompts like "what is the best course for agentic coding," I'd suggest:

1. **Primary system**: Obsidian or Notion with consistent tagging (use tags like #agentic-ai, #coding-courses, #tutorials)
2. **Web capture**: Use the respective web clipper to save articles with relevant tags
3. **PDF storage**: Import PDFs directly into your chosen system with descriptive titles
4. **AI integration**: Export your knowledge base periodically or use tools that have built-in AI features

This approach ensures your saved content is both human-browsable and AI-analyzable, making it easy to get comprehensive answers to specific queries about your research topics.
