### 🤖 Assistant

Got it 👍 You’re asking how to **repair an AirWeights (vacuum workholding) table that has had a hole drilled all the way through the surface**. Because the vacuum table relies on an airtight surface for holding power, a through-hole can cause major leaks. Here are some practical repair approaches depending on how permanent you want it to be:  

---

### 🔹 Quick Fix (Temporary)
- **Vacuum seal tape / Aluminum HVAC tape**:  
  - Clean the area thoroughly (degrease first).  
  - Apply a strong vacuum-rated tape and press firmly.  
  - You can reinforce with a thin metal backing plate glued down.  
  - Works for light-duty use but not ideal for heavy machining.

---

### 🔹 Permanent Repair Options
1. **Epoxy Plug Fill**
   - Clean and roughen around the hole.  
   - Fill the hole with a high-strength epoxy (e.g., JB Weld SteelStik or West System epoxy with filler).  
   - Sand flush with the surface once cured.  
   - Reseal with a thin epoxy top coat to ensure airtightness.  

2. **Threaded Plug Insert**
   -

Drill/tap the hole to a standard size (M6, 1/4-20, etc.).  
   - Install a short **set screw / grub screw** with thread sealant (Loctite 545 or Teflon paste).  
   - Sand or grind flush with the table surface.  
   - This is a rugged, serviceable fix.  

3. **Metal Plug / Press Fit**
   - Turn a small plug (aluminum/brass/stainless depending on your table) slightly oversize.  
   - Lightly press fit with some epoxy.  
   - Finish flush on the surface.  

---

### 🔹 Pro Tip
- After sealing, **resurface the vacuum table** (light face milling or lapping) if you need precision flatness.  
- Always test with vacuum gauge and leak detection (isopropyl alcohol or soapy water spray) before running parts.
