### 🤖 Assistant

Skillet cobblers are a classic comfort dessert, prized for the way a cast-iron pan creates crisp, buttery edges while keeping the center soft and cake-like. There are two main styles: the "dump and bake" method where fruit is placed over a simple batter, and the "biscuit/cookie" style where a thicker dough is dolloped over pre-cooked fruit.

### Southern Peach Skillet Cobbler
This version uses the traditional Southern method where the batter rises through the fruit as it bakes, creating a golden, crusty top.

**Ingredients:**
*   **Base:** 1/2 cup salted butter.
*   **Batter:** 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1 cup granulated sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder, pinch of salt, and 3/4 cup whole milk.
*   **Fruit:** 4 cups sliced peaches (fresh or canned), 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp almond extract, and 1/4 cup light brown sugar.

**Instructions:**
1.  **Melt Butter:** Place the butter in a 10.5-inch cast-iron skillet and put it in the oven while it preheats to 350°F. Remove once melted.
2.  **Prep Fruit:** Toss the peaches

with cinnamon and almond extract.
3.  **Layer:** Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, and milk together. Pour this batter directly over the melted butter in the hot skillet—**do not stir**.
4.  **Top:** Arrange the peaches over the batter and sprinkle with brown sugar.
5.  **Bake:** Bake for 50–60 minutes until the top is golden brown. The fruit will sink to the bottom while the cake rises.
*Source: [7 Empty Plates](https://7emptyplates.com/southern-peach-cobbler/)*

### Brown Sugar & Pecan Skillet Cobbler
For a richer, more textured dessert, this recipe features a "cookie-like" topping and browned butter.

**Ingredients:**
*   **Fruit Filling:** 4 tbsp salted butter, 5–6 ripe peaches (sliced), 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 tbsp bourbon (optional), and 2 tsp vanilla.
*   **Topping:** 1 cup flour, 1/4 cup finely ground pecans, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1/2 cup melted salted butter, and 1/2 cup whole milk.

**Instructions:**
1.  **Brown the Butter:** In a 12-inch skillet over

medium heat, brown 4 tbsp of butter until toasted. Stir in peaches, 1/2 cup brown sugar, bourbon, and vanilla.
2.  **Pre-Bake:** Bake the fruit mixture at 400°F for 10–15 minutes, then reduce oven heat to 375°F.
3.  **Add Topping:** Whisk the dry topping ingredients together, then stir in the melted butter and milk. Dollop spoonfuls of this batter over the hot peaches.
4.  **Final Bake:** Dust with cinnamon sugar and bake for 25–30 minutes until the topping is deep golden brown.
*Source: [Half Baked Harvest](https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/skillet-brown-sugar-peach-cobbler/)*

### Quick 3-Ingredient "Cheat" Cobbler
If you are short on time, you can make a highly effective cobbler using pantry staples.
*   **Ingredients:** 2 cans of fruit pie filling, 1 box of yellow cake mix, and 2 sticks of melted butter.
*   **Method:** Spread half the cake mix in a buttered skillet, add the pie filling, top with the remaining cake mix, and pour melted butter over the top. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
*Source: [The Tipsy

Housewife](https://thetipsyhousewife.org/2024/09/23/skillet-cobbler/)*

### Tips for Success
*   **Don't Stir:** In the traditional Southern method, leaving the layers separate is what allows the batter to "envelop" the fruit properly.
*   **Preheat the Skillet:** Melting the butter in the skillet while the oven heats ensures a crispy bottom crust.
*   **Fruit Variations:** While peach is the standard, you can easily swap in blackberries, blueberries, or thinly sliced apples.
