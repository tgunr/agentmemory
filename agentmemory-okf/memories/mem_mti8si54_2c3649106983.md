### 🤖 Assistant

To export your data from Abacus.AI / ChatLLM:

1. **Individual Conversation Export / Copy:**
   Within any chat session in the web interface, look for the share/export icon (or conversation options menu in the top-right corner of the chat window) to copy, download, or share the contents of that specific conversation.

2. **Account / Project Data:**
   If you are using the Abacus.AI developer or enterprise platform, datasets, models, and artifacts can be downloaded directly from their respective project views or retrieved programmatically via the [Abacus.AI APIs and SDK](https://abacus.ai/help/api/ref/ai_chat/listChatSessions).

3. **Full Data & History Requests:**
   For a complete archive or bulk export of your account history and personal data, you can submit a request directly through your account profile settings or reach out to support at [support@abacus.ai](mailto:support@abacus.ai).
