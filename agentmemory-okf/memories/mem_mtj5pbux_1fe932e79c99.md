### 🤖 Assistant

An inductive proximity switch is a type of non-contact sensor that uses electromagnetic induction to detect the presence of a metallic object. It consists of a coil that generates a magnetic field, and when a metal target enters this field, the change in the magnetic flux is detected, resulting in a switch output. Inductive proximity switches are commonly used for detecting the position or presence of metal parts in industrial automation and machinery applications.
