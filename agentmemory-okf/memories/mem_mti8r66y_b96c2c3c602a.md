### 👤 User

In Aspire how can i evenlu space text lines vertically when all lines are same size?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

In Vectric Aspire, you can evenly space text lines vertically using two primary methods: the manual interactive method or the automated alignment method.

### Method 1: Automated Alignment (Recommended for Precision)
This is the most accurate way to ensure the vertical gap between every line is identical.

1.  **Break the Text Block**: Right-click on your text block and select **Break Text Block into Lines**. This converts your single multi-line text object into individual, separate lines of text.
2.  **Select All Lines**: Click and drag a selection box around all the lines you want to space, or hold `Shift` and click each one.
3.  **Open Alignment Tools**: Click on the **Align Selected Objects** icon in the "Transform Objects" section of the Design tab.
4.  **Space Vertically**: At the bottom of the Alignment menu, look for the **Space Selection** section. Click the **Vertical** icon (it looks like three horizontal bars with arrows between them). This will instantly adjust the lines so the vertical distance

between them is perfectly even.
5.  **Group (Optional)**: Once spaced, you can press `G` on your keyboard to group them back together so they move as one unit again.

### Method 2: Interactive Manual Spacing
If you prefer to eyeball the spacing or make quick adjustments without breaking the text apart, you can use the **Edit Text** tool.

1.  **Select the Tool**: Click the **Edit Text** icon (the "T" with a cursor) or press `T` while the text is selected.
2.  **Position the Cursor**: Hover your mouse in the space between two lines of text. The cursor will change to a vertical line with two arrows pointing up and down.
3.  **Adjust Spacing**:
    *   **Click**: Moves the lines closer together.
    *   **Shift + Click**: Moves the lines further apart.
    *   **Ctrl + Click**: Moves the lines in larger increments.
    *   **Alt + Click**: Applies the spacing change to **all** lines in the text block simultaneously, which is useful for keeping everything uniform.

For more detailed technical steps, you can

refer to the [Vectric Aspire Documentation](https://docs.vectric.com/docs/V12.0/Aspire/ENU/Help/form/Text%20Selection/index.html) or follow this guide on [Spacing Text Lines Evenly](https://www.learnyourcnc.com/blog/space-text-lines-evenly).

### Correct Shortcut and Tool
The shortcut for the **Draw Text** tool is actually **`F9`** (to center) or simply clicking the **Draw Text** (capital 'T' icon) or **Edit Text** (the 'T' with a cursor icon) in the "Create Vectors" section.

#### To adjust spacing once the tool is active:
1.  Select your text and click the **Edit Text** icon (the one that looks like a 'T' with a small cursor next to it).
2.  Move your mouse cursor into the gap between the lines of text. The cursor will change to a vertical line with an up and down arrow.
3.  **Hold the `Alt` key** and click between any two lines. Using the `Alt` key while clicking will apply the spacing change to **every line** in that text block at once, ensuring they stay perfectly even.
    *   **Click**: Decreases spacing for all lines.
    *   **Shift +

Click**: Increases spacing for all lines.
