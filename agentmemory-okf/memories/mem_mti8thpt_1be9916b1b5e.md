### 🤖 Assistant

**The best way to do this is by using a single-use (one-time) virtual credit card number.** These generate a temporary 16-digit card number, expiration date, and CVV that can only be used once (or for a specific merchant/amount). After the transaction, the number is automatically disabled and cannot be reused.

### Top Recommended Option: Privacy.com (Easiest for One-Time Use)
[Privacy.com](https://www.privacy.com/) is specifically built for this exact use case. Their virtual cards can be set to **one-time use**, meaning they automatically close after a single transaction.

**Key features**:
- One-time use cards that expire immediately after the first purchase.
- Merchant-locked cards (they only work at the first merchant they’re used at).
- You can set exact spending limits.
- Works for online/phone purchases.

**How to get started**:
1. Sign up at Privacy.com (free basic plan available; US residents 18+ with a US bank account required).
2. Link a funding source (checking account or debit card).
3. Create a

new virtual card and select the **one-time use** option (or set a very low spending limit).
4. Use the generated card number at checkout.
5. The card automatically expires after the transaction.

Privacy.com is widely praised for fraud protection and is one of the most popular dedicated services for this.

### Other Options
- **Capital One (Eno virtual cards)**: If you have a Capital One credit card, you can generate virtual card numbers through their app, website, or the Eno browser extension. You can create store-specific virtual cards and set some for single use. These are convenient if you already bank with them.
- **Other banks/issuers**: Check your credit card issuer’s app or website (e.g., Citi, American Express, or others). Some offer virtual card numbers or tokenization features. Google Pay/Chrome autofill also supports virtual cards from participating banks.
- **Business-focused options**: Services like Ramp offer unlimited virtual cards with strong controls (more suited for companies).

###

Important Notes
- These virtual numbers are linked to your real funding source (usually a bank account or credit card), so the charge still appears on your statement.
- Availability is mostly limited to the US.
- Privacy.com and similar services do **not** pull your credit or affect your score.
- Always generate a fresh card for each one-time purchase for maximum security.
