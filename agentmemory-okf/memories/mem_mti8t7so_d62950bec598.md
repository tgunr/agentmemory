## Cost Comparison: Kilo.ai vs Abacus.AI for OpenClaw Hosting

Here's a detailed comparison of running OpenClaw on **Kilo.ai (KiloClaw)** versus **Abacus.AI (Abacus Claw)**:

---

### **Kilo.ai (KiloClaw)**

**Hosting Costs:**
- **Currently FREE during beta** (2 shared vCPUs, 3 GB RAM, 10 GB SSD)
- **After beta:**
  - **Month-to-month:** $9/month (first month $4)
  - **6-month commitment:** $8/month (billed as $48 upfront, saves 11%)

**AI Inference Costs:**
- Billed **separately** from hosting
- **0% markup** on provider rates (access to 500+ models including Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek)
- **Free models available**
- Options:
  - **Pay-as-you-go:** Add credits as needed (never expire)
  - **Kilo Pass subscriptions** (with bonus credits):
    - Starter: $19/month → $26.60 in credits
    - Pro: $49/month → $68.60 in credits
    - Expert: $199/month → $278.60 in credits
- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys):** Use your own API keys at no additional cost

**Total Estimated Cost:**
- **During beta:** $0 hosting + AI inference costs
- **After beta:** $8-9/month + AI inference ($20-200+/month depending on usage)
- **Typical range:** $28-209/month for moderate to heavy usage
