- The main Wikipedia entry for the ranch says it “spans **350,000 acres**” in total across its holdings in King, Carson, and Hutchinson counties in Texas ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6666_Ranch)).

- The Texas State Historical Association, looking specifically at the Four Sixes operation in King County, notes that “The Four Sixes Ranch, which occupies some **208,000 acres**, continues to be the primary economic mainstay of King County” ([TSHA](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/four-sixes-ranch)).

So, the number you quote depends on what you mean:

about **350,000 acres**.

### 🤖 Assistant

The ranch most associated with filming the *Yellowstone* TV series is the **Chief Joseph Ranch** in Darby, Montana.  

- In the show, it’s called the **Dutton family’s “Yellowstone Dutton Ranch.”**  
- In real life, the property’s historic name is **Chief Joseph Ranch** (a working ranch and guest ranch in Montana).

The **Four Sixes Ranch (6666 Ranch)** in Texas is also part of the *Yellowstone* universe and was used for filming for the spin‑off **“6666”** and some *Yellowstone* storylines, but the primary “Yellowstone Ranch” with the big log lodge is Chief Joseph Ranch in Montana.
