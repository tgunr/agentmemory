### 🤖 Assistant

Yes, this is almost certainly a scam.

**Why it's a scam:**
- **Too-good-to-be-true pay**: $200–$600 for 1–2 hours daily ($100–$300/hour) is unrealistic for legit remote jobs on platforms like Indeed.
- **Vague details**: No specific job title, company, or responsibilities—just generic "opportunity" after a "profile review"

# ⚠️ Yes, this is almost certainly a scam.

Here are the red flags:

## Critical Warning Signs

**Unrealistic compensation**
- $200–$600/day for 1–2 hours of work is not legitimate. No real employer offers this rate.
- This is the classic hook scammers use to lure desperate job seekers.

**Fake company impersonation**
- Scammers frequently impersonate Indeed. Real Indeed communication happens through their official platform, not SMS to random phone numbers.

**Contact method is wrong**
- Legitimate recruiters use official job boards or email, not text message requests to contact a phone number.
- Asking you to "respond with Details" initiates a conversation they can exploit.

**Generic, mass-appeal language**
- "After reviewing your profile" is templated to anyone.
- Minimum age 25 is suspiciously the only requirement—no actual job skills mentioned.

**No verifiable details**
- No company name, recruiter name, job title, or specific role details.

## What Usually Happens Next

If you respond, scammers

typically:
1. Ask for personal information (SSN, bank details, government ID)
2. Request an "application fee" or "processing fee" upfront
3. Send fake job contracts that look official
4. Commit identity theft or drain your bank account

## What To Do

- **Do not respond** to the phone number
- **Do not click any links** if they send them
- **Report it** to Indeed's official support if you received this claiming to be from them
- **Report it** to the FTC at [reportfraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov)

This is a textbook employment scam. Stay safe and avoid any further engagement.
