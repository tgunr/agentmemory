## ⚠️ First — Important Reality About Diode Lasers & Clear Acrylic

Standard blue diode lasers (≈455nm) **do not cut or engrave clear acrylic directly** because the beam passes through it.

You must use one of these methods:

### ✅ For Engraving Clear Acrylic
1. **Engrave from the back side** (mirror your design)
2. Apply one of:
   - Black tempera paint
   - Flat black spray paint
   - Black vinyl mask
   - Laser marking spray (CerMark/LaserBond)
3. Engrave through the coating — the coating absorbs the laser and etches the acrylic.

### ✅ For Cutting Clear Acrylic with Diode
You must:
- Leave protective paper on
- Or apply masking tape to both sides
- Use multiple passes
- Use strong air assist
- Elevate sheet above honeycomb

Without masking, you will struggle to cut 1/4"

cleanly.

# 🔷 STEP 1 – Prototype (1/8" Cast Acrylic)

### Engraving Strategy (Eagle + Text)
You want:
- Frosted background eagle
- Clean readable text
- Walnut behind it

### ✅ Best Method
**Reverse engrave from the BACK side**

Why:
- Gives smooth glossy front surface
- Text looks premium
- Backlighting optional later

---

### ✅ File Setup
- Mirror entire design horizontally
- Engrave eagle as raster fill
- Engrave text as slightly deeper pass
- Leave front side untouched

---

# 🔥 Engraving Settings (Starting Points)

These are conservative and safe.

## 1/8" Cast Acrylic Engraving (Rear Engrave)

### 24W
- Speed: 3000–4000 mm/min
- Power: 80–100%
- Passes: 1–2
- Line interval: 0.08–0.12mm
- Air assist: LOW (don’t cool too much)

### 44W
- Speed: 4000–6000 mm/min
- Power: 60–80%
- Passes: 1
- Line interval: 0.08mm

### 64W
- Speed: 6000–8000 mm/min
- Power: 50–70%
- Passes: 1
- Line interval: 0.08mm

If it looks glossy instead of frosted → increase power or reduce speed.

---

# 🦅 Eagle Background

Technique (Important)

You want a **light frosted fill**, not deep burning.

Use:
- Dither mode (Jarvis or Stucki)
- Lower power than text
- Slight defocus (+0.5mm) to soften texture

This gives a clean frosted glass effect.

---

# 🔥 Cutting 1/8" Clear Cast Acrylic

With masking applied to BOTH sides.

### 24W
- Speed: 300–500 mm/min
- Power: 100%
- Passes: 6–10
- Strong air assist
- Focus at surface first pass, then refocus slightly lower mid-cut

### 44W
- Speed: 500–700 mm/min
- Power: 100%
- Passes: 4–6

### 64W
- Speed: 700–900 mm/min
- Power: 100%
- Passes: 3–5

✅ Raise acrylic 10–15mm above honeycomb  
✅ Clean edge with flame polishing if needed

---

# 🔷 Mounting to Walnut (Prototype)

Best look:

- Pre-cut 4 mounting holes
- Countersink acrylic lightly (optional)
- Use 1/2" brushed aluminum standoffs
- Walnut sealed with satin polyurethane

Tip:
Drill acrylic with step bit, low speed, backer board.
