### 👤 User

How to use a Vivosun AeroZesh S as a smoke filter for a CNC laser?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Using a Vivosun AeroZesh S (typically the S4 or S6) as a smoke filter for a CNC laser is a common and effective DIY solution for managing fumes and odors. While originally designed for grow tents, its high CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) and compatibility with activated carbon filters make it ideal for laser exhaust systems.

### 1. Required Components
To set this up effectively, you will need:
*   **AeroZesh S Inline Fan:** The "S" series is the standard smart fan.
*   **Activated Carbon Filter:** This is essential for scrubbing the chemical odors and VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds) produced by laser cutting.
*   **Flexible Ducting:** Usually 4-inch or 6-inch, depending on your fan model.
*   **Duct Clamps:** To ensure airtight seals.
*   **Laser Enclosure:** Your CNC laser must be in an enclosure to capture the smoke before it can be filtered.

### 2. Configuration Options
There are two primary ways to orient the system:

#### Option A: Pull Configuration (Recommended)
In this setup, the fan pulls air

through the filter. This is generally more efficient for odor control.
1.  **Laser Enclosure** → **Ducting** → **AeroZesh Fan** → **Carbon Filter**.
2.  The fan "pushes" the dirty air into the filter.
3.  **Benefit:** This allows you to vent the scrubbed air directly back into the room if you cannot vent outside.

#### Option B: Push Configuration (Exhausting Outside)
If you are venting to a window, use the filter as a "pre-filter" to keep the fan clean.
1.  **Carbon Filter** → **AeroZesh Fan** → **Ducting** → **Window/Outside**.
2.  The fan pulls air through the filter first, then pushes it out the vent.
3.  **Benefit:** Protects the fan motor from heavy soot and debris buildup.

### 3. Installation Steps
*   **Seal the Enclosure:** Ensure your CNC laser enclosure is as airtight as possible, leaving only a small intake vent on the opposite side of the exhaust port to create a "cross-flow" of air.
*   **Connect the Ducting:** Use the [Vivosun Duct

Clamps](https://vivosun.com/vivosun-aerozesh-fan-air-filtration-kit-carbon-filter-ducting-p137517152318377993) to attach the ducting to the laser's exhaust port and the AeroZesh intake.
*   **Mount the Fan:** The AeroZesh S comes with a mounting plate. It is best to mount it to a wall or the side of your workbench to reduce vibration.
*   **Install the Filter:** Attach the carbon filter to the exhaust side of the fan. Ensure the "pre-filter" (the white cloth sleeve) is on the outside of the carbon filter to catch large dust particles.

### 4. Operation and Maintenance
*   **Fan Speed:** Use the [E12 or GrowHub controller](https://vivosun.com/vivosun-smart-grow-system-aerozesh-4-inch-kit-p144033807150777094-v145729559024154611) to set the fan to a high speed during cutting. Laser smoke is much denser than grow tent air, so you will likely need to run it at 80-100% power.
*   **Filter Life:** Laser cutting (especially wood and acrylic) will clog a carbon filter much faster than plants will. If you start to

smell "burnt" odors in the room, it is time to replace the carbon canister.
*   **Safety Note:** A carbon filter alone does not remove 100% of toxic sub-micron particulates. If you are cutting materials like PVC (which you should never do) or high volumes of MDF, it is highly recommended to vent the exhaust outside even after it passes through the filter.

For more detailed assembly diagrams, you can refer to the [Vivosun AeroZesh User Manual](https://image.next.vivosun.com/file-asset/file/0c331a41c0f4e107d8028b76af069c08.pdf).
