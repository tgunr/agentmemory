How to rip DVD movies I own into digital files for streaming using MacOS 14?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To rip DVD movies you own into digital files for streaming using macOS 14, follow these steps:

### 1. Install Necessary Software

**HandBrake:** This is a popular open-source video transcoder that can rip DVDs.

1. Go to the [HandBrake website](https://handbrake.fr/).
2. Download the macOS version of HandBrake.
3. Install HandBrake by dragging it to your Applications folder.

**VLC Media Player:** HandBrake relies on VLC to decode the DVD.

1. Go to the [VLC website](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/).
2. Download the macOS version of VLC.
3. Install VLC by dragging it to your Applications folder.

### 2. Rip the DVD

1. **Insert the DVD** into your Mac's DVD drive. If your Mac doesn’t have a built-in DVD drive, you’ll need an external USB DVD drive.

2. **Open HandBrake** from your Applications folder.

3. **Select the Source:**
   - In HandBrake, click on the "Open Source" button.
   - Select your DVD drive from the list of available devices. HandBrake will scan the DVD, which may take a few minutes.

4. **Choose the Title and Chapters:**
   - HandBrake will display a list of titles (movies or episodes) on the DVD. Select the title you want to rip.
   - You can also choose specific chapters if you don't want to rip the entire title.

5. **Set the Destination:**
   - Click the "Browse" button to choose where you want to save the ripped file on your Mac.

6. **Choose a Preset:**
   - HandBrake provides several presets for different devices and quality levels. For general use, the "Fast 1080p30" preset is a good starting point.

7. **Adjust Settings (Optional):**
   - You can customize video settings like codec, quality, and audio tracks if desired.

8. **Start the Ripping Process:**
   - Click the "Start Encode" button at the top of the HandBrake window to begin ripping the DVD. This process may take some time, depending on the length and complexity of the video.

### 3. Stream Your Digital Files

Once the DVD has been ripped, you can use various methods to stream the digital files:

- **AirPlay:** If you have an Apple TV, you can use AirPlay to stream the files from your Mac to your TV.
- **Plex:** Set up a Plex server on your Mac to organize and stream your media files to various devices.
- **VLC:** Use VLC or another media player on your Mac to play the files directly.

By following these steps, you should be able to rip your DVD movies into digital files for easy streaming on macOS 14.
