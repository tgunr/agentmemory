### Garden Vegetable Beef Skillet
This is a quick, one-pan meal that highlights the crunch of the peppers and the earthiness of the mushrooms.

1.  **Prep the Veggies**: Slice the mushrooms, red bell pepper, yellow squash, and a few of those long green chilies. Chop a handful of broccoli into small florets.
2.  **Brown the Beef**: In a large

skillet, brown the hamburger meat over medium-high heat. Drain any excess fat.
3.  **Sauté**: Add the mushrooms, peppers, squash, and broccoli to the pan with the beef. Cook for about 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
4.  **Finish with Greens**: Stir in a large couple of handfuls of the baby spinach at the very end, letting it wilt into the mixture for just a minute.
5.  **Serve**: Top with fresh avocado slices and halved cherry tomatoes for a burst of coolness and acidity.
