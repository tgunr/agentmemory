### 🤖 Assistant

"Genderqueer" is an identity used by people whose gender doesn’t fit neatly into traditional categories of strictly “male” or strictly “female.” It’s an umbrella term that can include:  

- **Nonbinary**: People who identify outside the male/female binary entirely.  
- **Genderfluid**: People whose gender identity shifts over time or depending on context.  
- **Agender**: People who don’t identify with any gender.  
- **Mix of genders**: People who identify with aspects of both male and female genders simultaneously.  

It’s both a **gender identity** and, for some, a **political statement**—rejecting rigid gender norms and affirming a more flexible, self-defined understanding of gender.
