### 🤖 Assistant

For epoxy-filled text carves in Aspire, here are some excellent Helvetica-like fonts that typically have clean, closed vectors:

## Best Options:

**1. Arial** - The most readily available Helvetica alternative. It has very similar proportions and clean, closed paths that work well in Aspire for pocketing toolpaths.

**2. Calibri** - Modern, clean sans-serif with excellent closed vector construction. Works very well for engraving.

**3. Futura** - Geometric sans-serif with simple, clean construction. The letterforms are very uniform and create reliable toolpaths.

**4. DIN** - Industrial-style font with clean, straightforward geometry. Excellent for CNC work.

**5. Roboto** - Free Google font with clean construction, similar feel to Helvetica, and works well in Aspire.

## Tips for Epoxy-Filled Engraving in Aspire:

- **Test before committing**: Create a small test piece with your chosen font to ensure the vectors are closed and the pockets are clean
- **Avoid decorative/script fonts**: Stick with simple

geometric sans-serifs
- **Check your vectors**: In Aspire, select your text and look for any red lines (open vectors) before creating toolpaths
- **Font weight matters**: Regular or Medium weights typically work better than Ultra-Light or Ultra-Bold for engraving

**Arial** is probably your safest bet since it's universally available on Windows and behaves almost identically to Helvetica with reliable closed paths.

### 👤 User

Whats the point in having single line fonts in aspire if they all have open vectors unsuitable for V carving. What are the sans serif fonts that don't have issues and kust be used as is?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

You're absolutely right to be frustrated! **Single line fonts in Aspire are designed for engraving (single-pass centerline cutting), NOT for V-carving.** They're useful for things like diamond drag engraving or single-line text etching, but they're fundamentally incompatible with V-carving because V-carving needs closed vector outlines.

## Sans-Serif Fonts That Work Reliably for V-Carving As-Is:

These standard fonts typically have clean, closed vectors and work without issues:

**Windows Standard Fonts:**
- **Arial** (all weights)
- **Arial Black**
- **Calibri**
- **Tahoma**
- **Trebuchet MS**
- **Verdana**
- **Franklin Gothic Medium**
- **Century Gothic**

**Free Fonts That Work Well:**
- **Roboto** (Google font)
- **Open Sans** (Google font)
- **Montserrat** (Google font)
