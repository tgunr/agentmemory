### 🤖 Assistant

Here’s a quick and delicious **Black-Eyed Peas and Sausage Recipe** that can serve as a hearty meal:

### Ingredients:
- **1 lb sausage** (smoked, Andouille, or your favorite type), sliced
- **2 cups black-eyed peas** (soaked overnight or canned, rinsed and drained)
- **1 medium onion**, chopped
- **2 cloves garlic**, minced
- **1 bell pepper**, diced (green, red, or yellow)
- **2 stalks celery**, diced
- **2 cups chicken broth** (or water)
- **1 teaspoon smoked paprika**
- **1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper** (optional)
- **1/2 teaspoon black pepper**
- **Salt to taste**
- **2 tablespoons olive oil** (or butter)
- **Optional garnish:** chopped green onions or parsley

### Instructions:
1. **Prepare the sausage:**  
   Heat a large skillet or pot over medium heat. Add the olive oil and sliced sausage. Cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes. Remove sausage and set aside.

2. **Sauté the vegetables:**  
   In the same skillet, add the onion, garlic, bell pepper, and celery. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

3. **Combine ingredients:**  
   Add the black-eyed peas, chicken broth, smoked paprika, cayenne pepper (if using), black pepper, and cooked sausage. Stir to combine.

4. **Simmer:**  
   Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 25-30 minutes if using canned peas (or until the flavors meld). If using soaked dry black-eyed peas, simmer for 1-1.5 hours until peas are tender.

5. **Adjust seasoning:**  
   Taste and adjust salt and spices as needed.

6. **Serve:**  
   Serve hot over rice, cornbread, or enjoy as a standalone dish. Garnish with green onions or parsley if desired.

Enjoy this flavorful Southern-inspired dish!
