### 👤 User

What Shopify category should I use for custom decals and coasters?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

For custom decals and coasters, you should use specific categories from Shopify's Standard Product Taxonomy to ensure accurate tax calculations and better visibility on sales channels like Google and Facebook.

### Recommended Categories

#### For Custom Decals
The most accurate category for decorative decals or stickers is:
*   **Arts & Entertainment > Hobbies & Creative Arts > Arts & Crafts > Art & Crafting Materials > Embellishments & Trims > Decorative Stickers**

If your decals are specifically for vehicles or walls, you might also consider:
*   **Vehicle Parts & Accessories > Vehicle Decor > Vehicle Decals**
*   **Home & Garden > Decor > Wall Decor > Wall Stickers & Decals**

#### For Coasters
Coasters fall under the kitchen and dining section:
*   **Home & Garden > Kitchen & Dining > Tableware > Coasters**

### Why Choosing the Right Category Matters
According to the [Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-category), assigning a standard category is important

for several reasons:
*   **Tax Accuracy:** Shopify uses these categories to determine the correct tax rates or exemptions for your products.
*   **Sales Channels:** Standardized categories are required for syncing products with external marketplaces like Facebook, Instagram, and Google.
*   **Category Metafields:** Selecting a standard category unlocks specific "metafields" (attributes like material, color, or size) that help customers filter and find your products more easily.

### Custom Product Types
If the standard categories don't perfectly describe your unique items, you can also use the **Product Type** field. While a product can only have one standard category, the product type is a custom label you create (e.g., "Handmade Marble Coasters" or "Vinyl Laptop Decals") to further organize your internal collections and smart filters.

For more details on managing these settings, you can refer to the [Shopify Standard Product Taxonomy

guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-category).
