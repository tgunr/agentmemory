Great choice! Here are two unique recipes featuring smoked salmon:

### 1. Smoked Salmon and Avocado Sushi Bowl

#### Ingredients:
- 200g smoked salmon, thinly sliced
- 1 cup sushi rice
- 1 avocado, diced
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- 1/2 cup edamame beans, shelled
- 2 tablespoons pickled ginger, chopped
- 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon wasabi paste
- 2 sheets of nori, cut into strips
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced

#### Instructions:
1. **Cook Sushi Rice:** Rinse the sushi rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Cook according to package instructions. Once cooked, stir in rice vinegar and let it cool slightly.
2. **Prepare Ingredients:** While the rice is cooking, prepare the avocado, cucumber, edamame, pickled ginger, and spring onion.
3. **Assemble the Bowl:** In a large bowl, layer the sushi rice at the bottom. Arrange the smoked salmon, avocado, cucumber, edamame, and pickled ginger on top.
4. **Add Flavors:** Drizzle soy sauce over the top and add a dollop of wasabi paste. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and nori strips.
5. **Serve:** Garnish with spring onion and serve immediately.

### 2. Smoked Salmon and Dill Quiche

#### Ingredients:
- 200g smoked salmon, flaked
- 1 pre-made pie crust
- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup grated Gruyère cheese
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tablespoon capers, drained
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced

#### Instructions:
1. **Preheat Oven:** Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Place the pie crust in a quiche or pie pan, trimming any excess edges.
2. **Prepare Filling:** In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, heavy cream, milk, salt, and pepper until smooth. Stir in the grated Gruyère, smoked salmon, fresh dill, capers, and red onion.
3. **Assemble Quiche:** Pour the filling into the prepared pie crust, spreading it out evenly.
4. **Bake:** Bake the quiche in the preheated oven for 35-40 minutes, or until the filling is set and the top is golden brown. Let it cool slightly before slicing.
5. **Serve:** Serve warm or at room temperature, garnished with extra dill if desired.

Enjoy these delightful dishes featuring smoked salmon!
