Here's a simple recipe for a classic Shepherd's Pie:

**Ingredients:**

For the filling:
- 1 lb ground lamb (or ground beef for cottage pie)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup peas (fresh or frozen)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tablespoons flour (optional, for thickening)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

For the mashed potato topping:
- 2 lbs potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1/2 cup milk or cream
- Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the filling:**
   - Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and diced carrots, cooking until softened, about 5 minutes.
   - Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
   - Add the ground lamb (or beef) to the skillet, breaking it apart as it cooks until browned.
   - Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, and flour (if using). Mix well.
   - Pour in the beef broth and bring to a simmer. Let it cook for about 10 minutes, until the sauce thickens slightly.
   - Add the peas and season with salt and pepper. Simmer for another 2-3 minutes. Set aside.

2. **Prepare the mashed potatoes:**
   - Boil the potatoes in a large pot of salted water until tender, about 15-20 minutes.
   - Drain the potatoes and return them to the pot. Add the butter, milk or cream, salt, and pepper. Mash until smooth and creamy.

3. **Assemble and bake:**
   - Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
   - Spread the meat filling evenly in a baking dish.
   - Top with the mashed potatoes, spreading them out evenly over the filling. You can use a fork to create a textured pattern on the surface.
   - Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the top is golden and the filling is bubbling.

4. **Serve:**
   - Let the Shepherd's Pie cool slightly before serving. Enjoy!

This recipe is a comforting classic that you can easily adjust to your taste.
