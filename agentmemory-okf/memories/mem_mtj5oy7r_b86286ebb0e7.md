Here’s a simple recipe for **Blackened Salmon**:

### Ingredients:
- **2 salmon fillets** (6-8 ounces each, skin on or off)
- **2 tablespoons butter** (melted) or olive oil  
- **1 tablespoon smoked paprika**  
- **1 teaspoon garlic powder**  
- **1 teaspoon onion powder**  
- **1 teaspoon dried thyme**  
- **1 teaspoon dried oregano**  
- **1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper** (adjust for heat preference)  
- **1/2 teaspoon black pepper**  
- **1/2 teaspoon salt**  
- Lemon wedges (for serving)  

---

### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Spice Mix**:  
   In a small bowl, combine smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, dried thyme, dried oregano, cayenne pepper, black pepper, and salt.

2. **Coat the Salmon**:  
   - Pat the salmon fillets dry with a paper towel.  
   - Brush both sides with melted butter or olive oil.  
   - Generously coat the salmon with the spice mix, pressing it into the fish.

3. **Cook the Salmon**:  
   - Heat a cast-iron skillet (or heavy-bottomed pan) over **medium-high heat**. Add a little oil or butter to the pan.  
   - Once hot, place the salmon fillets in the pan, flesh side down. Cook for about **2-3 minutes** until the spices form a crust.  
   - Flip the salmon carefully and cook skin-side down for an additional **4-5 minutes**, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.

4. **Serve**:  
   Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the salmon and serve with your favorite sides, like rice, steamed veggies, or a fresh salad.

Enjoy! Let me know if you'd like pairing ideas or further adjustments.
