### 🤖 Assistant

Here's a simple, comforting chicken pot pie recipe that doesn't use celery.

Ingredients:
- 1 prepared pie crust for the bottom and 1 for the top (or one double-crust package)
- 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded or diced
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 cup diced carrots (fresh or frozen)
- ½ cup diced onion
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1¾ cups chicken broth
- ⅔ cup milk or half-and-half
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 egg beaten with 1 tablespoon water (optional, for brushing the crust)

Instructions:
1. Heat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a skillet over medium heat, melt the butter. Cook the onion and carrots for about 5–7 minutes until the onion is soft and the carrots begin to tenderize.
3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
4. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth, then the milk. Simmer for 3–5 minutes until thickened.
5. Stir in the thyme, garlic

powder, salt, pepper, chicken, and peas. Remove from the heat.
6. Fit one pie crust into a 9-inch pie plate. Pour in the filling.
7. Place the second crust over the top. Trim and crimp the edges, then cut a few slits in the top to vent steam. Brush with the egg wash if using.
8. Bake for 35–45 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling. If the edges brown too quickly, cover them loosely with foil.
9. Let the pie rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing so the filling can set.

If you have common pantry ingredients like potatoes, mushrooms, corn, or green beans, any of those can be added or substituted for some of the carrots and peas.
