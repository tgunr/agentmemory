### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-05-19T18:47:27Z

**Yes, you can.** In Texas, you can have a personalized disabled veterans (DV) license plate on your vehicle and also use a separate disabled parking placard (the "handicap" hangtag) for accessible parking.<grok:render card_id="b3e579" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">30</argument></grok:render>

The DV plate itself can be personalized with up to six characters (must include "DV" as a prefix or suffix), and you can apply for up to two free or low-cost placards alongside it if you qualify medically.<grok:render card_id="b1e4ec" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">30</argument></grok:render>

**One catch since 2022:** For the placard or plate to let you park in handicapped spots, it needs the International Symbol of Access (ISA, the wheelchair symbol). Regular DV plates don't have it, so you'd rely on the placard—which must have the ISA—for parking privileges.<grok:render card_id="f68656" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">31</argument></grok:render>

Grab form **VTR-615** from the TxDMV site or your county tax office; it handles both the plate and placard application. You'll need your VA disability letter and possibly a doctor's statement for the placard.

### 👤 User — 2026-05-19T18:48:33Z

No, my question was, can you get the disabled plate with the D V plate? In other words, the international symbol on a personalized D V plate?

---

### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-05-19T18:48:44Z

**No, you can't.** Texas doesn't let you personalize a DV plate that has the International Symbol of Access (the wheelchair symbol) on it.

The form is clear: "The DV ISA and DV ISA plate with military Emblem are not eligible for personalization." You can get a personalized DV plate without the ISA, or you can get a DV plate *with* the ISA, but not both together.

If you want the handicap parking privileges on the plate itself, it'll be a standard (non-personalized) DV ISA plate. Otherwise, most people just run a regular personalized DV plate and use a separate ISA placard for parking.
