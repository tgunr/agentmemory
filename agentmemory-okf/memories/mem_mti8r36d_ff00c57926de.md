### 👤 User

Which UV flexible inks can be used in the Eufy UV1 printer?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

### Compatible flexible ink for the Eufy UV1

Assuming you mean the **eufyMake E1 / UV Printer E1**, the compatible flexible ink appears to be:

- **eufyMake UV Ink Cartridge Kit (W Flexible)**
  - Also described as **Flexible White Ink** or **Soft White Ink**
  - Made specifically for the **eufyMake E1 UV Printer**
  - Intended for **flexible materials** like fabric, leather, banners, and other pliable surfaces  
  - Sources: [eufyMake product page](https://www.eufymake.com/products/eufymake-e1-flexible-white-uv-ink), [eufyMake inks page](https://www.eufymake.com/collections/uv-inks), [JPPlus listing](https://www.jpplus.com/eufymake-uv-flexible-white-ink-3pack-100ml-e1-printer-ea)

#### Key point
From the manufacturer’s ink page, the system lets you switch between:
- **normal/standard white ink** for rigid materials, and
- **flexible white ink** for flexible materials.  
See: [eufyMake inks page](https://www.eufymake.com/collections/uv-inks)

So the answer is basically:

- **Use eufyMake’s proprietary

Flexible White UV Ink**
- I do **not** see evidence that third-party flexible UV inks are approved for the printer
- The official guidance suggests using **eufyMake-branded inks matched to the E1**

If you want, I can also make you a **material-to-ink cheat sheet** for the E1, like:
- acrylic → rigid ink
- leather → flexible ink
- canvas → depends
- tumblers → rigid or flexible depending on substrate/white layer use.

### 🤖 Assistant

### Short answer

**Yes — people have tried third-party ink in the eufyMake E1, but I’m not seeing solid evidence of a reliably successful third-party *Flexible White* option.**

### What I found

- There is at least one community thread specifically asking about **refilling E1 cartridges with third-party UV ink**, which suggests people are experimenting with it: [Reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/eufyMakeOfficial/comments/1sw6zen/anyone_using_thirdparty_uv_ink_in_a_refilled/).
- A [Hackster article](https://www.hackster.io/news/how-to-use-third-party-ink-in-a-eufymake-e1-uv-printer-c55addf8bf38) reports that **iMakeStuff demonstrated a refill method** for using third-party ink in the E1, but it explicitly warns that:
  - it may **void warranty**
  - it may only work **“for now”**
  - the approach **may not last**
- I also found a community report where a user tried non-original ink and then had **white ink flow/blocking problems** afterward. In that thread, an official eufyMake account replied that

**third-party inks aren’t compatible with the E1 UV ink system** and warned about **clogs and possible printhead damage**: [White ink issue thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/eufyMakeOfficial/comments/1s8w6bc/white_ink_issue/).
- eufyMake’s own guidance says their inks are **carefully tested and matched** to the printer, and for best results they recommend buying directly from eufyMake: [eufyMake ink FAQ](https://www.eufymake.com/collections/uv-inks).

### My read on it

What this seems to mean in practice:

- **Experimenters exist**

- But there is **not yet a clear, trusted third-party Flexible White ink “winner”** with broad positive results

My answer would be: **not that I could verify from the sources I found.**
