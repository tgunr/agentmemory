## Herb-Crusted Pork Loin with Garlic and Rosemary

**Ingredients:**
- 3-4 lb pork loin roast
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped (or 1 tbsp dried)
- 2 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped (or 1 tbsp dried)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 1 cup chicken broth

**Instructions:**

1. **Prep the pork:** Pat the pork loin dry and let it come to room temperature for 30 minutes. Preheat oven to 425°F.

2. **Make the herb paste:** Mix minced garlic, rosemary, thyme, olive oil, Dijon mustard, salt, pepper, and paprika in a small bowl.

3. **Season the roast:** Rub the herb paste all over the pork loin, coating it evenly.

4. **Sear:** Heat a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the pork loin on all sides until golden brown, about 2-3 minutes per side.

5. **Add aromatics:** Remove pork temporarily, add sliced onions to the pan, then place the pork back on top. Pour chicken broth around (not over) the roast.

6. **Roast:** Transfer to oven and cook for 20-25 minutes, or until internal temperature reaches 145°F.

7. **Rest:** Let the pork rest for 10 minutes before slicing to retain juices.

**Serving:** Slice into 1/2-inch thick pieces and serve with the pan juices and caramelized onions. Pairs beautifully with roasted vegetables or mashed potatoes.

The result is a juicy, flavorful roast with a fragrant herb crust that's impressive enough for company but easy enough for a weeknight dinner.

Here are some delicious pork loin recipes to try:

**Herb-Crusted Pork Loin**
Season a 2-3 lb pork loin with salt and pepper, then coat with a mixture of minced garlic, fresh rosemary, thyme, olive oil, and breadcrumbs. Roast at 425°F for 20 minutes, then reduce to 375°F and cook until internal temperature reaches 145°F (about 20-25 more minutes). Let rest before slicing.

**Honey Garlic Glazed Pork Loin**
Sear the pork loin in a hot skillet, then transfer to the oven. Brush with a glaze made from honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, and a splash of apple cider vinegar. Roast at 375°F, basting every 15 minutes until done.

**Apple Stuffed Pork Loin**
Butterfly the pork loin and stuff with a mixture of diced apples, breadcrumbs, sage, and onions. Tie with kitchen twine and roast at 350°F. The apples add moisture and sweetness that pairs beautifully with pork.

**Asian-Style Pork Tenderloin**
Marinate pork tenderloin in a mixture of soy sauce, ginger, garlic, brown sugar, and sesame oil for at least 2 hours. Grill or roast at high heat for a caramelized exterior and juicy interior.

**Slow Cooker Pork Loin**
Place pork loin in a slow cooker with sliced onions, carrots, and potatoes. Add chicken broth, herbs, and seasonings. Cook on low for 6-8 hours for incredibly tender meat that falls apart.

The key with pork loin is not to overcook it - aim for an internal temperature of 145°F and always let it rest for 5-10 minutes before slicing to retain the juices.
