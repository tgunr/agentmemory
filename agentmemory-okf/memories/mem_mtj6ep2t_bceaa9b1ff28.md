---

### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-08-26T18:50:51Z

ERL on a Pit Boss means the grill didn't reach the minimum startup temperature, usually around one hundred thirty degrees Fahrenheit. Common causes include a dirty or loose temperature probe, a jammed auger, wet or poor-quality pellets, too much ash in the fire pot, or a faulty igniter. First, unplug it for ten to fifteen minutes to reset. Then, clean the internal temperature probe with a vinegar-water mix and check its wiring connection at the control board. Empty the hopper, clear the auger by priming, and empty the fire pot of ash and debris. Use dry, high-quality pellets and avoid over-priming. If it still fails, check if the igniter rod glows red when powered on. Pit Boss support is at eight seven seven, nine four two, two two four six, and they often send free parts under warranty.
