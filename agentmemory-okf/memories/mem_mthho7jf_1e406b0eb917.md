**Bellofram (Marsh Bellofram / Bellofram Elastomers) Class 4 rolling diaphragm, Approximate Bellofram Code (ABC) style designation 4-137-30.**<grok:render card_id="85684f" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">88</argument></grok:render>

It follows the standard Bellofram ABC part-number format for fabric-reinforced elastomeric rolling diaphragms:

- **4** = Class 4 (most common design; flat-flange style for economical hardware with flat mating surfaces between cylinder and cap/bonnet; flange acts mainly as a gasket; convolution formed by inverting the head corner radius on installation; curved-lip retainer plate recommended).<grok:render card_id="770ffc" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">76</argument></grok:render>
- **137** = Cylinder bore (DC) ≈ 1.37 inches.
- **30** = Height (H) ≈ 0.30 inches (selected to suit the design stroke; height tolerance is typically ±0.015").

### Key characteristics of Class 4 diaphragms
- Fabric-reinforced elastomer (common materials include neoprene with Dacron or similar fabric; other compounds available for temperature/chemical needs).
- Suitable for sealing high pressures because the fabric overlay contacts the clamped surfaces over a large area.
- Flanges can be perforated/trimmed for bolts or custom shapes.
- Used in pressure regulators, actuators, relays, cylinders, instruments, and similar applications needing low friction, no leakage, and long life.
- Dimensions/tolerances (general for this size range, ~1.00–2.50" DC): sidewall thickness codes (e.g., B ≈ 0.015"), flange thicknesses, corner radii, and outside flange diameter (typically DC + 1"). Exact mating-part dimensions and stroke capacity depend on the full specification (piston diameter DP, convolution width, elastomer/fabric, coatings, etc.).<grok:render card_id="eb6307" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">75</argument></grok:render>

Full catalog numbers often expand further (e.g., sidewall thickness code, elastomer/fabric codes like CBJ, and options), as in related parts such as 4-137-112CBJ. Standard size tables and the full design manual provide stroke formulas, hardware recommendations (crimp ring, swaged lip, bolted flange), and application notes.
