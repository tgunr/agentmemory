## The 3-2-1 Method (Perfect for 8 hours)

**Prep (30 minutes before smoking):**
- Remove membrane from back of ribs
- Apply dry rub generously and let sit at room temperature
- Popular rub: brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper

**Hour 0-3: Naked Smoke**
- Set smoker to 225°F
- Use fruit woods (apple, cherry) or hickory for flavor
- Place ribs bone-side down
- Spritz with apple juice/cider vinegar mix every hour after the first hour

**Hour 3-5: Wrapped Phase** 
- Wrap ribs tightly in heavy-duty foil with:
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 2 tbsp brown sugar  
  - 1 tbsp honey
  - Splash of apple juice
- This steaming phase breaks down connective tissue for tenderness

**Hour 5-8: Sauce & Finish**
- Unwrap ribs and return to smoker
- Apply BBQ sauce in thin layers every 30 minutes
- Cook until internal temp hits 203°F and bones wiggle easily

**Tenderness Tests:**
- Toothpick slides through meat like butter
- Bones pull out cleanly when twisted
- Meat cracks slightly when rack is bent

**Pro tip:** If they're getting too dark, tent with foil. The long cook time with the wrapped phase guarantees that fall-off-the-bone texture you're after.

This method consistently produces restaurant-quality ribs that practically melt in your mouth!
