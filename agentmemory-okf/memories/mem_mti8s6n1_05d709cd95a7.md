### 🤖 Assistant

The 1964 movie *Zulu* was filmed primarily in South Africa:

- The main battle scenes were shot in the **Royal Natal National Park** in the **Drakensberg Mountains**, KwaZulu‑Natal, about 90 miles southwest of the real Rorke’s Drift site, using the Drakensberg Amphitheatre as the backdrop. [Movie-Locations](https://movie-locations.com/movies/z/Zulu-1964.php), [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0058777/locations/)
- Other listed locations include the **Natal Midlands**, **Tugela River**, and wider **KwaZulu‑Natal** region. [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0058777/locations/)
- Studio work (e.g., some interiors and additional scenes) was done at **Twickenham Film Studios** in London. [The Movie Map](http://www.themoviemap.com/Zulu-1964)

So in short: mostly on location in the Drakensberg/Royal Natal National Park in South Africa, plus some studio filming at Twickenham in the UK.
