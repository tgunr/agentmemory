### 👤 User

In macOS I have a mxmouse and clicking button 6 is performing action I do not want. I am using bettertouchtool and programmed button 6 to execute cmd-option-8 to zoom but instead  it looks like it is switching applications.

---

### 🤖 Assistant

It sounds like you are experiencing a conflict between **BetterTouchTool (BTT)** and the default Logitech software (Logi Options+ or Logitech Options). On the MX Master series, "Button 6" is typically the **Gesture Button** (the thumb rest button) or the **Mode Shift** button, both of which are often hard-coded or prioritized by Logitech's own drivers to perform "App Exposure" or "Switch Applications."

When Logitech's software is running, it often intercepts the button press before BetterTouchTool can process your custom `Cmd-Option-8` shortcut. Here is how you can troubleshoot and fix this:

#### 1. Check for Logi Options+ Conflicts
If you have **Logi Options+** or **Logitech Options** installed, it is likely overriding BTT.
*   Open **Logi Options+**.
*   Select your MX mouse.
*   Locate the button you are trying to use (Button 6).
*   Change its setting to **"None"** or **"Unassigned"**. This "releases" the button so that macOS (and subsequently BetterTouchTool) can see the raw click and apply your

custom shortcut.
*   Alternatively, you can set the button to "Keyboard Shortcut" directly within Logi Options+ and record `Cmd-Option-8` there, which might be more reliable than using BTT as a middleman for this specific device.

#### 2. Verify BetterTouchTool Settings
If you prefer to keep the logic in BTT, ensure the button is being recognized correctly:
*   In BTT, go to the **"Mouse Buttons"** section.
*   Click **"Add New Button Configuration"**.
*   Click the "Click to record" area and press Button 6 on your mouse.
*   If BTT records it as something like "Mission Control" or "App Switcher" instead of a button number, it means the Logitech driver is sending a complex command rather than a simple click.
*   Ensure that "Generic Mouse Buttons" are enabled in BTT settings.

#### 3. System Permissions
Sometimes macOS updates reset the accessibility permissions required for BTT to intercept hardware events.
*   Go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility**.
*   Remove BetterTouchTool from

the list (using the minus button) and then add it back manually. This often fixes "ghost" behaviors where the OS ignores BTT's overrides.

#### 4. Disable "App Switcher" in macOS Shortcuts
If the button is triggering a native macOS feature regardless of the mouse software:
*   Go to **System Settings > Keyboard > Keyboard Shortcuts**.
*   Check under **Mission Control** or **Keyboard** to see if a shortcut is assigned to a mouse button that matches your "switching applications" behavior.

If you find that Logi Options+ is still being stubborn, many users find that completely quitting the Logitech background processes allows BetterTouchTool to have full control over every button on the MX Master. For more advanced troubleshooting, you can refer to the [Logitech Support](https://support.logi.com) or the [BetterTouchTool Community](https://community.folivora.ai/).
