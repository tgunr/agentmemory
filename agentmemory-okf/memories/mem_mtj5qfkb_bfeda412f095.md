### 🤖 Assistant

Sous vide is an excellent way to cook frozen salmon, as it ensures even cooking while maintaining the fish's moisture and flavor. Here's a step-by-step guide:

### What You Need:
- Frozen salmon fillets (vacuum-sealed or sealed in a freezer-safe bag)
- Sous vide setup (immersion circulator, pot, etc.)
- Seasoning (salt, pepper, herbs, olive oil, or butter)

---

### Instructions:
1. **Set Up the Sous Vide:**
   - Fill your sous vide container with water and set the temperature based on your preferred doneness:
     - **Medium-rare:** 120°F (49°C) for tender, slightly translucent salmon.
     - **Medium:** 130°F (54°C) for firmer, fully opaque salmon.
     - **Well-done:** 140°F (60°C) for a firmer, flaky texture.

2. **Prepare the Salmon:**
   - If the salmon isn’t already vacuum-sealed, place it in a freezer-safe, resealable bag. Use the water displacement method to remove air when sealing.

3. **Cooking Time:**
   - Add 30-45 minutes to the typical sous vide cooking time for salmon to account for starting from frozen.
   - Cook for **1 to 1.5 hours**.

4. **Optional Finishing (Searing):**
   - After cooking, remove the salmon from the bag and pat it dry.
   - Heat a skillet with oil or butter until very hot, and quickly sear the salmon for 30 seconds to 1 minute per side for a golden crust.

5. **Serve:**
   - Serve immediately with your favorite sides or sauces, such as lemon butter, dill, or a simple herb garnish.

---

### Tips:
- Frozen salmon can be seasoned lightly before freezing, or you can add fresh seasoning right before sealing the bag.
- If you're cooking salmon with skin, searing after sous vide can help crisp it up.

Enjoy your perfectly cooked salmon!
