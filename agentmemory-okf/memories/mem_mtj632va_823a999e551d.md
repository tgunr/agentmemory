## What You Need to Know:

## How to Control Monitor Programmatically:

**For Linux:**
- **ddcutil** - The most comprehensive tool for monitor control via DDC/CI
- **ddccontrol** - Alternative tool (though less maintained)
- Commands like: `ddcutil setvcp 10 50` (sets brightness to 50%)

**For Windows:**
- **TwinkleTray** - Popular brightness control utility
- **ControlMyMonitor** (NirSoft) - Command-line monitor control
- **Display Control** utilities

**For macOS:**
