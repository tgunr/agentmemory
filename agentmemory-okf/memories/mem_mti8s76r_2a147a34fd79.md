### 🤖 Assistant

Working with Creatorally colored plywood is a great way to achieve high-contrast designs without the mess of post-process painting. Since you have 24W, 44W, and 64W lasers, you have significant power at your disposal, which means you will likely need to focus on high speeds to avoid over-burning the delicate paint layers.

### General Guidelines for Creatorally Colored Plywood

Creatorally offers two main types of colored plywood: **Standard Pre-Painted** (double-sided single color) and **3-Layer Multi-Color** (Black top, Blue middle, White bottom, Wood core).

#### 1. Standard Pre-Painted Plywood
This material is designed to reveal the natural wood grain underneath the paint.
*   **Engraving Goal:** Remove only the paint layer to expose the wood.
*   **Text:** Use high speed and moderate power. Because text requires sharp edges, a higher DPI (300-400) is recommended.
*   **Images:** For photos, use a "Dithered" mode in your software (like LightBurn) to prevent the wood from charring too deeply in dark

areas.

#### 2. 3-Layer Multi-Color Plywood
This is a "color sandwich" where different depths reveal different colors: [Creatorally Multi-Color Guide](https://creatorally.com/blogs/news/multi-color-laser-engraving-made-easy-meet-creatorally-s-3-layer-colored-plywood).
*   **Blue Layer:** Light engraving (High speed, Low power).
*   **White Layer:** Medium engraving (Medium speed/power).
*   **Natural Wood:** Deepest engraving (Slow speed, Higher power).

### Estimated Settings by Laser Power

Since your lasers are quite powerful (especially the 44W and 64W), you should start with a **Power Scale** or **Material Test** to find the sweet spot. Below are starting estimates for 3mm (1/8") Creatorally plywood:

| Laser Power | Task | Speed (mm/min) | Power (%) | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **24W** | Engraving (Reveal Wood) | 6000 - 8000 | 20% - 30% | Good for crisp text. |
| | Cutting (3mm) | 600 - 800 | 80% - 100% | Use Air Assist for clean edges. |
| **44W** | Engraving (Reveal Wood) | 10000

- 12000 | 15% - 25% | Very fast; watch for "ghosting" at high speeds. |
| | Cutting (3mm) | 1200 - 1500 | 60% - 80% | Single pass is usually sufficient. |
| **64W** | Engraving (Reveal Wood) | 15000+ | 10% - 20% | May need to lower power significantly to avoid charring. |
| | Cutting (3mm) | 2000 - 2500 | 50% - 70% | Extremely fast; ensure your belts are tight. |

### Best Practices for Images and Text
*   **Air Assist:** Always use air assist when cutting to prevent smoke stains on the pre-painted surface. For engraving, a lower air flow can help prevent "frosting" the paint while still clearing debris.
*   **Masking:** Creatorally boards are semi-gloss or matte and generally don't require masking, but if you notice smoke residue, a light application of blue painter's tape can help.
*   **Focus:** Ensure your laser is perfectly focused. A slight defocus (moving the laser 1-2mm further away) can sometimes result in a smoother "fill" for large engraved areas on colored wood.
*   **Cleaning:** After engraving,

use a soft, damp cloth or a "Magic Eraser" to gently wipe away any soot from the revealed wood or paint surface.

For more specific project ideas and community tips, you can visit the [Creatorally Blog](https://creatorally.com/blogs/news).
