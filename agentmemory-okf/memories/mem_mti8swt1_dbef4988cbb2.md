### 1. What “pig-butchering” is (in plain language)

“Pig-butchering” (also called Sha Zhu Pan) is a long-con, mostly online scam:
- The “pig” = the victim, “fattened up” emotionally and financially.
- The scammer spends weeks or months:
  - Building trust/romance/friendship.
  - Then introducing “great investment opportunities” (often crypto/FX).
  - Getting you to deposit small amounts first, then larger and larger.
  - Letting you “see” fake profits in a bogus app or website.
  - Finally, they “butcher” = lock your account / disappear with everything.

This is different from quick phishing; it’s slow grooming plus financial fraud.

---

### 2. How AI is making pig-butchering scams more dangerous

Scammers are now using AI to:

#### a. Generate realistic chat scripts
- Use LLMs to:
  - Craft “perfect” opening messages: “Hi, is this Anna? Oh sorry, wrong

number 😊”
  - Sustain long conversations with:
    - Flirty / romantic banter
    - Supportive “therapy-talk” style messages
    - Tailored responses to your hobbies, job, and fears
- Result: Conversations feel attentive, consistent, and emotionally tuned to you.

**Red flags:**
- Replies that are unusually fast, long, and emotionally smooth.
- They rarely ignore your questions, they always “say the right thing.”
- Language feels polished, even if they claim poor education or rural background.

#### b. Maintain multiple identities at scale
- A single scammer or group can:
  - Run many simultaneous chats (WhatsApp/Telegram/Line/WeChat/Instagram).
  - Use AI to maintain “character notes” about each victim (your pets, ex, job).
  - Keep tone consistent over weeks or months.
- This makes it feel like you’re uniquely special; in reality, it’s mass-produced.

#### c. Deepfake photos, profiles, and even voice
- AI tools to:
  - Generate attractive profile photos.
  - Slightly alter stolen photos to evade

reverse-image search.
  - Clone voices from short audio clips and do voice calls.
- Some scams now layer:
  - Text (AI-written),
  - Voice notes (AI-cloned),
  - Occasional video calls (sometimes brief, blurry, or pre-recorded).

**Red flags:**
- They avoid long, natural video calls.
- Backgrounds are always generic (hotel lobby, car, plain wall).
- Lighting is weird or face movement seems slightly off/out of sync.

#### d. Professional “investment scripts”
- Organized crime groups use AI to:
  - Develop and iterate highly optimized scripts:
    - Day 1–3: Light chat, “wrong number” acquaintance.
    - Week 1–2: Establish shared interests, talk about “hard work,” “discipline.”
    - Week 3+: Introduce “how I achieved financial freedom” / “uncle’s strategy.”
  - Localize the pitch (US stocks, HK stocks, Japanese FX, EU crypto, etc.).
  - Generate FAQ-like answers to every skeptical question:
    - “Is this legal?”  
    - “Why haven’t I heard of this?”  
    - “Why can’t I do it on Binance/Coinbase?”
