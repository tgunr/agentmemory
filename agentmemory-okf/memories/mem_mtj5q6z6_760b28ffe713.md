### 🤖 Assistant

Sure, here's a step-by-step guide on how to sous vide a steak:

### Equipment:
- Sous vide immersion circulator
- Vacuum sealer or zip-lock bags
- Large pot or container
- Tongs
- Cast iron skillet (optional, for searing)
- Thermometer (optional, for checking doneness)

### Ingredients:
- Steak (ribeye, filet mignon, strip steak, etc.)
- Salt and pepper (to taste)
- Optional seasonings: garlic, thyme, rosemary, or any other herbs and spices you prefer
- Olive oil or butter (for searing)

### Instructions:

1. **Preheat Water Bath:**
   - Fill a large pot or container with water.
   - Attach your sous vide immersion circulator to the container and set it to the desired temperature:
     - Rare: 120°F (49°C)
     - Medium-Rare: 129°F (54°C)
     - Medium: 135°F (57°C)
     - Medium-Well: 145°F (63°C)
     - Well-Done: 156°F (69°C)

2. **Season the Steak:**
   - Season your steak generously with salt and pepper on both sides. Add any optional seasonings or herbs.

3. **Bag the Steak:**
   - Place the steak in a vacuum-seal bag or a zip-lock bag. If using a zip-lock bag, use the water displacement method to remove the air: slowly lower the bag into the water, letting the pressure of the water push the air out, then seal the bag just above the water line.

4. **Cook the Steak:**
   - Submerge the bagged steak in the preheated water bath.
   - Cook the steak for at least 1 hour, but you can leave it in for up to 4 hours for a more tender result.

5. **Finish the Steak:**
   - Once the steak is done cooking, remove it from the bag and pat it dry with paper towels.
   - If you prefer a seared finish, heat a cast iron skillet over high heat until it's smoking hot.
   - Add a small amount of oil or butter to the skillet.
   - Sear the steak for 1-2 minutes on each side, just until a brown crust forms.
   - You can also sear the edges of the steak for a few seconds to get an even crust all around.

6. **Rest and Serve:**
   - Let the steak rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
   - Slice the steak against the grain for the most tender bites.

Enjoy your perfectly cooked sous vide steak!
