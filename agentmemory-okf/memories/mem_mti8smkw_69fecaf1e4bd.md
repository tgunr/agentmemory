### 👤 User

Dumped a tank containing water and urine onto sand how do I get rid of the smeel

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To get rid of the smell of urine and water dumped onto sand, you need to neutralize the uric acid crystals that cause the lingering odor. Sand is porous, so the liquid likely traveled deep, meaning surface treatments might not be enough.

The most effective way to handle this is to use an enzymatic cleaner specifically designed for pet or human waste. These cleaners contain bacteria that "eat" the organic compounds causing the smell. You should saturate the affected area of the sand with the cleaner, ensuring it reaches the same depth as the original spill, and let it air dry completely.

If you prefer a DIY approach, you can try the following steps:

*   Generously sprinkle baking soda over the damp sand to help absorb the odor and moisture.
*   Mix a solution of one part white vinegar to one part water and spray it heavily over the area. The acid in the vinegar helps break down the urea.
*   If the spill is in a contained area like a sandbox, the best solution may be to physically remove the top few inches

of contaminated sand and replace it with fresh, clean sand.
*   Expose the area to as much direct sunlight as possible, as UV rays can help kill bacteria and dissipate odors.

If the smell persists after these treatments, it usually indicates that the liquid has soaked deeper than the treatment reached, and excavation of the sand is likely necessary.
