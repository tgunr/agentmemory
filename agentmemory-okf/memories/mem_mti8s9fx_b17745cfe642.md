### 🤖 Assistant

That is a fantastic set of ingredients to work with. Since you have a mix of hearty vegetables and two types of seafood, you can go in a few different directions, from a light stir-fry to a comforting seafood stew.

Here are three recipe ideas based on what you have:

### 1. Pan-Seared Mahi Mahi with Braised Leeks and Carrots
This is a more refined, "bistro-style" dish where the sweetness of the leeks and carrots complements the mild mahi mahi.

*   **The Base:** Thinly slice the leeks (white and light green parts only) and julienne the carrots. Sauté them in butter or oil with a little celery until soft and slightly caramelized.
*   **The Fish:** Season the mahi mahi fillets with salt, pepper, and perhaps some garlic powder or paprika. Pan-sear them in a hot skillet for about 3-4 minutes per side until golden and opaque.
*   **The Finish:** Serve the fish directly on top of the bed of leeks and carrots. If you have lemon or white wine, a quick splash in the pan at the end makes a great pan sauce.

### 2.

Seafood & Bok Choy Stir-Fry
This is a fast, high-heat method that uses almost all your ingredients and keeps the vegetables crisp.

*   **The Prep:** Slice the carrots and celery thinly on a diagonal. Chop the bok choy, keeping the crunchy white stems separate from the green leaves. Cut the mahi mahi into bite-sized chunks.
*   **The Cook:** Sear the shrimp and mahi mahi chunks first in a very hot wok or skillet with oil, then remove them so they don't overcook. 
*   **The Veggies:** Toss in the carrots, celery, and bok choy stems. After 2 minutes, add the leeks and bok choy leaves. 
*   **The Spices:** Use ginger, garlic, or red pepper flakes if you have them. Toss the seafood back in with a splash of soy sauce or just salt and pepper to finish.

### 3. Aromatic Seafood "Chowder" (Dairy-Free)
If you want something cozy, you can use the celery, leeks, and carrots as a classic flavor base (mirepoix) for a soup.

*   **The Broth:** Sauté diced leeks, celery, and carrots until soft. Add water or broth and let

it simmer until the carrots are tender.
*   **The Seafood:** Add the mahi mahi (cut into chunks) and the shrimp during the last 5 minutes of cooking so they poach gently in the liquid.
*   **The Greens:** Stir in chopped bok choy at the very end; the residual heat will wilt the leaves perfectly while keeping the stems slightly crunchy.
*   **The Spices:** Thyme, bay leaves, or even a bit of curry powder would work beautifully here depending on what spices you have on hand.
