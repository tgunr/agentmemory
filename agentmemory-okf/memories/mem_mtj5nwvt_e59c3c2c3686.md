The primary difference between a NEMA L5 and an L6 receptacle is the voltage and wiring configuration they support. 

- **NEMA L5 receptacles** are used for **120V** circuits, with two hot conductors (one hot and one neutral) and a ground. They're commonly found in standard single-phase power applications in homes or small businesses.
  
- **NEMA L6 receptacles**, on the other hand, are used for **240V** circuits and have two hot wires and a ground, but no neutral. They're typically found in industrial or commercial settings where higher voltage is required, like for powering larger equipment.

Both have a locking mechanism (the "L" in L5 and L6), ensuring that plugs don't disconnect easily once inserted.
