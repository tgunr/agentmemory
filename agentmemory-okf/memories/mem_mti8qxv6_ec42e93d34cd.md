Decal pricing Strategy for 3 1/2, 4, 6, 8, 10, and 12" decals with $.43/ml of ink on UV DTF film sheets at $100 for 25 sheets.

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To develop a profitable pricing strategy for your UV DTF decals, we first established your base costs. Your film cost is $4.00 per sheet ($100 for 25 sheets), and your ink cost is approximately $0.43 per ml. Based on standard UV DTF ink consumption (roughly 18ml per square meter), the ink cost per square inch is negligible compared to the film, but it scales with size.

The following strategy assumes a standard A3 sheet size (11.7" x 16.5") and accounts for "nesting" (how many decals fit on one sheet).

### Strategic Recommendations

#### 1. The "Dead Space" Premium
Notice that 6" and 8" decals both fit 2 per sheet. Because they consume the same amount of film real estate, their base costs are very similar despite the size difference. You should price the 8" significantly higher than the 6" to account for the perceived value, even though your overhead is nearly identical.

#### 2. Bundling and Gang Sheets
Since your biggest cost is the $4.00 film sheet, encourage customers to buy "Gang Sheets" rather than individual decals. 
*   **Individual Price:** $2.00 for a 3.5" decal.
*   **Gang Sheet Price:** $15.00 - $20.00 for a full sheet.
This guarantees you cover the $4.00 film cost and a high labor margin in one transaction, rather than selling single small decals which have high administrative overhead.

#### 3. Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)
For the smaller sizes (3.5" and 4"), consider a minimum order of 6 or 12 units. Selling a single 3.5" decal for $2.00 is often not worth the labor of

weeding, packing, and shipping. Setting an MOQ ensures that every "run" on your UV printer is profitable.

#### 4. Labor and Overhead
The "Total Base Cost" above only includes raw materials. It does not include electricity, machine depreciation, or your time. A 5x markup for retail is standard in the custom printing industry to ensure these "hidden" costs are covered while still remaining competitive in the market.

### 🤖 Assistant

For custom text entry, you are moving from a "commodity" product (pre-designed) to a "service" product. This requires additional time for layout, font selection, and proofing. 

A standard industry practice is to apply a **20% to 50% surcharge** on top of your base retail price, or a flat **"Setup Fee"** per design.

### Custom Text Pricing Models

#### 1. The Flat Setup Fee (Recommended for Bulk)
Instead of marking up every single decal, charge a one-time fee for the design work. This is best if a customer wants 50 decals with the same custom text.
*   **Setup Fee:** $5.00 – $15.00 per unique text design.
*   **Benefit:** Covers your "desk time" (opening the software, typing, centering) regardless of how many decals they buy.
