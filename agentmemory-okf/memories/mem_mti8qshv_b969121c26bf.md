### 👤 User

An intermittent fasting. I am drinking lots of soda water. What kind of flavoring can I add that do not break my fast

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Short answer: stick to **zero-calorie, zero-sweetener, non-insulin-triggering flavors**.

Here’s what generally *won’t* break a fast (for fat loss/metabolic fasting):

### ✅ Safe additions
- **Fresh citrus**: lemon, lime, a little orange zest (not juice-heavy)
- **Cucumber slices**
- **Fresh herbs**: mint, basil, rosemary
- **Apple cider vinegar** (1–2 tsp; strong but effective)
- **Plain bitters** (a few drops — check no added sugar)
- **Electrolytes** (unsweetened, no sugar, no dextrose)
- **Infused water** (berries, ginger slices — minimal actual sugar if not eaten)

### ⚠️ Gray area (depends on your fasting goals)
- **“Natural flavors” drops** (like Mio, etc.)
- **Artificial sweeteners** (sucralose, aspartame, stevia, monk fruit)

These are technically zero-calorie, but:
- Can trigger insulin in some people
- May increase hunger/cravings
- Might blunt some benefits of fasting (especially autophagy)

### ❌ Avoid if you want a “clean” fast
- Juice (even a splash adds calories)
- Honey, sugar, syrups
-

Anything with calories/protein/BCAAs

---

### Simple combos that work well
- Lemon + mint
- Lime + cucumber
- Apple cider vinegar + cinnamon stick
- Ginger slices + lemon peel

---

If your goal is **pure fat loss**, small amounts of artificial sweetener probably won’t ruin everything.

If your goal is **autophagy / strict fasting**, keep it to **water, minerals, and natural non-caloric infusions**.
