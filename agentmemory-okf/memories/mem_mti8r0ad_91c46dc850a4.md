### 🤖 Assistant

To factory reset your **HP laptop (model 17Z-CA100)** to its original default settings, you can use several methods depending on whether you can boot into Windows or not. 

Below are the most effective step-by-step methods to reset your notebook.

---

### Method 1: The Built-In HP Recovery Environment (F11)
*This is the best method to use if your laptop won't boot into Windows, or if you want a deep, clean factory reset directly from HP's built-in system recovery partition.*

1. **Power off** your HP laptop completely (hold down the power button for about 5–10 seconds).
2. Ensure the laptop is plugged into its **AC power adapter/wall outlet** so it does not lose power during the process.
3. Turn on the laptop, and immediately begin **tapping the `F11` key** repeatedly (about once every second) until you see the "Choose an Option" screen.
   * *Note: If `F11` doesn't work, turn off the computer, press power, and immediately tap the `Esc` key repeatedly until the Startup Menu appears. From there, press `F11`

for System Recovery.*
4. From the menu, navigate to: **Troubleshoot** > **Reset this PC**.
5. Choose one of the following:
   * **Keep my files** (reinstalls Windows but preserves your documents and pictures).
   * **Remove everything** (completely wipes the hard drive of all personal files, apps, and settings—recommended if you are selling, donating, or experiencing highly stubborn malware/errors).
6. Choose **Local reinstall** (takes the pre-installed recovery files directly from your HP partition).
7. Review the security warnings and click **Reset**. The laptop will automatically reboot and start the process. This can take anywhere from 30 minutes to an hour of unattended run-time.

---

### Method 2: Resetting via Windows Settings
*Use this method if you can easily sign into your Windows desktop.*

#### **For Windows 11:**
1. Click the **Start Menu** and open **Settings** (or press the `Windows Key + I` shortcut).
2. Go to **System** > **Recovery** on the left menu.
3. Find **Reset this PC** on the right

side and click **Reset PC**.
4. Choose either **Keep my files** or **Remove everything** based on your preferences.
5. Select **Cloud download** (to download a fresh version of Windows from Microsoft) or **Local reinstall** (uses existing system files on your HP partition).
6. Click **Next** and then **Reset**.

#### **For Windows 10:**
1. Click the **Start Menu** and choose **Settings** (the gear icon).
2. Click **Update & Security** > **Recovery** on the sidebar.
3. Under the **Reset this PC** header, click **Get started**.
4. Select **Keep my files** or **Remove everything**.
5. Select **Cloud download** or **Local reinstall**.
6. Review the settings and click **Reset**.

---

### Method 3: Resetting from the Windows Sign-In Screen
*Use this method if your computer boots up to the login screen, but you have forgotten your password or cannot log in.*

1. On the bottom-right corner of the Windows sign-in screen, click the **Power** icon.
2. Hold down the **`Shift` key** on your keyboard, and click

**Restart** with your mouse while continuing to hold the Shift key.
3. Your computer will reboot into a blue screen showing the Advanced Recovery options.
4. Go to **Troubleshoot** > **Reset this PC**.
5. Choose **Keep my files** or **Remove everything** and follow the remaining prompts to start the reset.

---
