### 🤖 Assistant

There are several methods to remove algae from a koi pond with running water:

1. **Manual Removal**: Using a pond net or algae brush, you can manually remove algae that float on the surface or cling to the pond walls. It's a simple but effective short-term solution.

2. **Install a UV Clarifier**: A UV clarifier kills algae by exposing the water to ultraviolet light as it passes through, preventing algae growth without harming the koi.

3. **Add Beneficial Bacteria**: Introducing beneficial bacteria can help break down organic matter and reduce nutrients in the water that feed algae, promoting a healthier pond ecosystem.

4. **Use Barley Straw Extract**: Barley straw or its extract releases chemicals as it decomposes, which can help inhibit algae growth. It’s a natural solution that’s safe for koi.

5. **Reduce Sunlight**: Algae thrive on sunlight. Adding pond plants like water lilies or using a pond cover to create shade can help reduce algae growth.

6. **Adjust Feeding Habits**: Overfeeding your koi can lead to excess nutrients in the water, which encourages algae growth. Reducing feeding can help control this.

7. **Use an Algaecide**: For a more aggressive approach, you can use an algaecide designed for ponds. Be cautious with this method to ensure it's safe for koi and other pond life.

8. **Add Pond Plants**: Aquatic plants can compete with algae for nutrients, helping to starve the algae and naturally control its growth.

These methods can help keep your pond clearer and healthier, providing a better environment for your koi.
