### 🤖 Assistant

Communicating about sexual intimacy within a relationship can be delicate, especially when responding to a message that expresses a strong moral stance on fidelity. To clarify that your concern is about the quality or frequency of your connection rather than a lack of trust, you can acknowledge her commitment first and then pivot to the emotional and physical connection you feel is missing.

#### Direct and Honest
"Hi Jessica, I really appreciate you being so open and sharing that with me. I want you to know that I’ve never doubted your loyalty or your respect for our marriage. My struggle hasn't been about whether you're being faithful—I know you are. It’s more about the intimacy between us. I feel like we’ve lost that close physical and emotional connection, and I want us to find a way to get that back because I value our bond so much."

#### Soft and Reassuring
"Thank you for reaching out and for your honesty, Jessica. I hear how much you value the sanctity of our marriage, and I value it too. I want to

clear something up: I don't have any doubts about your fidelity. When I bring up our sex life, it’s not because I think you’re sleeping with anyone else. It’s because I miss the closeness and the intimacy that I feel only with you, and I want to work on making that part of our relationship stronger."

#### Short and Clear
"I appreciate you clearing the air, but I want to be clear as well: I have never doubted your loyalty or that you take our marriage seriously. My concerns have always been about the intimacy just between the two of us. It’s not about 'sleeping around' or a lack of trust; it’s about wanting to feel more connected to you in our private life."

#### Key points to remember when sending this:
*   **Validate her values:** By acknowledging her commitment to God and marriage, you lower her defenses.
*   **Use "I" statements:** Focus on your feelings of connection ("I miss our closeness") rather than what she isn't doing.
*   **Separate the issues:** Explicitly state that "fidelity" (loyalty) and

"intimacy" (connection) are two different things in your mind.
