In StoreKit, what's the difference between consumable and non-consumable?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

In StoreKit, which is used for in-app purchases in iOS apps, the main difference between consumable and non-consumable products lies in their usage and availability after purchase:

1. **Consumable** products are those that can be purchased multiple times and are used up. They are typically used for things like in-game currency, extra lives, or temporary boosts. Once a consumable item is used, it is gone and can be purchased again.

2. **Non-consumable** products are purchased once and then they are available for the user indefinitely. Examples include unlocking a full game, removing ads permanently, or accessing premium content. These purchases are typically restored on all of the user’s devices linked to their Apple ID.

So, if you're developing an app where you need items that can be bought and used repeatedly, you'll use consumable products. If you want to sell items that permanently enhance the user's experience, you'll choose non-consumable products.

### 🤖 Assistant

In StoreKit, renewable and non-renewable subscriptions refer to different types of recurring payment models for in-app purchases:

1. **Renewable subscriptions** automatically renew at the end of their subscription period until the user chooses to cancel. These are commonly used for services like streaming platforms or online magazines where content is continually updated. Examples include weekly, monthly, or yearly subscriptions.

2. **Non-renewable subscriptions** do not automatically renew and must be purchased again by the user if they want to extend access. These are typically used for short-term access to content or services, like a one-month pass to a set of features or content that does not need to be updated frequently.

Renewable subscriptions help provide a steady revenue stream and are encouraged for ongoing services, while non-renewable subscriptions might be suitable for limited or one-time use cases.
