### 👤 User

How to make whitewash for a fence

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Making whitewash for a fence is a traditional, eco-friendly, and cost-effective way to protect wood while giving it a classic "country" aesthetic. Unlike paint, whitewash is breathable and naturally prevents rot and insect damage.

### Ingredients and Supplies
The most common traditional recipe uses a simple ratio of hydrated lime, salt, and water:

*   **Hydrated Lime (Type S):** 50 lbs (available at most hardware or masonry stores).
*   **Table Salt:** 8 to 12 lbs (helps the mixture bond to the wood).
*   **Water:** Approximately 8 to 10 gallons.
*   **Optional:** 1 lb of Alum (potassium aluminum sulfate) can be added to help the whitewash resist rubbing off on clothing.

### Mixing Instructions
1.  **Safety First:** Always wear a mask, safety goggles, and gloves. Lime is alkaline and can irritate your skin, eyes, and lungs.
2.  **Dissolve the Salt:** In a large bucket or barrel, dissolve the salt in about 2 gallons of warm water.
3.  **Add the Lime:** Slowly add the 50 lb bag of hydrated lime to the salt

water.
4.  **Thin the Mixture:** Gradually add the remaining water while stirring constantly until you reach the consistency of thin cream or whole milk. 
5.  **Let it Sit:** Allow the mixture to cure for at least a few hours (or overnight) before application. This ensures the lime is fully hydrated.

### How to Apply
*   **Prep the Fence:** The wood must be clean and unpainted. If the fence is very dry, lightly dampen the wood with a garden hose before applying the whitewash; this prevents the wood from sucking the moisture out of the mix too quickly, which can cause flaking.
*   **Use the Right Brush:** Use a large, stiff-bristled masonry or "whitewash" brush. Don't use a standard fine-bristle paintbrush.
*   **Apply Thin Coats:** Whitewash looks transparent when wet. Do not keep layering it on while it’s wet, or it will crack and peel when it dries. Apply one thin coat, let it dry completely (it will turn bright white as it dries), and then decide if you want a second coat.
*   **Maintain:** Whitewash is

not permanent like modern latex paint. It will slowly wear away over 2–5 years, adding to the "weathered" look. You can simply brush on a fresh coat whenever it begins to look thin.
