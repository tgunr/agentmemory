# Updating Snapmaker 2 Firmware Over Wi-Fi: Step-by-Step Guide

Here's how to update your Snapmaker 2 firmware using the Wi-Fi connection:

## Prerequisites
- Your Snapmaker 2 is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your computer
- You have Snapmaker Luban software installed on your computer
- Your machine is powered on

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Start your Snapmaker 2**
   - Power on the machine and wait for it to completely boot up

2. **Launch Snapmaker Luban software** on your computer

3. **Connect to your Snapmaker**
   - Click on the "Connection" button in the top-right corner
   - Select "Connect to Wi-Fi"
   - Choose your Snapmaker from the list of available devices
   - Wait for the connection to be established

4. **Check for firmware updates**
   - Go to "Settings" in Luban (gear icon)
   - Select the "Machine" tab
   - Click on "Firmware" or "Update Firmware"
   - The software will automatically check for new firmware versions

5. **Download new firmware**
   - If an update is available, Luban will display the new version
   - Click "Download" to get the firmware package

6. **Install the firmware update**
   - After the download completes, click "Update" or "Install"
   - Confirm the update when prompted

7. **Wait for the update to complete**
   - Do not power off your Snapmaker during this process
   - The touchscreen will display the update progress
   - The machine will restart automatically when the update is finished

8. **Verify the update**
   - Once the machine restarts, check the firmware version on the touchscreen
   - Go to Settings > About to confirm the new version is installed

If you encounter any issues during the update process, make sure your Wi-Fi connection is stable and try again. For persistent problems, you can also update using the USB method with an SD card.
