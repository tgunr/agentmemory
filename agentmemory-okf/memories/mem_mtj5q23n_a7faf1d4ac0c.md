You could make a delicious ham and egg scramble. Here's a simple recipe:

1. Chop the ham, onions, and tomatoes into small pieces.
2. In a non-stick skillet, sauté the onions until they're translucent.
3. Add the chopped ham and continue to sauté until it's heated through.
4. Beat the eggs in a bowl and pour them over the ham and onions. Let it sit for a moment without stirring so it starts to set.
5. When the eggs start to firm up, add the tomatoes and begin to gently stir the mixture until the eggs are cooked to your liking.
6. Season with salt and pepper, and enjoy!

This could be served with a side of toast or wrapped in a tortilla for a breakfast burrito.
