At what temperature should you ferment a hefeweizen at?

---

### 🤖 Assistant — 2025-03-15T17:06:10Z

For fermenting a hefeweizen, you’ll want to keep the temperature between 62 and 68 degrees Fahrenheit. That range helps bring out those classic banana and clove flavors from the yeast. Too warm, and it might get overly fruity or spicy; too cool, and the yeast might not do its thing properly. Stick around 65 if you’re aiming for a nice balance!
