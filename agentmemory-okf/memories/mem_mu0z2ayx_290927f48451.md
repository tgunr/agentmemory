In the **Ring app**:

1. Tap the **Device Settings** (the ⚙️) on your Stick Up Cam.
2. Go to **Device Settings** → **General Settings** → **Status LED**.
3. Toggle it **Off**.

That’s the supported method. If your firmware or model doesn’t expose the toggle there, the fallback is a small piece of opaque tape over the LED—but that’s obviously not waterproof and can trap moisture, so app-based is the better fix.
