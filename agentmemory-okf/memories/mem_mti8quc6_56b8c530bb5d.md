### Alternatives to Etsy for selling online (by category)

#### Handmade + vintage marketplaces
- **eBay** — huge buyer base; good for vintage, collectibles, refurbished items, and one-offs.
- **Amazon Handmade** — handmade-focused, access to Amazon traffic (more requirements/competition).
- **Ruby Lane** — stronger for **vintage/antiques** and higher-priced items (more curated).
- **Bonanza** — marketplace with lighter branding constraints than some larger platforms.
- **Chairish** — great for **vintage furniture/home décor** (more selective; higher AOV).
- **Depop** / **Poshmark** — best for **vintage clothing/accessories** and trend-driven resale.

#### Design + print-on-demand (if you sell art, merch, stationery)
- **Redbubble** / **Society6** — upload designs; they handle production/fulfillment (lower control, smaller margins).
- **TeePublic** / **Zazzle** — similar POD model; good for specific product types.
- **Creative Market** — good if you sell **digital design assets** (fonts, templates,

graphics).

#### Your own online store (more control, more marketing responsibility)
- **Shopify** — full-featured storefront, lots of integrations, scalable.
- **Squarespace Commerce** — easiest “site + store” bundle, strong design templates.
- **Wix eCommerce** — flexible site builder with store features.
- **WooCommerce (WordPress)** — most customizable if you’re comfortable managing a site.
- **Big Cartel** — simple, artist-friendly, lighter-weight than Shopify.

#### Social-first selling (good if you can create content)
- **TikTok Shop** — strong discovery; can be great for impulse-friendly products.
- **Instagram Shopping / Facebook Shops** — works well if you already have an audience.
- **Pinterest** — not a checkout platform by itself, but excellent for product discovery (especially decor, weddings, DIY).

#### Digital products + downloads (patterns, planners, presets, files)
- **Gumroad** — simple digital downloads + subscriptions.
- **Payhip** — digital products with VAT handling options and

storefront features.
- **Ko-fi** — good for creators selling downloads/commissions and building memberships.

### Quick way to pick (tell me which fits you best)
1) What are you selling (handmade physical, vintage, digital downloads, POD designs)?  
2) Do you want a marketplace (built-in traffic) or your own store (more control)?  
3) What price range and shipping complexity (small/light vs large/fragile)?

Answer those and I’ll narrow it to the best 3–5 options for your situation, including typical fee tradeoffs and what tends to perform well on each.
