To export your data from Claude.ai:

1. Click your initials/profile icon in the bottom-left corner
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Open the **Privacy** section
4. Click **Export data** and confirm

Anthropic will then email you a download link (can take a few minutes to a few hours) with a ZIP file containing your conversation history in JSON format, plus other account data.

A few notes:
- This works on the web app or Claude Desktop — it's not currently possible from the iOS or Android app.
- If you're on a **Team or Enterprise** plan, individual members can't export their own data this way — only the workspace's Primary Owner can run an org-wide export from Organization Settings → Data and Privacy.

Since this is a product detail that could change, it's worth double-checking in your own Settings menu in case the exact wording or location has shifted.
