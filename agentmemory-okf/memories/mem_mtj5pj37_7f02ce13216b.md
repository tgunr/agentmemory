### 🤖 Assistant

**Chicken Caldo (Caldo de Pollo)** is a comforting, hearty Mexican chicken soup made with vegetables, spices, and tender chicken. Here's a simple recipe:

---

### Ingredients:
- **2 lbs chicken pieces** (bone-in for flavor; thighs, drumsticks, or a whole chicken)
- **8 cups chicken broth** (or water with bouillon cubes)
- **3-4 garlic cloves**, minced
- **1 large onion**, quartered
- **3-4 carrots**, peeled and sliced
- **3-4 potatoes**, peeled and quartered
- **2 zucchini**, sliced
- **1-2 ears of corn**, cut into 2-inch pieces
- **1/2 head cabbage**, cut into large wedges
- **2-3 Roma tomatoes**, diced
- **1 bunch fresh cilantro**
- **1 lime**, cut into wedges
- **Salt and pepper**, to taste
- **1-2 tsp ground cumin** (optional)
- **Cooked rice** (optional, for serving)

---

### Instructions:

1. **Prepare the Chicken**  
   In a large pot, place the chicken pieces and cover them with the chicken broth. Add garlic, onion, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 20-25 minutes, skimming off any foam.

2. **Add the Vegetables**  
   Add the carrots, potatoes, and corn to the pot. Simmer for 10 minutes.

3. **Add More Vegetables**  
   Stir in the zucchini, cabbage, and diced tomatoes. Let it simmer until all vegetables are tender, about 10-15 minutes.

4. **Season the Soup**  
   Taste the broth and season with salt, pepper, and cumin if desired. Add cilantro during the last 5 minutes of cooking.

5. **Serve**  
   Ladle the caldo into bowls, making sure each serving has a mix of chicken, vegetables, and broth. Serve with lime wedges on the side for added brightness. You can also add cooked rice to the bowl for a heartier meal.

---

### Optional Toppings:
- Freshly chopped cilantro
- Diced avocado
- Sliced jalapeños
- Warm tortillas or crusty bread on the side

Enjoy your warm and comforting Chicken Caldo!
