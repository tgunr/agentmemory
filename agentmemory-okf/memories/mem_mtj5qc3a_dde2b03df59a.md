### Step 1: Calculate the volume in cubic feet.
\[
\text{Volume (cubic feet)} = \text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height}
\]
\[
\text{Volume} = 10 \, \text{ft} \times 40 \, \text{ft} \times 1.5 \, \text{ft} = 600 \, \text{cubic feet}
\]

### Step 2: Convert cubic feet to cubic yards.
Since there are 27 cubic feet in a cubic yard:
\[
\text{Cubic yards} = \frac{\text{Cubic feet}}{27}
\]
\[
\text{Cubic yards} = \frac{600}{27} \approx 22.22 \, \text{cubic yards}
\]

### Final Answer:
The strip contains approximately **22.22 cubic yards**.
