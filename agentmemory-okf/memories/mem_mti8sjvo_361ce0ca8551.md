### 🤖 Assistant

A bulging belly button from a known umbilical (navel) hernia that is suddenly more prominent, taut, and painful is concerning because it can mean tissue has become trapped (an incarcerated hernia) or, less commonly, its blood supply is being cut off (a strangulated hernia). Those situations need prompt medical evaluation.

You should be evaluated today—ideally at an emergency department or an urgent care that can rapidly refer you if needed. If the pain is significant or worsening, or if you have any concern that the bulge cannot be pushed back in, I would choose the emergency department.

Go to the emergency department immediately if you have any of the following:
- Severe or rapidly worsening pain.
- The bulge is firm, very tender, or cannot be gently pushed back in when you're lying flat.
- Redness, purple, or dark discoloration over the hernia.
- Nausea, vomiting, abdominal swelling, inability to pass gas or have a bowel

movement.
- Fever or feeling very unwell.
