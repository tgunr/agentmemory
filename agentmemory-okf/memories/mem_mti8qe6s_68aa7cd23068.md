How much should I charge for the 12X15" DTF sticker for a business?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Here’s a practical way to price a 12"×15" DTF sticker for a business client.

### 1. Calculate your hard costs (per piece)

You’ll want to know these numbers for *your* setup, but here are typical ranges for a 12×15" DTF:

1. **DTF materials (film + powder + ink)**  
   - Commonly in the range of **$0.02–$0.05 per sq. in.** all‑in for small runs.  
   - 12" × 15" = 180 sq. in.  
   - Estimated materials cost:  
     - Low: 180 × $0.02 ≈ **$3.60**  
     - High: 180 × $0.05 ≈ **$9.00**

2. **Machine + maintenance + electricity (buried in overhead)**  
   - Often estimated another **$1–$3** per print.

3. **Labor**  
   - Time to prep file, print, cure, trim, pack, communicate with client.  
   - If your time is valued at $25–$40/hr and you spend ~10–15 minutes per order, that’s about **$4–$10** in labor per design/run (divide by quantity if they order multiples).

**Rough real-world cost per sticker (single piece, complex graphic):**  
Probably **$8–$15** when you include everything.

### 2. Add a healthy

profit margin

For business clients, you’re not just selling a sticker—you’re selling:

- Commercial usage
- Design expertise & reliability
- Small‑batch customization

For this reason, a **2–3× markup on true cost** is common:

- If your all‑in cost is $8 → charge **$20–$25+**
- If your all‑in cost is $12 → charge **$25–$35+**

### 3. Market-based sanity check

For a **full‑color, 12×15" custom DTF decal** sold B2B, these ranges are typical:

- **Low end (wholesale, big volume):** $15–$20 each  
- **Mid / normal retail B2B:** **$25–$40 each**  
- **Premium / rush / small one‑off:** $40–$60+

Given your design is detailed and clearly professional, and assuming this is for *business use* (signage, branding, etc.), a very reasonable starting price is:

> **$30–$40 per 12×15" DTF sticker**  
> (with discounts for quantity – e.g., 10+ pieces)

### 4. How to tier pricing

You could structure it like this (example):

- 1–4 pieces: **$40 each**
- 5–9 pieces: **$32 each**
- 10–24 pieces: **$28 each**
- 25+ pieces:

custom quote (aim to keep at least 40–50% margin)

### 5. Things that justify the higher end of the range

Charge toward the higher end if:

- Complex, highly detailed art (like the one shown)  
- Rush turnaround
- You provide design tweaks / revisions
- They are using it in a revenue‑generating way (storefront, product branding, etc.)
- You’re doing color‑critical proofing and test prints
