**Bellofram rolling diaphragms** (also called Bellofram Rolling Diaphragms or BRDs) are frictionless, leak-proof fabric-reinforced elastomer seals invented by Bellofram Diaphragm in 1950. They are now produced by Bellofram Elastomers (part of the Marsh Bellofram Group), based in Newell, West Virginia.<grok:render card_id="ecef4e" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>

### How They Work
Formed as a truncated cone or “top-hat,” the diaphragm is inverted on installation. During piston stroke it rolls and unrolls alternately between the piston skirt and cylinder wall. This eliminates sliding friction and breakaway friction while creating a complete barrier against blow-by leakage.<grok:render card_id="ca8c7d" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">8</argument></grok:render>

Key performance characteristics include:
- Pressures up to ~3,000 psi (depending on materials and geometry).
- Temperatures from approximately –120°F to +600°F.
- Resistance to oil, ozone, acids, alkalies, steam, and many corrosive fluids.
- Long stroke capability with smooth, sensitive response.
- No lubrication required and very low hysteresis.<grok:render card_id="15eedf" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">8</argument></grok:render>

Construction typically consists of a specially woven fabric layer impregnated with a thin elastomer coating (single- or double-coated options available). Homogeneous (non-reinforced) and metal-inserted versions also exist.

### Main Classes / Designs
Classes primarily describe the flange or bead style used for mounting and sealing:

- **Class 4** — Most common; flat gasket-type flange for flat mating surfaces. Economical hardware; suitable for higher pressures. Pre-convoluted version is Class 4C.
- **Class 3** — D-shaped bead for axial compression in a mating groove; no flange perforations needed. Pre-convoluted version is Class 3C.
- **Class 1A** — O-ring-type bead around the full circumference for minimum outside flange diameter; no bolt holes required. Recommended for differential pressures ≤150 psi. Pre-convoluted options available.
- **Class 1B** — Rectangular bead clamped inside the cylinder bore for the smallest package size.
- **Involuted (IVD)** — Patented design with a different sidewall geometry for reduced hardware envelope and lower assembly cost in certain applications.<grok:render card_id="162495" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

“Top-hat” styles are inverted at assembly for long strokes; “C-style” (pre-convoluted) versions are molded in the installed shape for easier assembly and limited stroke, though they introduce a small spring gradient/centering effect.

### Typical Applications
- Pressure regulators, air relays, and instrumentation/process control.
- Linear actuators and diaphragm air cylinders (single- and double-acting).
- Pumps, valves, demand valves, dashpots, and gauge protectors.
- Aerospace, automotive, medical, industrial manufacturing, oil & gas, and specialized uses (e.g., soil testing cells or historical blood pumps).<grok:render card_id="ee01f8" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">5</argument></grok:render>

Bellofram also produces related products such as diaphragm air cylinders that incorporate these seals for low-friction, sensitive actuation.
