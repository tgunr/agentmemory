This copycat recipe for Buffalo Wild Wings Caribbean Jerk sauce captures the signature balance of tropical sweetness, tangy vinegar, and earthy island spices like allspice, cloves, and nutmeg.

### **Buffalo Wild Wings Caribbean Jerk Sauce Recipe**
According to [Kitchen Divas](https://kitchendivas.com/buffalo-wild-wings-caribbean-jerk-sauce/), the key to this sauce is a long simmer to allow the complex spices to meld and the sugars to caramelize.

#### **Ingredients**
*   **Base:** 1 cup water, ¾ cup ketchup, 10 tbsp dark brown sugar.
*   **Acids:** ¼ cup apple cider vinegar, 2 tbsp white vinegar, 2 tsp lemon juice.
*   **Heat:** 2 tbsp Frank’s RedHot (or similar cayenne sauce), 1 tsp cayenne pepper (add more for extra heat), 2 tsp ground black pepper.
*   **Aromatics:** 3 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp minced green onion (white and light green parts only).
*   **Spices & Seasoning:** 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce, ¾ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp onion powder, ½ tsp dried thyme, 1 tsp dried parsley, ½ tsp salt, ⅛ tsp rubbed

sage.
*   **The "Jerk" Spices:** ¼ to ½ tsp each of ground allspice, ground cloves, and ground nutmeg.
*   **Thickener:** 1 tsp cornstarch.

#### **Instructions**
1.  **Sauté Aromatics:** Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the minced green onions and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened. Do not let them brown.
2.  **Combine:** Remove the pan from the heat. Whisk in all remaining ingredients until the sugar and cornstarch are fully dissolved.
3.  **Simmer:** Return the pan to the heat and bring to a boil. Immediately reduce the heat to low and simmer uncovered for 40–45 minutes. The sauce is ready when it has darkened in color and thickened to a syrupy consistency.
4.  **Cool and Serve:** Allow the sauce to cool slightly before tossing with crispy fried or baked wings.

### **Tips for Success**
*   **Adjust the Spices:** Some users suggest increasing the allspice, cloves, and nutmeg to a full ½ teaspoon each if you want a more pronounced "island" flavor that cuts through the ketchup base

([Kitchen Divas](https://kitchendivas.com/buffalo-wild-wings-caribbean-jerk-sauce/)).
*   **Storage:** This sauce can be made in advance and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
*   **Application:** For the best restaurant-style experience, toss the wings in the sauce while they are still hot from the fryer or oven so the sauce "sets" on the skin.
