### 🤖 Assistant

Based on the visual characteristics of the text and official United States Postal Inspection Service (USPIS) branding, here is an identification of the fonts used on the plaque:

### Main Heading ("HOUSTON DIVISION")
The blue heading uses a **Heavy/Black Sans-Serif** font with a modern, blocky aesthetic. 
*   **Likely Font:** **Eurostile Bold Extended / Microgramma Bold Extended**
*   **Characteristics:** These fonts are characterized by square-shouldered curves and wide proportions, which are commonly used in government and military-adjacent branding for a sense of technological authority and strength.

### Body Text & Subheaders
The mixed-case and italicized serif sections ("Our Commitment," "Shared Responsibility," etc.) utilize a traditional, authoritative typeface.
*   **Likely Font:** **Goudy Old Style** (specifically the Bold, Italic, and Regular weights).
*   **Characteristics:** You can identify Goudy by the distinctive ear on the lowercase `g`, the slanted cross-stroke on the lowercase `e`, and the

diamond-shaped dots (titles) on the `i` and `j`. 
*   **Alternative:** **Adobe Jenson** or **Garamond** variants are sometimes used in similar official capacities, but the specific weight and "old-style" feel in the image closely match the Goudy family.

### Signature Line ("One Division. One Mission. One Standard.")
The footer line uses a cleaner, more contemporary serif font.
*   **Likely Font:** **Century Schoolbook** or **Cambria**.
*   **Characteristics:** This font is designed for high legibility and is standard in many U.S. government documents and plaques.

### Specific Brand Alignment
According to [USPIS Press Kits](https://www.uspis.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/uspis-press-kit-division-map.pdf), the agency frequently utilizes strong, traditional typography for their official seals and mission statements to convey a sense of tradition and trust. The combination of an "Extended" sans-serif for titles and "Goudy-style" serifs for text is a classic aesthetic for federal law enforcement plaques.
