Here's a breakdown of their beliefs and characteristics:

Adherents believe that African Americans are descendants of the Moabites and are "Moorish" by nationality and Islamic by faith. They claim legal immunity from U.S. federal, state, and local laws, based on a mistaken belief that the 1786 Moroccan-American Treaty of Friendship grants them sovereignty [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moorish_sovereign_citizens), [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/explainers/moorish-sovereign-citizens/).
*

**Anti-Government Ideology:** Like other sovereign citizens, Moorish sovereign citizens believe the current American government is illegitimate and has been operating under false pretenses since as early as the 19th century. They often cite events like the U.S. abandonment of the gold standard in the 1930s or the Reconstruction Era as catalysts for this perceived illegitimacy [Emory International Law Review](https://scholarlycommons.law.emory.edu/eilr/vol35/iss2/4/).
*   **Tactics:** They engage in various anti-government activities, including creating their own legal documents, claiming diplomatic immunity, refusing to recognize the authority of law enforcement, and filing false liens against government officials. They may also use fake license plates and identification cards with Moorish imagery [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/explainers/moorish-sovereign-citizens/).
*   **"Paper Terrorism" and Other Actions:** Their actions can range from filing paperwork to declare their "free man" status to criminal

activities like claiming rights to residences they don't own or engaging in confrontations with law enforcement [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/explainers/moorish-sovereign-citizens/).
*   **Disavowal by Moorish Science Temple:** It's important to note that several factions of the Moorish Science Temple of America have disavowed any affiliation with these groups, calling them "radical and subversive fringe groups" [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moorish_sovereign_citizens/).
*   **Classification:** The Southern Poverty Law Center classifies Moorish sovereign citizens as an extremist anti-government group [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moorish_sovereign_citizens), [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/explainers/moorish-sovereign-citizens/).

In essence, a "Moorish National Citizen" is an individual who adheres to the Moorish sovereign citizen movement's ideology, believing themselves to be part of a sovereign nation not subject to U.S. laws, often based on a unique interpretation of history

and legal documents.
