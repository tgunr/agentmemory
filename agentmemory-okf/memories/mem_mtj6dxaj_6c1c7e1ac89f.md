**HomemadeTools.net** is a long-running membership site (founded 2012) focused on helping people build their own shop tools, jigs, fixtures, machine tools, and equipment instead of buying expensive commercial versions.<grok:render card_id="8b2804" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

### Core offering
- Access to a curated library of **3,000+ tool plans and build guides** (drawings, bills of materials, photos, videos, build notes, and reference links).
- 23 downloadable shop guides.
- An active builder community/forum of machinists, metalworkers, woodworkers, and DIYers.
- Regular additions of new plans and member projects.
- Related resources such as an encyclopedia of tens of thousands of homemade tools from across the web and a daily newsletter of notable builds.

The site is operated by **Buildthreads, Inc.** (Boulder, Colorado area), which is BBB-accredited with an A+ rating. Members have collectively documented thousands of homemade tools over the years.<grok:render card_id="9c6fef" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>

### Membership
- **$9/month** or **$69/year** (roughly $5.75/month equivalent).
- Full digital access (works on computers, tablets, and phones; nothing is mailed).
- 30-day money-back guarantee.
- Over 3,300 active paying members reported.

### Related network
It is part of a broader “BuildThreads” ecosystem that also includes sites focused on specific vehicles, boats, planes, etc., and surfaces hundreds of fresh build threads daily from many forums.

In short, it’s a specialized paid resource and community for people who like fabricating their own tools and shop equipment. The public-facing pages emphasize organized access to practical build documentation and peer support rather than free unlimited browsing of everything.
