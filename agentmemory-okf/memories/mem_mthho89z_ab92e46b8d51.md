   - You use 0.25 pounds (a quarter pound) of grass seed for 40 square feet.

2. **Determine the area of a quarter acre:**
   - 1 acre = 43,560 square feet.
   - A quarter of an acre = 43,560 ÷ 4 = 10,890 square feet.

3. **Calculate the grass seed per square foot:**
   - For 40 ft², you use 0.25 pounds.
   - Grass seed per square foot = 0.25 ÷ 40 = 0.00625 pounds per ft².

4. **Calculate the total pounds needed for 10,890 ft²:**
   - Total pounds = 0.00625 × 10,890 = 68.0625 pounds.
