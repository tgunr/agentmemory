Short answer: Early–mid 1960s.

- The Secret Service was using AR-15 rifles by 1963. There is contemporaneous documentation that an AR-15 was in the Secret Service follow‑up car during the JFK motorcade in November 1963. - The USSS is widely understood to have transitioned to M16/M16A1 variants later in the 1960s and then to M4‑type carbines in later decades; but precise internal “issue” dates aren’t publicly detailed.
