### Key Details

**Robinhood Banking**
- Full checking + savings accounts provided by Coastal Community Bank (Member FDIC), with funds swept across program banks for higher coverage.
- **3.50% APY** on both checking and savings is available with an annual Gold subscription. Full benefits (especially checking interest and better fee structure) generally require at least $1,000 in monthly direct deposits. Premier tier ($100k+ assets under custody) unlocks better ATM treatment and free wires.
- Strong integration with Robinhood brokerage (instant transfers between banking and investing accounts, net-worth tracking).
- Features include debit card, mobile check deposit, physical checks, cash deposits at retailers, and limited cash delivery (currently restricted/pricey).
- No overdraft fees and competitive foreign transaction treatment (0% on debit).
- Main drawbacks: Paid Gold membership required, benefits are conditional on direct deposits and assets, relatively new product, and no branches.
