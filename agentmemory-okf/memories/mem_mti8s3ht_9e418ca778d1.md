The US Treasury does not launch cryptocurrencies or "reserve" tokens on the Solana blockchain. *   **Market Manipulation**: Analysis of the $USRNR token shows it has already experienced a **96.96% price drop** in a single day, which is

a major red flag for a "rug pull" where creators dump their holdings on retail investors [Bitrue](https://www.bitrue.com/blog/is-usrnr-coin-legit-rug-pull-analysis).

In reality, $USRNR is a "narrative-driven" micro-cap token with no actual utility or backing by physical natural resources [WEEX](https://www.weex.com/wiki/article/what-is-us-reserve-for-natural-resources-usrnr-coin-53000).
