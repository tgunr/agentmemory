---

### 🤖 Assistant

To pair AirPods on a Mac:

1.  **On your Mac**, click the **Control Center icon** in the menu bar (it looks like two toggles).
2.  Click **Bluetooth**.
3.  **Open the lid of your AirPods case** (with the AirPods inside) and hold it close to your Mac.
4.  **On your Mac**, select your AirPods from the list of Bluetooth devices.
5.  Click **Connect**.

If your AirPods don't appear, make sure they are charged and in pairing mode (press and hold the button on the back of the case until the status light flashes white).
