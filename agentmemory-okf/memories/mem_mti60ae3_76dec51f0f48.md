## Official Onefinity Replacement Controller

Onefinity offers a replacement BB (Buildbotics) Controller for Original/Pro/X35/X50 Series machines (does not work with ELITE machines). You must have an order number for a machine to purchase this item, and orders not associated with a preexisting machine order number will be canceled.

**Key Requirements:**
- You need your original machine order number
- Available for 110v (North America) or 220v (international)
- Comes with a one-year warranty
- Must reset configuration after installation

**Contact Information:**
- Email: info@onefinitycnc.com (especially if you need 220v version)
- Include your order number when contacting them

## Alternative Sources

1. **Buildbotics Direct**: Since Onefinity uses Buildbotics controllers, you can contact Buildbotics directly at info@buildbotics.com or call 707-559-8539

2. **Forum Community**: Recent forum posts show other users looking for replacement controllers, suggesting you might find used units from members who have upgraded

## Additional Parts Available

Onefinity also sells specific controller components:
- Replacement HDMI internal control box pigtail
- Emergency stop switches (for BB controllers only)

## Next Steps

1. Contact Onefinity support at info@onefinitycnc.com with your machine's order number
2. If you don't have the order number, try reaching out to their support team anyway
3. Consider posting on the Onefinity CNC Forum to see if any community members have spare controllers
4. Contact Buildbotics directly as an alternative source

The official replacement route through Onefinity appears to be your best option, especially since it comes with warranty coverage and proper configuration support.
