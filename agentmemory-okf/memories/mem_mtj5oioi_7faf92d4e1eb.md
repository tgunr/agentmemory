Recipe for breading shrimp that sticks well

### 🤖 Assistant

Certainly! Here's a recipe for breading shrimp that sticks well:

Ingredients:
- 1 pound (450 grams) shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- Vegetable oil, for frying

Instructions:

1. In a shallow bowl, combine the flour, salt, black pepper, paprika, and garlic powder. Mix well to evenly distribute the spices.

2. Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture. This will help the breading adhere better.

3. Dip each shrimp into the flour mixture, coating it thoroughly on all sides. Press gently to ensure the flour adheres well to the shrimp. Shake off any excess flour.

4. Place the beaten eggs in another shallow bowl.

5. Dip the floured shrimp into the beaten eggs, ensuring they are fully coated. Allow any excess egg to drip off.

6. Return the shrimp to the flour mixture and coat them once again. Press gently to help the second layer of flour adhere to the shrimp. Shake off any excess flour.

7. Place the breaded shrimp on a plate or wire rack and let them rest for a few minutes. This resting period will help the breading set and adhere even better.

8. Heat vegetable oil in a deep pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Make sure there's enough oil to fully submerge the shrimp.

9. Once the oil is hot (around 350°F or 175°C), carefully add the breaded shrimp, a few at a time, to the pan. Do not overcrowd the pan, as it can lower the oil temperature and make the shrimp greasy.

10. Fry the shrimp for about 2-3 minutes per side or until they turn golden brown and crispy. Use tongs or a slotted spoon to flip them halfway through the cooking process.

11. Once cooked, remove the shrimp from the oil and transfer them to a plate lined with paper towels to drain excess oil.

12. Repeat the frying process with the remaining shrimp, ensuring the oil stays hot.

13. Serve the breaded shrimp immediately as an appetizer or main dish. They can be enjoyed plain, dipped in your favorite sauce, or as part of a shrimp po' boy sandwich.

Enjoy your delicious crispy breaded shrimp!
