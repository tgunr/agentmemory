### 🤖 Assistant

Based on the interface shown in your screenshot, the **Ink Mode** settings in EufyMaker (likely for a UV printer or similar specialized hardware) determine the order and layering of white ink and color (CMYK) ink.

To achieve your goal of printing the white on the eagle while keeping the background transparent (not printing the white background of the image), you need to understand how the software interprets "white" and how it layers the ink.

#### Understanding the Ink Modes

*   **White > CMYK (Selected):** This is the most common mode for printing on dark or transparent substrates. It prints a layer of white ink first as a "base," then prints the color on top. If your file has a white background, the printer will likely try to print a solid white block under everything.
*   **CMYK:** This prints only the color ink. Any white areas in your image will be treated as "no ink," meaning the eagle's white feathers would simply be the color of the material you are printing on.
*   **Gloss Varnish:** Adds a

clear, shiny protective or decorative layer.
*   **White:** Prints only the white ink channel.
*   **CMYK > White:** Used for "second surface" printing (e.g., printing on the back of clear acrylic to be viewed from the front). It prints color first, then covers it with white.
*   **CMYK > White > CMYK:** Creates a double-sided effect on clear materials with a white block-out layer in the middle.
*   **White > CMYK > Gloss Varnish:** A full-stack print including the base, color, and a top gloss coat.

#### How to Print Only the Eagle's White

To print the white feathers of the eagle in `Color Eagle.png` without printing the white rectangular background, you must address the **Source File** and the **Ink Mode** together:

**1. Prepare the File (Crucial Step)**
The printer generally sees "White" in a standard JPG or PNG as a color to be printed. To prevent the background from printing, you should use a PNG with a **transparent background**. You need to remove the white background around the eagle and the text

in a photo editor so that only the eagle and text remain on a "checkerboard" transparent layer.

**2. Select the Correct Mode**
Once you have a transparent background file:
*   **Use "White > CMYK":** Because you want the white feathers to be vibrant, the printer needs to use its white ink channel. With a transparent background, the software will only trigger the white ink for the pixels that actually contain data (the eagle and text).
*   **Check "Skip Printing":** If there is an option to "Skip White" or "Ignore Background," ensure it is configured to treat transparency as "no ink."

**3. The "Sticker" or "Customize" Option**
Some versions of this software have a **Sticker** mode that automatically detects the edges of an object and applies white ink only within those bounds. If your file is not perfectly transparent, you might explore the **Customize** menu to see if you can set a "White Ink Limit" or "Spot Color" setting that tells the printer to only use white ink where the CMYK values are above a

certain threshold.

#### Summary for your Poster
If you print the `Color Eagle.png` as it currently looks (with a solid white background) using **White > CMYK**, you will get a solid white rectangle on your material. You must **remove the background** from the image first, then use **White > CMYK** to ensure the white feathers of the eagle are printed with actual white ink.
