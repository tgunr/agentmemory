### 1. Search terms to use
In your image site of choice (Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, iStock, Freepik, etc.), search:

- `AC-130 gunship side view PNG`
- `AC-130 Spectre gunship illustration`
- `AC-130 silhouette vector`
- Add `transparent background` or filter for PNG / vector.

For a tumbler, **vector** (SVG/AI/EPS) or very high‑res PNG is ideal.

### 2. Technical specs for DTF/UV tumbler printing
When choosing the file, check:

- **Resolution**: at least **300 DPI at final print size**  
  - For a 20 oz tumbler wrap, a good working canvas is about **9.3" × 8.2" (236 mm × 208 mm)**  
  - That means an image of around **2800 × 2500 px** or larger.
- **Background**: preferably **transparent** or pure white so you can knock it out.
-

**Orientation**: landscape, with the AC‑130 flying horizontally, so it wraps nicely around the tumbler.
- **File type**: PNG, SVG, or high‑quality JPEG (if background is simple).

### 3. Legal/usage considerations
Since you’ll be *printing* the image:

- Make sure the license allows **commercial use** if you plan to sell the tumblers.
- Avoid images with unit patches, nose art, or recognizable people unless the stock site explicitly allows it.

### 4. Preparing it for your tumbler
Once you’ve downloaded a candidate image:

1. Create a canvas at your tumbler’s template size (from your Dufy printer docs).
2. Place and scale the AC‑130 so it’s:
   - Not too close to the top/bottom bleed areas.
   - Centered horizontally or composed where you want it (e.g., flying left to right).
3. Export as **PNG at 300 DPI** (or as the format your Dufy software recommends).
