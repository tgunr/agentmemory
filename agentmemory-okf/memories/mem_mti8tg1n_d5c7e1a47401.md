### 🤖 Assistant

Here’s a simple, reliable way to make great French fries at home.

### 1. Ingredients
- Potatoes (Russet/Idaho work best)
- Neutral oil with high smoke point (canola, peanut, sunflower, etc.)
- Salt
- Optional: garlic powder, paprika, pepper, herbs, etc.

### 2. Prep the Potatoes
1. Peel (optional) and cut into sticks:
   - About ¼–½ inch (0.5–1.2 cm) thick for classic fries.
2. Rinse the cut potatoes under cold water until the water runs mostly clear.
3. Soak in cold water for 30–60 minutes (or up to a few hours in the fridge).
   - This removes excess starch and helps them get crisp.

### 3. Dry Thoroughly
- Drain the potatoes and pat completely dry with clean towels or paper towels.
- Moisture = splattering oil and soggy fries, so get them as dry as you can.

### 4. Two-Step Frying Method (Best Texture)

#### First Fry (Blanch)
1. Heat oil to about **300°F / 150°C**.
2. Fry potatoes in small batches for **4–6 minutes**:
   - They should soften but not brown.
3. Remove to a paper towel–lined tray or

rack.
4. Let them cool at least 10–15 minutes (you can even refrigerate or freeze briefly).

#### Second Fry (Crisp)
1. Increase oil to **375°F / 190°C**.
2. Fry the blanched potatoes again, in batches, for **2–4 minutes**:
   - Until golden and crisp.
3. Remove, drain, and immediately sprinkle with salt and any seasonings.

### 5. Oven or Air Fryer Option (Healthier)

#### Oven Fries
1. Heat oven to **425°F / 220°C**.
2. Toss dried potato sticks with:
   - 1–2 tbsp oil per large potato
   - Salt and seasonings
3. Spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
4. Bake **25–35 minutes**, flipping halfway, until browned and crispy.

#### Air Fryer Fries
1. Toss dried sticks with a little oil and salt.
2. Cook at **375–400°F / 190–200°C** for **15–25 minutes**, shaking basket or flipping a couple of times.
