Here’s a classic **Blooming Onion** recipe like you’d get at a steakhouse:

---

## Ingredients
- 1 large sweet onion (like Vidalia)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup buttermilk (or regular milk with a splash of lemon juice)
- 2 eggs
- Oil for deep frying (peanut or vegetable oil)

### For dipping sauce:
- ½ cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp prepared horseradish
- ½ tsp paprika
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- Salt & pepper to taste

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prep the onion
1. Cut about ½ inch off the top of the onion (leave root intact).
2. Peel skin. Place onion root-side down.
3. Starting ½ inch from root, make 12–16 cuts downward (like petals). Don’t cut through the root.
4. Flip upright and gently separate petals.

### 2. Make the batter
- In one bowl, whisk together **flour, paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, black pepper, salt**.
- In another bowl, whisk **buttermilk + eggs**.

### 3. Coat the onion
1. Dip onion into flour mixture, shaking off excess.
2. Submerge into egg mixture (use a spoon to coat all layers).
3. Dip back into flour mixture, pressing lightly to coat thoroughly.

### 4. Fry
- Heat oil to **375°F (190°C)** in a deep fryer or heavy pot.
- Place onion root-side up on a slotted spoon and carefully lower into oil.
- Fry about **6–8 minutes**, until golden brown and crispy.
- Remove, drain on paper towels.

### 5. Sauce
- Mix all dipping sauce ingredients until smooth.
- Serve onion hot with sauce on the side.
