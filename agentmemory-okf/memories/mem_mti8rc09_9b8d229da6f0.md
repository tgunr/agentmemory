### 👤 User

How deep is a normal 8’5” fence post supposed to be in the ground

---

### 🤖 Assistant

For a standard fence post that is 8 feet 5 inches long, the general rule of thumb is to bury at least one-third of the total length of the post in the ground.

Based on this rule, an 8'5" (101 inches) post should be buried approximately 34 inches deep. This depth ensures that the post is stable enough to withstand wind loads and the weight of the fence panels. In many regions, it is also critical to ensure the post hole extends below the local frost line to prevent "frost heave," which can push the post out of the ground during freeze-thaw cycles.

Most professional installers aim for a depth of 3 feet (36 inches) for a post of this size, which leaves about 5 feet 5 inches of post above the ground. This is a common height for a standard 5-foot or 6-foot privacy fence once you account for a few inches of clearance at the bottom. Additionally, the hole should typically be about three times the width of the post to allow for a proper concrete collar.
