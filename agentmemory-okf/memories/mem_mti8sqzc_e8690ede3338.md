Here's the best way to apply a sticker to a white oak plaque:

**Preparation:**
- Clean the plaque surface with a damp cloth and let it dry completely—dust and moisture prevent proper adhesion
- Lightly sand the area where the sticker will go with fine-grit sandpaper (220+) to help it stick better
- Wipe away any dust with a tack cloth

**Application:**
1. **Peel slowly** — Remove the backing carefully, starting from one corner
2. **Position first** — Place the sticker without pressing down initially; adjust until it's perfectly aligned
3. **Press from center outward** — Use a squeegee, credit card, or soft cloth to smooth out air bubbles, working from the middle toward the edges
4. **Apply firm, even pressure** — Spend 30-60 seconds pressing to ensure good contact with the wood grain

**Pro Tips:**
- Use a **transfer tape** (clear adhesive tape) if the sticker has small or intricate details—it helps keep pieces together during application
- For textured oak, a **vinyl sticker** adheres better than paper

stickers
- Avoid applying stickers in direct sunlight or extreme heat, which can cause bubbling
- Let the adhesive cure for 24 hours before handling or sealing the plaque
- If you plan to seal the plaque, use a **clear polyurethane or epoxy** over the sticker for protection

**If bubbles appear:**
- Use a small needle to poke a tiny hole and press out the air
- Or carefully lift the edge and re-smooth it down

### 🤖 Assistant

After applying the sticker, here's what to do next:

**Immediate (First 24 hours):**
- **Don't touch or handle** the plaque—let the adhesive fully cure
- Keep it in a stable environment (avoid temperature fluctuations)
- Don't expose it to moisture or humidity

**After 24 hours:**

1. **Seal/Protect the sticker** (recommended):
   - Apply a **clear polyurethane, polycrylic, or epoxy resin** over the entire plaque to protect the sticker from peeling, fading, and wear
   - This is especially important if the plaque will be displayed outdoors or in high-traffic areas
   - Use 2-3 thin coats rather than one thick coat

2. **Display or use the plaque**:
   - Hang it on a wall, place it on a shelf, or gift it
   - If it's an award or recognition plaque, you're ready to present it

3. **Maintenance**:
   - Dust gently with a soft cloth
   - Avoid harsh cleaners that could damage the sticker
   - Keep away from direct sunlight if the sticker has vibrant colors (prevents fading)

**Optional finishing touches:**
- Add

a **felt backing** or mounting hardware if hanging
- Apply wood stain or finish to the edges/back if desired (before sealing over the sticker)
