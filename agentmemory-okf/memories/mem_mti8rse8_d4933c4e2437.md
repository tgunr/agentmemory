### 🤖 Assistant

Lamb shank is perfect for slow cooking—rich, tender, and fall-off-the-bone when done right. Here are a few solid ways to make it, depending on your vibe:

---

### 🍷 Classic Red Wine Braised Lamb Shanks
Deep, comforting, restaurant-style.

**Ingredients:**
- 2–4 lamb shanks  
- Salt, pepper  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 onion (chopped)  
- 2 carrots + 2 celery stalks (chopped)  
- 4 garlic cloves  
- 2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 2 cups red wine  
- 2 cups beef or lamb stock  
- 2 sprigs rosemary + thyme  
- 1 bay leaf  

**Steps:**
1. Season and sear lamb shanks until browned. Remove.
2. Sauté veg, add garlic + tomato paste.
3. Deglaze with wine, reduce slightly.
4. Add stock + herbs, return lamb.
5. Cover and braise at 325°F (160°C) for ~2.5–3 hours.
6. Finish by reducing sauce if needed.

👉 Serve with mashed potatoes or polenta.

---

### 🍅 Mediterranean Tomato & Herb Lamb Shanks
Lighter, bright flavors.

**Add/Swap:**
- Use canned crushed tomatoes instead of wine-heavy base  
- Add cinnamon stick + oregano  
-

Finish with lemon zest + parsley  

👉 Great with couscous or orzo.

---

### 🧄 Garlic & Rosemary Slow Cooker Lamb Shanks
Low effort, still amazing.

**Ingredients:**
- Lamb shanks, garlic (lots), rosemary  
- Onion, carrots  
- 1 cup red wine (optional)  
- 1–2 cups stock  

**Steps:**
1. Sear first (worth it).
2. Toss everything into slow cooker.
3. Cook low 8 hours or high 4–5 hours.

---

### 🌶️ Moroccan-Spiced Lamb Shanks
Warm, slightly sweet, super flavorful.

**Spices:**
- Cumin, coriander, cinnamon, paprika, turmeric  
- Add chickpeas + dried apricots  

**Liquid:**
- Stock + a bit of tomato  

👉 Finish with cilantro + serve over couscous.

---

### 🍺 Beer-Braised Lamb Shanks
Hearty and slightly bitter edge.

**Swap wine for:**
- Dark beer (stout or porter)

👉 Works great with roasted root veg.
