### 🤖 Assistant

The **USAF Survival, Evasion, Resistance, and Escape (SERE) training school** is the U.S. Air Force program that teaches personnel how to survive and return with honor if isolated in hostile environments.

### Basics
- Officially known as **SERE training**
- Main Air Force schoolhouse: **Fairchild Air Force Base, Washington**
- It prepares aircrew and other at-risk personnel for:
  - wilderness survival
  - evasion from capture
  - resistance if captured
  - escape planning and mindset

### What it covers
- Shelter building
- Fire starting
- Water procurement
- Food sourcing basics
- Navigation and signaling
- Survival in different climates
- Code of Conduct training
- Captivity resistance concepts

### Who goes
Typically:
- Pilots
- Aircrew
- Special operations personnel
- Others whose missions put them at higher risk of isolation or capture

### Different levels
There are multiple SERE training levels depending on career field and risk exposure. Not every Air Force member attends the full high-risk

course.
