### **Vector Badge Design Breakdown**

#### **1. Shape & Layout**
- **Base:** Start with a perfect circle for the badge.
- **Borders:**  
  - Outer border: Thick, dark charcoal (almost black) ring.  
  - Just inside: Thin, light (off-white or light gray) ring.  
  - Next: Slightly thinner, darker ring (deep green or dark gray).
- **Background:**  
  - Inside the rings, use a solid, deep green or midnight blue fill to evoke a night-ops feel.

#### **2. Central Graphic (No Text)**
- **Skull:**  
  - Stylized, aggressive skull facing left, with sharp, clean lines.  
  - Use solid white for the skull, with dark charcoal for outlines and details (eye sockets, nose, jaw, teeth).
- **Flames:**  
  - Flames trailing from the

back of the skull, flowing rightward.  
  - Use flat, solid fills: deep blue, indigo, and a touch of purple for dramatic effect.  
  - No gradients—just layered, jagged flame shapes with clean separation.
- **Gatling Gun:**  
  - The skull grips a stylized, simplified minigun.  
  - Use flat grays and dark charcoal for the gun body, with a bright yellow-orange muzzle flash at the barrel tip.
  - The muzzle flash should be a sharp, angular burst—no glow or blur.
- **Circular Framing:**  
  - The graphic is centered and sized to fit comfortably within the inner circle, leaving a margin all around.

#### **3. Style & Finish**
- **No Text:**  
  - Omit all wording (“GHOSTRIDER”, “AC-130J GUNSHIP”).
- **No Texture:**  
  - Absolutely no fabric, embroidery, or shadow effects.
- **No Patch Edge:**  
  - The outermost ring is a clean, solid vector line—no thread or stitching.
- **No Perspective:**  
  - The badge is perfectly flat and front-facing.
- **Contrast:**  
  - Use high-contrast colors for all elements to

ensure crisp UV print results.
- **Background:**  
  - Transparent outside the badge circle.
