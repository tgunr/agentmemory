### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-08-15T16:58:54Z

There are actually two different projects with similar names. The open-source Nearchat on GitHub is a location-based community social app. It's ad-free, lets you see nearby users on a map, join local groups, post in a feed, and send private messages after mutual acceptance. It's still in early MVP stage with just one star and no releases yet. Then there's NearChat, the offline Bluetooth mesh messaging app from nearchat.net. This one works without any internet or cell service, using Bluetooth to create a self-healing network where phones relay messages. It features end-to-end encryption, QR code contact exchange, group chats, and voice notes. It's privacy-first with no accounts or phone numbers, and it's available on Android with iOS coming soon.
