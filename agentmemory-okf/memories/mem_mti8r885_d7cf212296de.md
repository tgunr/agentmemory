### 👤 User

How do I change the focus on Apple iPhone?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

To change the Focus mode on your iPhone, you can use the Control Center or the Settings app to customize how you receive notifications and which apps can reach you.

### Using Control Center
The quickest way to switch between Focus modes is through the Control Center:

*   Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen (or swipe up from the bottom if your iPhone has a Home button).
*   Tap the Focus button, which usually displays the icon of your current mode, such as a moon for Do Not Disturb or a person for Personal.
*   Select the Focus mode you want to turn on, such as Work, Sleep, or Do Not Disturb.
*   To turn a Focus off, simply tap the active Focus icon again.

### Using Settings for Customization
If you want to create a new Focus or change the rules for an existing one, you can do so in the Settings app:

*   Open Settings and tap on Focus.
*   Select an existing Focus to edit its settings, or tap the plus (+) icon in the top-right corner to create a new one.
*   Within each Focus, you can

choose specific people and apps that are allowed to send notifications.
*   You can also set a schedule or automation to have the Focus turn on automatically based on time, location, or when you open a specific app.
*   Focus Filters allow you to customize how apps like Calendar, Mail, and Messages behave when that specific Focus is active.

### Switching from the Lock Screen
If you have linked a specific Lock Screen to a Focus, you can change it directly from there:

*   Press and hold your current Lock Screen.
*   Tap the Focus button at the bottom of the screen.
*   Choose a different Focus to switch both the mode and your wallpaper/widgets simultaneously.
