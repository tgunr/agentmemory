### 🤖 Assistant

With scallops, whole blue crab, carrots, onion, ginger, bell pepper, and celery, you can make a delicious seafood stir-fry. Here's a simple recipe:

Ingredients:
- Scallops
- Whole blue crab
- Carrots
- Onion
- Ginger
- Bell pepper
- Celery
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Garlic (if available)
- Rice or noodles (for serving)

Instructions:
1. Prepare the seafood: Clean and prepare the crab, and if the scallops are large, you can slice them in half. Remove any shells or inedible parts.

2. Chop the vegetables: Dice the carrots, onion, ginger, bell pepper, and celery into bite-sized pieces.

3. Heat a large pan or wok over high heat and add a bit of oil. Stir-fry the ginger and garlic for a minute or until fragrant.

4. Add the crab and stir-fry for a few minutes until it turns pink and slightly crispy.

5. Add the rest of the vegetables and stir-fry for a few more minutes until they start to soften.

6. Add the scallops and continue to stir-fry for a few minutes until they're cooked through and slightly caramelized.

7. Season the stir-fry with soy sauce and a drizzle of sesame oil to taste. Adjust the seasoning according to your preference.

8. Serve the seafood stir-fry hot over cooked rice or noodles.

Enjoy your homemade seafood stir-fry!
