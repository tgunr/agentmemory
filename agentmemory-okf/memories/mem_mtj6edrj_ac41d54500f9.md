**Easy One-Pan Coconut Shrimp Curry**  
*Perfect for beginners – ready in about 30 minutes with simple ingredients and one skillet.*

This creamy, mildly spiced shrimp curry is beginner-friendly, forgiving, and packed with flavor. It uses everyday ingredients and clear visual cues (like shrimp turning pink) so you know exactly when it’s done. No complicated spice blends or fancy techniques required!

### Quick Facts
- **Prep time:** 10 minutes  
- **Cook time:** 15–20 minutes  
- **Total time:** ~30 minutes  
- **Servings:** 4  
- **Difficulty:** Beginner  

### Ingredients
- 1 lb (450 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined (fresh or frozen)  
- 1 tablespoon oil or butter  
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped (about 1 cup)  
- 1 bell pepper (red, green, or yellow), chopped (optional but recommended)  
- 2–3 garlic cloves, minced (optional but adds great flavor)  
- 1 tablespoon curry powder (mild or medium – your favorite brand works)  
- ½ teaspoon salt (plus more to taste)  
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper  
- 1 can (13.5–14 oz / 400 ml) **full-fat** coconut milk (not light)  
- ¼–½ cup water or broth (to adjust sauce thickness)  
- **Optional add-ins:** 1 tsp grated fresh ginger, handful of spinach or peas, fresh cilantro + lime wedges for serving  

**For serving:** Hot cooked rice (jasmine or basmati is ideal)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Prep the shrimp** (if frozen): Thaw in a bowl of cold water for 10–15 minutes. Pat very dry with paper towels. This helps them cook nicely instead of steaming.

2. Heat the oil or butter in a large skillet or sauté pan over **medium heat**.

3. Add the chopped onion (and bell pepper if using). Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden, about 5 minutes.

4. Add the minced garlic (and ginger if using). Cook 30–60 seconds until fragrant. Don’t let the garlic burn.

5. Stir in the **curry powder**, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly. This step wakes up the spices and makes the curry taste much better.

6. Pour in the **coconut milk** and ¼ cup water or broth. Stir well. Bring to a gentle simmer (small bubbles around the edges). Let it cook 3–5 minutes so the sauce thickens slightly and flavors come together. Taste and add a little more salt if needed.

7. Add the shrimp to the simmering sauce. Spread them into an even layer. Cook **3–5 minutes**, stirring gently once or twice, until the shrimp turn **bright pink/orange** and curl into a loose “C” shape. They should be opaque (no longer gray or translucent in the center).  
   **Key beginner tip:** Shrimp cook fast! Take them off the heat as soon as they look done — they’ll finish cooking in the hot sauce.

8. Remove from heat. Stir in spinach or peas (if using) — they wilt from the residual heat. Add a squeeze of fresh lime juice if you like brightness. Taste one last time and adjust seasoning.

9. Serve immediately over hot rice. Garnish with chopped cilantro or green onions.

### Beginner Tips for Success
- **Don’t overcook the shrimp** — rubbery shrimp is the #1 beginner mistake. Watch the color change and err on the side of slightly underdone when you remove the pan from heat.

- **Spice level:** Start with 1 tablespoon curry powder. You can always add a pinch of chili flakes or cayenne at the end if you want more heat.
