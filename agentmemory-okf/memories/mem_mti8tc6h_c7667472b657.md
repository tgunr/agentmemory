### 👤 User

What is the print bed size maximum for a eufy I’ve one printer

---

### 🤖 Assistant

If you're referring to the eufyMake E1 UV printer, the maximum flatbed print area is:

- 330 × 420 mm (13 × 16.5 inches), which is approximately A3 size.

Other relevant limits include:
- Maximum 3D texture height: up to 5 mm.
- Roll-to-film mode: up to 10 meters (33 feet) of continuous printing when using the optional roll attachment.
- Mini flatbed area: approximately 330 × 90 mm (13 × 3.5 inches).

The standard flatbed (330 × 420 mm) is the largest direct-to-object print area available on the E1. This is suitable for items such as:
- Signs and plaques
- Laptop covers
- Multiple phone cases in one job
- Acrylic, wood, metal, and other flat objects within that footprint
