### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-08-29T20:11:09Z

They do — most insects have a quiet, low-activity state that looks a lot like sleep. Fruit flies are the best-studied case: they go still, their antennae droop, and they get cranky if you wake them up, just like a tired person.

Some even show **REM-like** brain activity during it. So yeah, the little ones dream too, apparently.

### 🤖 Assistant — 2026-08-29T20:11:41Z

So the honest answer is: we can't ask them, but the evidence says their brains are doing something dream-shaped at night.
