How do I transfer a 401k account into ira on robinhood

### 🤖 Assistant — 2025-07-13T19:46:32Z

To transfer a 401(k) to an IRA on Robinhood, you'll need to do a rollover. Here's the process:

**First, open a Robinhood IRA:**
- Log into your Robinhood account or download the app
- Navigate to the retirement section and select "Open IRA"
- Choose between Traditional IRA (if rolling over from traditional 401k) or Roth IRA (if you want to convert and pay taxes now)
- Complete the application

**Initiate the rollover:**
- In your Robinhood IRA, look for "Transfer" or "Rollover" options
- Select "401(k) rollover" 
- You'll need information about your current 401(k): plan administrator name, account number, and contact details
- Robinhood will provide you with rollover forms and instructions

**Two rollover methods:**
1. **Direct rollover** (recommended): Your 401(k) provider sends funds directly to Robinhood - no taxes withheld
2. **Indirect rollover**: You receive a check and have 60 days to deposit it into your IRA - 20% may be withheld for taxes

**Important considerations:**
- Check if your current employer allows in-service withdrawals if you're still employed
- Consider any employer match you might lose
- Review investment options and fees between your 401(k) and Robinhood
- Traditional to Traditional = no immediate taxes; Traditional to Roth = you'll owe taxes on the converted amount

The process typically takes 2-4 weeks. Robinhood's customer support can walk you through the specific steps and provide the necessary paperwork.
