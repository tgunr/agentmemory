Sure! Here are a couple of classic liver and onions recipes:

### Classic Liver and Onions

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb calf or beef liver, sliced
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 tablespoons butter or oil
- 1/2 cup beef broth or water
- Optional: bacon slices (for extra flavor)

**Instructions:**
1. Pat the liver dry with paper towels and season with salt and pepper.
2. Dredge the liver slices in flour, shaking off excess.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter or oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Add liver slices to the skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes per side until browned and cooked through. Remove and keep warm.
5. In the same skillet, add the remaining butter or oil.
6. Add the sliced onions and cook over medium heat until softened and caramelized, about 10-15 minutes.
7. If using bacon, cook it until crispy in a separate pan and set aside.
8. Deglaze the skillet with beef broth or water, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom.
9. Return liver slices to the skillet to heat through.
10. Serve liver topped with caramelized onions (and bacon if desired).

### German-Style Liver and Onions

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb pork or calf liver, sliced
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup flour
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tablespoons vinegar (apple cider or white wine vinegar)
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- Chopped parsley for garnish

**Instructions:**
1. Season liver slices with salt and pepper, then dredge in flour.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter and oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Fry liver slices for 2-3 minutes per side until browned. Remove and keep warm.
4. In the same skillet, add the remaining butter and sauté onions until caramelized, about 10-15 minutes.
5. Deglaze the skillet with beef broth, vinegar, sugar, and mustard.
6. Bring to a simmer and cook until sauce thickens slightly.
7. Return liver slices to the skillet and heat through.
8. Serve liver topped with caramelized onions and sauce, garnished with chopped parsley.

These recipes should give you a good starting point for preparing delicious liver and onions dishes!
