This doesn't require any web search since this is standard software uninstallation procedures that haven't changed significantly.The steps to uninstall Google Drive depend on your operating system:

**Windows:**
1. Open Settings (Windows key + I)
2. Go to "Apps" or "Apps & features"
3. Search for "Google Drive" in the list
4. Click on it and select "Uninstall"
5. Follow the prompts to complete removal

Alternatively, you can use Control Panel → Programs → Programs and Features → find Google Drive → Uninstall.

**Mac:**
1. Open Finder and go to Applications folder
2. Find "Google Drive" 
3. Drag it to the Trash, or right-click and select "Move to Trash"
4. Empty the Trash to complete removal
5. You may also want to remove leftover files from ~/Library/Application Support/Google/Drive

**Mobile devices:**
- **Android:** Long-press the Google Drive app → Uninstall
- **iPhone/iPad:** Long-press the app → Remove App → Delete App

**Important notes:**
- Uninstalling the desktop app won't delete your files from Google Drive cloud storage - they'll still be accessible via web browser
- If you want to stop syncing but keep the app, you can just sign out instead
- Any locally synced files will remain on your computer unless you delete them separately
