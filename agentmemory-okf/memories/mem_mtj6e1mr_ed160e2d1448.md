### Chicken Cacciatore Recipe
**Ingredients:**
- 4-6 chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on for max flavor, but boneless works too)
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 1-2 bell peppers (any color), sliced
- 3-4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups diced tomatoes (fresh or a 14-oz can, with juice)
- 1/2 cup chicken broth (or water in a pinch)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil (or a handful of fresh if you’ve got it)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Olive oil for cooking
- Optional: a splash of red wine, red pepper flakes for heat, or olives for a twist

**Steps:**
1. **Brown the Chicken:** Heat a couple tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper, then cook them skin-side down until golden, about 5-7 minutes per side. Remove them to a plate—they don’t need to be fully cooked yet.
   
2. **Cook the Veggies:** In the same pan, toss in the chopped onion and sliced bell peppers. Sauté for 4-5 minutes until they start to soften. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute until it smells amazing.

3. **Build the Sauce:** Stir in the diced tomatoes, chicken broth, oregano, and basil. If you’re using wine or red pepper flakes, add them now. Give it a good stir, scraping up any tasty bits stuck to the pan.

4. **Simmer:** Nestle the chicken thighs back into the sauce, skin-side up. Lower the heat to medium-low, cover, and let it simmer for 25-30 minutes. The chicken should be cooked through (internal temp of 165°F/74°C if you’re checking), and the sauce will thicken up nicely.

5. **Finish:** Taste the sauce and adjust with more salt, pepper, or herbs if needed. If it’s too thick, splash in a bit more broth; if too thin, let it simmer uncovered for a few extra minutes.

**Serving:** Spoon it over pasta, rice, or polenta, or just grab some crusty bread to soak up the sauce. It’s rustic and comforting either way.
