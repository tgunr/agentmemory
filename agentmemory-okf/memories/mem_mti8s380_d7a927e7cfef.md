### 🤖 Assistant

Yes — for what you’re describing, I would **stop searching for “turnstile” or even “turntable” as a finished epoxy product**.

The better fit is usually one of these:

1. **A rotating platform / lazy-Susan-style base**
2. **Mounted on adjustable leveling feet**
3. **With a custom 30" × 30" top**

### What I’d look for

For a **30" × 30" epoxy work surface**, the safest path is usually a **2-part setup**:

- **Top:** a 30" × 30" sealed plywood, HDPE, melamine, or phenolic panel
- **Rotation:** heavy-duty rotating bearing/platform underneath
- **Leveling:** adjustable machine feet on the base

That gives you:
- enough surface area for your stock
- smooth rotation
- actual leveling adjustment independent of the tabletop

### Why this is probably better than a single off-the-shelf “epoxy turntable”

The search results I found for “turntable adjustable leveling” mostly point to **audio/vinyl turntable feet and leveling accessories**, which confirm the leveling concept but are **not sized for a 30" × 30" epoxy

work platform**. For example:
- [Mnpctech adjustable height leveling turntable feet](https://www.mnpctech.com/products/adjustable-height-leveling-turntable-isolation-feet)
- [U-Turn Iso-Level Feet](https://uturnaudio.com/products/iso-level-feet)
- [Yamaha’s note on using adjustable feet to level a turntable](https://hub.yamaha.com/audio/vinyl/how-to-level-and-position-your-turntable/)

Those are useful mainly because they reinforce that **independent adjustable feet are the right design idea**.

### Better component types for your project

For the **leveling part**, look at actual **machine leveling feet** rather than furniture feet:

- [WDS leveling feet](https://www.wdscomponents.com/en-us/machine-and-hardware/leveling-feet)
- [MachineLevelingFeet.com heavy-duty leveling feet](https://www.machinelevelingfeet.com/)
- [UNWIREDD swivel adjustable leveler feet](https://www.amazon.com/Swivel-Adjustable-Leg-Leveler-Feet/dp/B075C3Q4B1)

From those sources, the key features you want are:
- **swivel/self-aligning

pads**
- **threaded height adjustment**
- **non-slip base**
- enough **load capacity** for your platform + piece + epoxy

### My recommendation

If this were my setup, I’d build or buy around this spec:

- **30" × 30" top**
- **base slightly smaller**, like 24"–28" square
- **4 heavy-duty adjustable leveling feet**
- **central rotating bearing/platform**
- **bullseye bubble level** mounted on top or kept nearby
