### 🤖 Assistant

This image is the famous "The Dress" (also known as #TheDress), which became a global internet sensation in February 2015 due to a massive disagreement over its colors.

The controversy stems from the fact that people see the dress in two main color combinations: either **blue and black** or **white and gold**. This difference is not due to a trick or monitor settings, but rather how the human brain perceives color and light.

#### The Science Behind It

The Phenomenon is known as color constancy. The photo was taken under ambiguous lighting conditions, which causes the brain to make an unconscious "executive decision" about the lighting source:

*   **White and Gold:** If your brain interprets the light as being cool or bluish (like being in a shadow), it discounts the blue tint and perceives the dress as white and gold.
*   **Blue and Black:** If your brain assumes the light is warm or yellow (like artificial indoor light), it perceives the dress as its true colors, which are blue and black.

#### The

Reality

The actual dress, manufactured by the British retailer Roman Originals, was confirmed to be **royal blue and black**. Despite the objective reality, the image remains the most famous example of how subjective visual perception can be, leading to intense debates and scientific studies on vision and neurology.
