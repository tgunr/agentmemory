### 🤖 Assistant

Egg salad is a classic, versatile dish that can be served in sandwiches, wraps, or on a bed of greens. Here is a simple, creamy recipe to get you started:

### Classic Egg Salad

**Ingredients**
*   6 large hard-boiled eggs, peeled and chopped
*   1/4 cup mayonnaise
*   1 teaspoon yellow or Dijon mustard
*   1/4 cup finely diced celery (for crunch)
*   1 tablespoon chopped fresh chives or green onions
*   Salt and black pepper to taste
*   Optional: A pinch of paprika or a dash of hot sauce

**Instructions**
1.  In a medium bowl, combine the mayonnaise, mustard, salt, and pepper. Stir until smooth.
2.  Add the chopped hard-boiled eggs, celery, and chives to the bowl.
3.  Gently fold the ingredients together until the eggs are evenly coated with the dressing.
4.  Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
5.  Serve immediately or refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.

**Variations to Try**
*   **Curried Egg Salad:** Add 1/2 teaspoon of curry powder and a few raisins or dried

cranberries for a sweet and spicy twist.
*   **Avocado Egg Salad:** Replace half of the mayonnaise with mashed ripe avocado for a healthier, green version.
*   **Dill Pickle Egg Salad:** Stir in 2 tablespoons of finely chopped dill pickles or relish for extra tang.
*   **Protein Boost:** Mix in some canned tuna or shredded chicken to make it even more filling.
