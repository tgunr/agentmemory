## The field in 2026

The landscape has narrowed to two benchmark standards — **LongMemEval-S** (500 questions, ~115K tokens/session, 48 sessions) and **LoCoMo** (1,540 questions). BEAM (1M/10M token scale) is the newer production stress test. Anything not benchmarked on one of these is a claim, not a measurement.

## Honcho (Plastic Labs)

**Philosophy:** Memory as a *peer graph*. The primitive is a "peer" — a human, agent, project, or idea. Sessions are many-to-many with peers, so you can cleanly model "what does Alice know about Bob" or "what does the support persona think the customer wants." Background reasoning ("dreams") draws inferences over time. You don't search a vector store; you ask a research agent a natural-language question and get a synthesized answer.

**Benchmark:** Honcho claims **90.4% on LongMem S** (92.6% with Gemini 3 Pro), 89.9% on LoCoMo, "top scores across all BEAM tests" (per their blog). These are vendor-reported; not independently reproduced.
