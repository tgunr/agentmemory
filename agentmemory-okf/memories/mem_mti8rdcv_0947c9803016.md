### 🤖 Assistant

Got it — here’s a simple boiled egg method 👍

**1. Place eggs in a pot**  
Put the eggs in a single layer and cover with cold water (about 1 inch above eggs).

**2. Heat**  
Bring the water to a boil over medium-high heat.

**3. Turn off heat**  
Once it reaches a rolling boil, turn off the heat and cover the pot.

**4. Let sit (timing depends on doneness):**  
- Soft: 6–7 minutes  
- Medium: 9–10 minutes  
- Hard: 11–12 minutes  

**5. Ice bath**  
Transfer eggs to cold water or ice bath for 5 minutes — this stops cooking and makes peeling easier.

**6. Peel & eat 😄**

If you’re cooking a lot (like 10+ eggs), same method works — just don’t overcrowd the pot.
