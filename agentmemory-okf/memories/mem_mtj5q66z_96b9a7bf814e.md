### 🤖 Assistant

Here is a recipe for a Middle Eastern-inspired lamb and vegetable stew:

Ingredients:
- 1 pound ground lamb
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup diced tomatoes
- 1 cup diced carrots
- 1 cup diced potatoes
- 1 cup green peas
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. In a large pot, heat a small amount of oil over medium-high heat.
2. Add the ground lamb and cook until browned.
3. Add the onion and garlic and cook until the onion is translucent.
4. Add the tomatoes, carrots, potatoes, and green peas and stir to combine.
5. Add the chicken broth, cumin, coriander, paprika, salt, and pepper and stir to combine.
6. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 20-30 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
7. Serve hot and enjoy!
