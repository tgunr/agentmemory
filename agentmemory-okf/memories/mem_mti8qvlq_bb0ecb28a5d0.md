### Quick “scam check” for TikTok posts selling/claiming sizes
Common red flags:
- “Free” but you must pay **shipping/insurance/admin fee**
- They push you **off-platform** (WhatsApp/Telegram/“link in bio” checkout) or ask for personal info
- Unrealistic claims with no continuous proof (lots of cuts, no full shot, no tape

measure shown end-to-end)
- New/blank account, comments look like bots, no clear return policy

TikTok’s own safety page summarizes typical scam patterns (money + personal info harvesting) here: [TikTok – Frauds & scams](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/community-support/scams).
