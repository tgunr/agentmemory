### 👤 User

My desired shop name for Etsy is taken and I need to come up with a new one. I have the domains CarltonCrafted.com. - Carltoncrafted.org
- CarltonCreation.com
- PolyMicro.net

---

### 🤖 Assistant

It is a common hurdle on Etsy, but since you already own several strong domains, you have a great head start on building a cohesive brand. Having the `.com` for your shop name is a huge advantage for long-term marketing and off-platform sales.

Since "Carlton Crafted" is likely the name you wanted but is taken on Etsy, here are some strategies and specific name ideas based on the domains you already own:

### Using Your Existing Domains
If you want to stick as close as possible to your domains, you can add a small modifier to the Etsy shop name. Etsy allows up to 20 characters with no spaces.

#### Carlton Crafted Options
Since `CarltonCrafted` is taken, these variations stay true to your `.com` and `.org` domains:
*   **CarltonCraftedCo** (Carlton Crafted Co)
*   **CarltonCraftedShop**
*   **CarltonCraftedStudio**
*   **TheCarltonCrafted**
*   **CarltonCraftedHome** (if you sell home goods)
*   **CarltonCraftedArts**

#### Carlton Creation Options
This matches your `CarltonCreation.com` domain perfectly:
*

**CarltonCreation** (Check if this is available without the 's')
*   **CarltonCreationsCo**
*   **CarltonCreationStore**
*   **CarltonCreationLab**

#### Poly Micro Options
This has a very different, more technical or modern vibe, which might be perfect if you are doing 3D printing, miniatures, or precision work:
*   **PolyMicro**
*   **PolyMicroWorks**
*   **PolyMicroStudio**
*   **PolyMicroDesign**

### Creative Alternatives
If you want to pivot slightly while keeping the "Carlton" or "Poly" branding, consider these:
*   **CarltonHandmade**
*   **CarltonCustoms**
*   **PolyCrafted** (A hybrid of your domains)
*   **MicroCarlton**
*   **CarltonBoutique**

### Important Tips for Choosing
*   **Check Availability:** You can check if a name is available by typing `etsy.com/shop/YourDesiredName` into your browser. If it says "The shop you're looking for cannot be found," the name might be available (though sometimes Etsy holds names from closed shops).
*   **Brand Consistency:** Even if your Etsy name is

`CarltonCraftedCo`, you can still use `CarltonCrafted.com` on your business cards and social media. You can simply set your domain to redirect automatically to your Etsy shop until you are ready to build a full website.
*   **Social Media:** Before finalizing, check if the handle is available on Instagram or TikTok so your branding is the same across all platforms.

Which of your domains feels most like the "soul" of what you are making? If you tell me more about what you sell, I can help you narrow down which name fits the product best.
