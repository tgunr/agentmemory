### 👤 User

Are there any lasers that can be mounted to the onefinity foreman inc machine that can cut at least 1/4" plywood that are reasonable in price around $2000?

---

### 🤖 Assistant

Yes. There **are** laser modules that will mount on a Onefinity Foreman and reliably cut 1/4" plywood within about a **$2,000 budget**. The best‑supported options are from **J Tech Photonics** and **Opt Lasers**, both of which sell Foreman‑specific kits.

Below is a concise breakdown, focusing on options that:

- Are **compatible with Onefinity Foreman**
- Can **cut at least 1/4" (≈6 mm) plywood**
- Are **around or under $2,000** (for the full kit, not counting enclosure/extraction)

---

### 1. J Tech 64W PRO 3‑in‑1 Laser Kit (Onefinity Version)

**This is the closest “no‑compromise” answer to your question.**

- **Price**: About **$1,999.99** from J Tech; similar via Onefinity’s store, sometimes slightly discounted.  
  - J Tech product page: [J Tech 64W PRO Onefinity Kit](https://jtechphotonics.com/?product=64w-pro-laser-kit-with-air-assist-onefinity-version)  
  - Onefinity store listing: [Onefinity J Tech Laser](https://www.onefinitycnc.com/product-page/jtech-laser)
- **Compatibility**: Sold

specifically as a **Onefinity version**, with correct mounts and cable lengths for Foreman‑size machines.
- **Power**: Up to **64 W optical** (array of 12 × ~7 W diodes), with selectable 64 W / 44 W / 24 W modes.
- **Cutting capability**:
  - Designed to **cut 1/4" plywood easily** (often single pass or a few passes, depending on material and settings).  
  - J Tech notes performance comparable to small CO₂ hobby lasers for wood cutting in this thickness range [J Tech 64W product page](https://jtechphotonics.com/?product=64w-pro-laser-kit-with-air-assist-onefinity-version).
- **What’s included**:
  - Laser head
  - Safety interface board & wiring for Onefinity
  - 24 V power supply
  - Magnetic or dedicated mount for Onefinity
  - Air‑assist plumbing (you usually provide the air pump separately)
  - Safety goggles, focus tools, etc.
- **Why it’s a good fit**:
  - Plug‑and‑play for Foreman (mounts and cables are already tailored)
  - Well‑documented with a lot of CNC/Onefinity user experience online
  - Sits

right at your **$2,000** budget and comfortably meets the **1/4" plywood** requirement.

If you want **maximum cutting performance without jumping to a full CO₂ gantry machine**, this is the straightforward choice.

---

### 2. J Tech 44W OCTO PRO Laser Kit (Onefinity Version)

If you want to save a few hundred dollars and accept slightly slower or multi‑pass cuts:

- **Price**: About **$1,599.99**  
  - [J Tech 44W OCTO PRO Onefinity Kit](https://jtechphotonics.com/?product=44w-octo-pro-laser-kit-with-air-assist-onefinity-version)
- **Power**: **44 W optical** (8‑diode array).
- **Cutting capability**:
  - Designed to handle **1/8" plywood easily**, and **1/4" plywood is achievable** with appropriate speeds, multiple passes, and strong air assist [J Tech high‑power overview](https://jtechphotonics.com/?p=40443).
- **Same integration**:
  - Onefinity‑specific mount and wiring
  - Safety board, key switch, air‑assist hardware, etc., similar to the 64 W kit.

This is a good “value” choice if your 1/4" cuts don

’t need to be super fast and you’re okay optimizing settings.
