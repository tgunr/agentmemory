### Typical used price
- Usually about **$3,500–$4,000+** for used/refurbished units in the US.
- Private/community sales can sometimes land closer to **$3,500–$3,800**.

### What to look for
- **Tested / working** status
- **Return policy** or warranty
- **US-based seller**
- Whether it’s a **PNY**, **Dell OEM**, or **HP OEM** card
- Photos of the actual card,

especially the PCIe connector, cooler, and serial label

### Quick buying advice
- If a listing is **far below market**, treat it as suspicious.
- Prefer sellers with strong feedback and clear photos.
- Confirm whether it includes:
  - original box
  - power adapter if needed
  - proof of testing
