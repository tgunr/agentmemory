### 🤖 Assistant

Here’s a classic Southern-style mustard greens recipe—savory, a little smoky, and full of flavor:

### Ingredients
- 2–3 bunches mustard greens (or 2 lbs), washed well and chopped  
- 1 smoked ham hock (or 4–6 slices bacon / smoked turkey wing)  
- 1 small onion, chopped  
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced  
- 4 cups chicken broth (or water + bouillon)  
- 1–2 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 tsp sugar (optional, balances bitterness)  
- 1/2–1 tsp red pepper flakes (to taste)  
- Salt & black pepper, to taste  

### Instructions
1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, cook the bacon (if using) until crisp. Remove and set aside, leaving some fat. If using ham hock, just add it in the next step.
2. Sauté onion in the pot until soft, then add garlic and cook about 30 seconds.
3. Add ham hock (or turkey), broth, pepper flakes, and bring to a simmer. Let it cook 20–30 minutes to build flavor.
4. Add the chopped mustard greens in batches, letting them wilt down.
5. Cover and simmer on low for about 45–60 minutes, until greens

are tender.
6. Stir in vinegar and sugar, then taste and adjust salt/pepper.
7. If using bacon, crumble and add back in at the end.

### Tips
- The pot liquor (the broth left behind) is liquid gold—serve with cornbread to soak it up.
- Want it spicier? Add a dash of hot sauce or chopped chili.
- For a vegetarian version, skip the meat and use smoked paprika + veggie broth for that smoky depth.
