### 🤖 Assistant

For a **standard, rectangular First‑Class letter** (your typical envelope) that weighs **½ oz**, the USPS rate is the **same as 1 oz**: **$0.78** (one **Forever®** stamp). Source: [USPS – First‑Class Mail & Postage](https://www.usps.com/ship/first-class-mail.htm)

A couple common exceptions (cost more even if it’s under 1 oz):

- **Square / oversized / unusually shaped** envelope: starts at **$1.27** ([USPS](https://www.usps.com/ship/first-class-mail.htm))
- **Large envelope (“flat”)**: starts at **$1.63** ([USPS](https://www.usps.com/ship/first-class-mail.htm))

If you tell me whether it’s a **normal #10 envelope** or something **square/thick/rigid**, I can confirm which category applies.
