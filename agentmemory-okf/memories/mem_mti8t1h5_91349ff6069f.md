### Can Telegram be installed on Apple CarPlay?

No—**Telegram can’t be installed on CarPlay**, and there isn’t an official Telegram CarPlay app.

Apple only allows certain app categories on CarPlay (mainly **audio, messaging, navigation, EV charging, and a few others**). Telegram has not shipped a CarPlay-enabled messaging app, so it won’t appear on your CarPlay screen.

### What you *can* do instead

#### Option 1: Use Siri to handle Telegram hands-free (limited)
You can try Siri voice control for basic actions, but **support varies** and is often limited compared to Apple Messages/WhatsApp.
- Invoke Siri: “Hey Siri…”
- Try: “Send a Telegram message to John…”  
If Siri doesn’t offer Telegram as an option, it means Telegram isn’t exposed to Siri in a way your iPhone can use.

#### Option 2: Use notifications + voice (safest/most practical)
You can still receive Telegram notifications on your iPhone while driving, and you can:
- Have Siri read notifications (if enabled)
- Reply by voice when possible (again,

depends on what iOS offers for Telegram)

Check:
- **Settings → Notifications → Telegram**
- Ensure **Allow Notifications** is on  
- Also check **Settings → Siri & Search → Telegram** (if present) for any Siri permissions

#### Option 3: Use CarPlay + your phone (not on-screen)
You can’t interact with Telegram in CarPlay UI, but you can still use Telegram on the phone when parked.

### Avoid “workarounds”
If you see guides suggesting jailbreaks or “CarPlay app enablers,” they’re generally **unreliable**, can **break with iOS updates**, and may introduce security/safety issues.

Are you trying to **(a)** read incoming Telegram messages, **(b)** reply by voice, or **(c)** start new Telegram messages while driving? Also: what iPhone/iOS version?
