about how many pounds of grass a day does an Aberdeen heifer eat

---

### 🤖 Assistant

An American Aberdeen heifer typically eats between **20 and 33 pounds** of grass or hay per day, depending on her weight, the quality of the forage, and her stage of development (such as whether she is growing or gestating).

Because Aberdeen cattle are a moderate-sized breed, they eat significantly less than standard large-frame cattle. While a typical 1,500-pound Angus cow might consume 45 pounds of forage daily, a mature Aberdeen cow (averaging 1,000 pounds) generally consumes about **33 pounds** of grass or hay per day [Hobby Farms](https://www.hobbyfarms.com/get-to-know-aberdeen-cattle-breed/). 

Key factors that influence this daily intake include:

*   **Body Weight:** Cattle generally consume about 2% to 2.5% of their body weight in dry matter daily. Since Aberdeens are smaller (900–1,000 lbs for mature cows), their total intake is lower than larger breeds [UNL Beef](https://beef.unl.edu/cattleproduction/forageconsumed-day/).
*   **Forage Quality:** Higher-quality, more digestible grass allows for

higher intake because it passes through the rumen faster. Low-quality forage (like straw or dry stalks) is digested slowly, which can limit intake to as low as 1.8% of body weight [The Beef Site](https://www.thebeefsite.com/articles/3154/how-much-forage-does-a-beef-cow-consume-each-day/).
*   **Development Stage:** Heifers that are still growing or those in the later stages of pregnancy will have higher nutritional demands and may consume closer to 2.5% or 3% of their body weight if high-quality forage is available.

For specific herd management, it is also important to account for "as-fed" weight versus "dry matter" weight. Since grass and hay contain moisture, the actual weight of the feed you put out may be slightly higher (roughly 10-15% more for hay) to ensure they receive the necessary dry matter nutrients [AgUpdate](https://agupdate.com/agriview/news/livestock/calculate-how-much-hay-cows-consume/article_d80ef52c-c027-5cfc-ac01-6b3f627a1e29.html).
