# Blocking and Reporting Text Messages on Apple Messages

Here's how to block and report spam text messages on your iPhone:

## To Block a Sender:

1. Open the Messages app
2. Open the conversation with the sender you want to block
3. Tap the contact name/phone number at the top of the screen
4. Tap "info" (i) button
5. Scroll down and tap "Block this Caller"
6. Confirm by tapping "Block Contact"

## To Report Spam/Junk Messages:

### For iMessage (blue bubbles):
1. Open the conversation with the suspicious message
2. Tap the sender's name at the top
3. Tap "info" (i) button
4. Scroll down and tap "Report Junk"

### For SMS/MMS (green bubbles):
1. Tap the message from an unknown sender
2. Look for the "Report Junk" link below the message
3. Tap "Report Junk" and confirm

### Additional Reporting Options:
- Forward spam texts to 7726 (SPAM) - this reports to your carrier
- Report scam texts to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov

### Additional Protection:
- Go to Settings → Messages → Toggle on "Filter Unknown Senders" to separate messages from unknown senders into a separate list
