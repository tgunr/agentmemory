### 🤖 Assistant

You can see “Shared with me” folders on your desktop in a couple of different ways, depending on whether you want:

- just quick access to them, or  
- the actual folder contents synced to your computer via Google Drive for desktop.

Below are the usual options.

---

### 1. Use Google Drive for desktop (to sync folders locally)

If you want the folders to appear like normal folders in Finder (Mac) or File Explorer (Windows):

#### Step 1 – Make sure you have Google Drive for desktop installed
- Download and install from: [Google Drive for desktop](https://www.google.com/drive/download/)
- Sign in with your Google account.

#### Step 2 – Add the shared folder to “My Drive”
Google Drive for desktop only syncs:
- `My Drive`
- Any “Shared drives” you have access to  

So first you have to add a shared folder to your `My Drive`:

1. Open [Google Drive in your browser](https://drive.google.com/).
2. Click `Shared with me` on the left.
3. Find the folder you want.
4. Right‑click it and choose:
   - **“Add shortcut

to Drive”** (newer UI), then choose a location in “My Drive”, or  
   - If you see **“Add to My Drive”**, click that.

This does *not* copy the folder; it just creates a shortcut under `My Drive` that Drive for desktop can sync.

#### Step 3 – Ensure it’s set to sync on desktop
1. On your desktop, click the **Google Drive icon** in your system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac).
2. Open **Preferences** (gear icon → `Preferences`).
3. Under **My Drive**:
   - Make sure “Stream files” or “Mirror files” is set according to your preference.
   - Confirm the folder/shortcut you just added is visible under `My Drive`.

Now you’ll see:
- On **Windows**: the folder under `Google Drive > My Drive` in File Explorer.
- On **Mac**: the folder under `Google Drive > My Drive` in Finder.

---

### 2. Just view “Shared with me” via your browser

If you just mean “see which folders are shared with me” while on a desktop/laptop (not necessarily syncing them):

1. Go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/).
2. In the left

sidebar, click **“Shared with me”**.
3. This shows all files and folders others have shared with you.
4. If you want any of these to appear in your local Drive folder (for Drive for desktop), follow the “Add shortcut to Drive / My Drive” step above.

---

### 3. Quick checklist / troubleshooting

If you **don’t** see a shared folder on your desktop:

- Check that:
  - You **added a shortcut to My Drive** from `Shared with me`.
  - Google Drive for desktop is **running and signed in**.
  - In Drive for desktop preferences, `My Drive` is actually being synced (whether streaming or mirroring).
- Remember: folders will show under the `Google Drive` drive/folder on your computer, not as totally separate drives by default.
