**CAB-O-SIL** is Cabot Corporation’s brand of **fumed silica** (also called pyrogenic silica)—a high-purity, amorphous silicon dioxide (SiO₂) sold as a very fine, lightweight white powder.<grok:render card_id="151fde" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

It is made by flame hydrolysis of silicon tetrachloride. Primary particles (typically 5–50 nm) fuse into branched, chain-like aggregates that form a three-dimensional network. That structure gives it high surface area (often 50–400+ m²/g depending on grade), low bulk density, and strong effects on viscosity and flow even at low loadings.<grok:render card_id="020ffd" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

### Main uses
- **Rheology control** — Thickens liquids and creates thixotropy (shear-thinning). Common in paints, coatings, adhesives, sealants, inks, unsaturated polyester resins, and greases. Prevents sagging, settling, and dripping.
- **Reinforcing filler** — Strengthens silicone rubbers (HTV and LSR) and some organic elastomers.
- **Anti-caking / free-flow agent** — Improves powder flow and reduces lumping in foods, fertilizers, pharmaceuticals, and other dry products.
- **Pharmaceuticals** — Glidant and flow aid in tablets and capsules; carrier and thickener in creams and gels (grades such as M-5P that meet pharmacopeia specs).
- **Food** — Anti-caking, carrier, flavor-masking, and fat-substitution uses (food-grade grades such as M-5F).
- **Other** — Cosmetics (light-diffusing), composites, seed coatings, and moisture control.

### Hydrophilic vs. hydrophobic grades
- **Untreated (hydrophilic)** grades such as **M-5**, LM-150, and H-300 work well in polar systems and many general thickening jobs. M-5 is widely used for thickening epoxy in composites and boat work.
- **Surface-treated (hydrophobic)** grades such as **TS-720**, TS-610, and TS-530 are modified (e.g., with silanes or PDMS) so they perform better in non-polar oils, certain coatings, and moisture-sensitive systems. They also reduce water uptake.

Cabot offers many specific grades (M-5, M-5F, M-5P, H-300, TS-720, densified “D” versions for easier handling, etc.) chosen by surface area, treatment, and end-use (coatings, rubber, food, pharma).

Fumed silica is chemically inert, odorless, and tasteless in the appropriate grades. Handle it as a fine dust: use respiratory protection and good ventilation because the powder is extremely light and airborne. Always consult the specific product SDS and applicable regulations for food or pharmaceutical use.

Checking how Cab-O-Sil behaves in clear epoxy flood coats, since thickening usually trades off optical clarity.**Not for a clear flood coat.** Leave Cab-O-Sil out of that pour.

Fumed silica is excellent when you *want* epoxy to stay put — fillets, vertical surfaces, fairing paste, structural glue. Even a little of it (often 0.7–3% by weight) builds a particle network that thickens and makes the mix thixotropic. That same network scatters light. In a thin, see-through flood coat you will lose the glass look and pick up haze or milkiness.
