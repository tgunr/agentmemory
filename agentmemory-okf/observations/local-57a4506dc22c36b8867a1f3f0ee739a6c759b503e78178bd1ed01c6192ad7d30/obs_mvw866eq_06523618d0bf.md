# Summary

The user specifies the model to switch to, and the skill applies the router's priority-list pick.
This is the reconciliation of two previously-duplicated commands:
- `/route` = **advisory** scorer/classifier (does NOT switch).
- `/model-switch` = **actuator** that applies a `/route`-style pick.

## Facts
- To switch the active model to one of three intent categories —\n**coding**, **general**, or **research** — by applying the router's own\npriority-list pick for that task type.
- The router's `models.yaml` is the single source of truth — `switch.py`.\nknowns no registry and no classifier.
