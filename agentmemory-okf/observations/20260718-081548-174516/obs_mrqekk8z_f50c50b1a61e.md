# Summary

Searched for compatibility of Zinsser SealCoat (dewaxed shellac) under various topcoats. While the manufacturer confirms compatibility with water-based polyurethanes, expert woodworkers warn of potential failure due to pH and ammonia reactions in waterborne chemistries, suggesting pure shellac flakes as a safer alternative.

## Facts
- Zinsser SealCoat (dewaxed shellac) is officially guaranteed compatible with oil-based poly, acrylic poly, lacquer, varnish, and catalyzed finishes.
- Rust-Oleum support states SealCoat can be used under water-based polyurethane to ensure adhesion.
- Woodworking experts warn SealCoat can fail under waterborne finishes due to pH and ammonia reactions in the chemistry.
- Experts recommend using pure dewaxed shellac flakes cut in denatured alcohol as a safer alternative for waterborne topcoats.
