# Summary

Web search confirmed Zinsser Bulls Eye Shellac 701 traditional amber contains wax, which affects finish compatibility. While suitable as a sealer under solvent-based lacquer, it cannot be reliably top-coated with polyurethane or waterborne finishes due to adhesion problems.

## Facts
- Zinsser Bulls Eye Shellac 701 contains wax according to multiple sources
- Shellac with wax adheres well under solvent-based lacquer finishes
- Waxed shellac has adhesion issues with oil-base polyurethane and some waterborne finishes
- Waxed shellac should only be top-coated with additional shellac, not other finish types
- Product is a shellac-based sealer providing natural mid-gloss finish without yellowing
