# Summary

The xTool X1 is a multi-functional laser cutter and engraver that offers cutting-edge capabilities for various applications, including cutting through wood, acrylic, leather, and paper, as well as fast and detailed engraving on wood, leather, dark acrylic, and cardstock.

## Facts
- The xTool X1 starts with a 55W CO2 laser
- The 20W Diode Galvo Module enables expansion to UV, MOPA, and Fiber light sources
