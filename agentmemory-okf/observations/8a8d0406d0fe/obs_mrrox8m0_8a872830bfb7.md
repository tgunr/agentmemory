# Summary

Vision analysis of 3D CAD model confirmed the slotted sharpening wheel design has a top-mounted cylindrical hub positioned above the flat disc and inner flange. The central bore for motor arbor mounting is located at the top of the hub and extends vertically through the entire assembly.

## Facts
- Main disc (yellow) is flat abrasive surface with radial slots for visibility during grinding
- Inner flange (light blue) is flat circular reinforcement plate between disc and hub
- Hub (dark green) is raised cylindrical mounting component oriented upward on TOP of disc assembly
- Central bore/hole is located at TOP of green hub, travels vertically through center of assembly
- Hub has starburst/faceted geometry on top face representing bore opening for arbor insertion
