# Summary

The macos-app-diagnostics skill was loaded to guide investigation of macOS GUI applications that quit or crash at startup, particularly after beta OS upgrades. It emphasizes finding Chromium-specific crash evidence, empirically testing launch-path fixes, and making successful workarounds durable through a wrapper app.

## Facts
- CEF and Electron crashes often appear in app-specific Crashpad or Sentry .SentryDmp directories rather than ~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports.
- The workflow profiles the app, discovers its actual support directory, compares terminal and LaunchServices launches, and inspects minidump strings for GPU, Metal, sandbox, renderer, and fatal-error signatures.
- A terminal launch succeeding while GUI launch fails indicates a likely LaunchServices or GPU-path issue rather than ordinary app logic.
- Suggested checks include app framework inspection, code-signature validation, process visibility checks, application logs, debug.log, and completed or in-progress minidumps.
